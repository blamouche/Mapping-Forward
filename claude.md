## Stats (README)

- The `## Statistics` section in `README.md` shows `Articles per month`.
- Compute the monthly totals by counting article lines under each month header: lines starting with `- [` under `#### <Month> (<N> articles)`.
- Render the bar chart using `█` blocks where **1 block = 3 articles** (round up).
- Keep months in chronological order (oldest first).
- Add `<br>` at the end of each chart line except the last.
