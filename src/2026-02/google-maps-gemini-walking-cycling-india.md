# Google Maps expands Gemini AI to walking and cycling routes

**Source**: https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/tech-news/google-maps-expands-gemini-ai-to-walking-and-cycling-routes/articleshow/127790335.cms

**Date**: January 30, 2026

**Author**: Times of India

**Keywords**: Google Maps, Gemini AI, walking, cycling, navigation, voice assistant, real-time data, India, hands-free

## Elevator pitch

Google expands Gemini AI to walking and cycling navigation in Google Maps, offering real-time route adjustments based on weather and road conditions while enabling hands-free voice interaction for safer travel.

## Takeaways

- Gemini AI now supports walking and cycling modes in Google Maps with voice-activated assistance
- Real-time data integration adjusts routes based on weather conditions, crowded areas, and flooded underpasses
- Voice commands enable hands-free tasks including sending messages, checking calendars, and getting ETA updates
- The feature currently only works as a voice assistant without text input or response options
- Global rollout is underway on both iOS and Android platforms wherever Gemini is available

## Synthesis

Google has announced a significant expansion of its Gemini AI capabilities within Google Maps, extending the conversational assistant to pedestrians and cyclists who rely on the platform for navigation. This update represents the natural evolution of Gemini's integration into Maps, which initially launched in November 2025 with more limited functionality focused on driving.

For pedestrians, Gemini functions as an intelligent companion capable of answering contextual questions during walks. Users can ask queries like "Where am I?" to get neighborhood identification or "What's a good place to eat around here?" for dining recommendations. The AI responds both audibly and visually, with suggestions appearing directly on the map interface. This combination of voice and visual feedback creates a more intuitive navigation experience than traditional text-based search.

The cycling implementation emphasizes safety through hands-free operation. Cyclists can check their estimated arrival times, discover nearby points of interest, or get detailed route information without touching their phones. This approach directly addresses the safety concerns inherent in using smartphones while riding, where physical interaction with devices creates dangerous distractions.

A notable advancement in this update is the integration of real-time environmental data into route planning. Gemini can now adjust suggested routes based on current conditions such as weather, crowded pedestrian areas, or infrastructure issues like flooded underpasses. This dynamic adaptation ensures users receive guidance that reflects actual on-the-ground conditions rather than static map data.

Beyond navigation queries, Gemini handles productivity tasks during travel. Users can check their upcoming meeting schedules or send text messages through voice commands. An example command like "Text Richa, I'll come home in 30 minutes" demonstrates the assistant's ability to compose and send messages without manual phone interaction.

The feature is rolling out globally on both iOS and Android platforms in regions where Gemini is available. Currently, the assistant operates exclusively through voice interaction, with text input and response capabilities not yet implemented. Users can activate Gemini by tapping its icon in the navigation interface or using the "Hey Google" voice trigger.
