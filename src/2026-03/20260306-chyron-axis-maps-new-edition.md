# Chyron Releases New Edition of AXIS Maps
**Source**: https://www.sportsvideo.org/2026/03/06/chyron-releases-new-edition-of-axis-maps/
**Date**: March 6, 2026
**Author**: Sports Video Group
**Keywords**: AXIS Maps, Chyron, broadcast graphics, sports broadcasting, mobile-first, map production, SSO, FTP

## Elevator pitch
Chyron's AXIS Maps 2.0 overhauls the broadcast mapping platform with a mobile-first interface, enhanced export workflows, and improved brand consistency tools built from client feedback.

## Takeaways
- AXIS Maps 2.0 introduces mobile-first vertical ratio support for multi-platform sports and news storytelling
- Refined label controls allow language-based customization of place names while maintaining readability
- FTP delivery automation and AXIS Track integration streamline distribution workflows
- Single Sign-On implementation and improved session management strengthen security
- A rebuilt bookmark system enables users to save and restore precise map states for live broadcasts

## Synthesis
Chyron's AXIS Maps 2.0 represents a significant generational update to a platform used in sports broadcasting and news production for geographic visualization. The release reflects months of client-informed development, according to Product Manager Peter Matis, translating broadcast team feedback into concrete workflow improvements.

The most strategically important addition is mobile-first vertical ratio support. As sports and news organizations increasingly produce content for both traditional broadcast formats and vertical-orientation social and mobile platforms, the ability to adapt map visualizations across aspect ratios within a single workflow reduces production complexity. This directly addresses a gap that forced broadcast teams to maintain separate production pipelines for different output formats.

The label control enhancements — allowing producers to customize place name display based on language preferences — address a practical need in international broadcast environments. Major sporting events, election coverage, and geopolitical news require geographic labels that are simultaneously accurate, readable, and appropriate for the target audience's language context. The ability to toggle between label styles without disrupting map design is a meaningful operational improvement.

Workflow streamlining through FTP delivery automation and AXIS Track integration reduces the friction between map production and asset delivery to broadcast systems. In live production environments, where speed is critical and error correction is costly, automation of delivery steps represents genuine risk reduction.

The security improvements — Single Sign-On and improved session management — reflect the enterprise IT requirements of broadcast organizations, which operate sensitive production networks with strict access control requirements. SSO adoption aligns AXIS Maps with standard enterprise authentication practices that broadcast IT departments increasingly mandate.

The rebuilt bookmark system serves a specific live production need: the ability to return instantly to precise geographic views during fast-moving live coverage of events like sporting competitions or breaking news situations.
