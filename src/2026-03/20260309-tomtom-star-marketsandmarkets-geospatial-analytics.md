# TomTom recognized as 'Star' in MarketsandMarkets' latest 360Quadrant for the Geospatial Analytics Market
**Source**: https://finance.yahoo.com/news/tomtom-recognized-star-marketsandmarkets-latest-144500361.html
**Date**: March 9, 2026
**Author**: PR Newswire
**Keywords**: TomTom, MarketsandMarkets, 360Quadrant, geospatial analytics, location intelligence, enterprise, autonomous driving

## Elevator pitch
TomTom earns a "Star" designation in MarketsandMarkets' geospatial analytics assessment, recognizing its real-time traffic data, AI-driven mapping, and enterprise location intelligence capabilities.

## Takeaways
- TomTom was assessed among 190+ companies in the 360Quadrant evaluation framework
- Key recognized capabilities include real-time traffic data APIs, geocoding, spatial visualization, and mobility pattern analysis
- TomTom serves insurance, retail, logistics, and urban planning verticals with location intelligence solutions
- Strategic focus areas span autonomous driving, ADAS systems, open ecosystem collaboration, and smart mobility
- Membership in the Overture Maps Foundation is highlighted as part of TomTom's open ecosystem approach

## Synthesis
MarketsandMarkets' recognition of TomTom as a "Star" in the geospatial analytics sector provides an external validation of the company's market positioning during a period of significant internal transition. The assessment evaluates companies across revenue, geographic reach, technology offerings, and growth strategies — criteria that TomTom's recent strategic moves are explicitly designed to address.

The specific capabilities cited in the assessment reflect TomTom's deliberate portfolio evolution. Rather than competing on consumer navigation brand recognition — a battle largely won by Google Maps — the company has repositioned around enterprise data services: geocoding and spatial APIs for developers, real-time traffic data for urban planners and logistics operators, and mobility analytics for insurers and retailers. The Junction Analytics and Location Intelligence platforms represent this data-as-a-service direction concretely.

The recognition of TomTom's AI-driven map production automation is particularly relevant in context. The company has invested in reducing the human labor cost of map updates by applying machine learning to satellite imagery and sensor data processing. This automation capability is a genuine differentiator against competitors relying on larger field teams or crowdsourced update processes.

The Overture Maps Foundation membership, mentioned in the assessment, serves a dual strategic function. It demonstrates commitment to open geospatial infrastructure standards — important for enterprise customers who require interoperable data — while positioning TomTom as a collaborative industry actor rather than a closed proprietary provider.

The timing of the recognition coincides with TomTom's leadership transition, providing a positive external signal for incoming CEO Mike Schoofs as he takes over a company navigating the gap between legacy navigation revenue decline and emerging data services growth.
