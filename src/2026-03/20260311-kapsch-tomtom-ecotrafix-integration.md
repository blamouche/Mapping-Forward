# Kapsch TrafficCom integrates TomTom data into EcoTrafiX
**Source**: https://www.automotiveworld.com/news/kapsch-trafficcom-integrates-tomtom-data-into-ecotrafix/
**Date**: March 11, 2026
**Author**: Automotive World
**Keywords**: Kapsch TrafficCom, TomTom, EcoTrafiX, floating car data, traffic management, smart cities, real-time traffic, Intertraffic 2026

## Elevator pitch
Kapsch TrafficCom integrates TomTom's floating car data into its EcoTrafiX platform, giving cities and road authorities real-time traffic intelligence without additional infrastructure deployment.

## Takeaways
- EcoTrafiX now embeds TomTom's Floating Car Data for real-time traffic flow, incidents, congestion patterns, and travel times
- The integration eliminates the need for custom project-specific modifications, reducing deployment complexity
- Cities and road authorities gain multimodal movement information for data-driven mobility management
- COO Samuel Kapsch stated data "allows cities to better understand the impact of their actions on their mobility ecosystem"
- The enhanced platform was demonstrated at Intertraffic Amsterdam 2026

## Synthesis
The integration of TomTom's Floating Car Data into Kapsch TrafficCom's EcoTrafiX platform represents a practical convergence of two distinct layers of the intelligent transportation stack: the data collection and aggregation layer (TomTom) and the traffic management platform layer (Kapsch). By combining them, the partnership delivers something neither company provides alone — a complete operational picture linking real-time traffic conditions to active traffic management interventions.

EcoTrafiX is deployed globally by public authorities and road operators as an end-to-end traffic management system. It manages traffic signals, monitors network performance, and coordinates incident response. The previous limitation was that it operated on infrastructure-derived data — sensor loops, cameras — which require physical installation and ongoing maintenance. Integrating TomTom's Floating Car Data, which aggregates anonymous position data from smartphones and vehicle navigation systems already in use, supplements this fixed infrastructure with a continuously updated, infrastructure-free data layer.

The operational benefit is coverage and latency. Floating Car Data provides near-real-time visibility into traffic conditions across entire road networks, including sections without fixed sensors. For city traffic managers, this means incidents on non-monitored streets become visible in the EcoTrafiX dashboard alongside data from instrumented corridors, enabling coordinated responses that were previously impossible.

Samuel Kapsch's framing of the partnership — "data is making a real difference in combatting congestion, because it allows cities to better understand the impact of their actions on their mobility ecosystem" — points to the platform's analytical value beyond real-time monitoring. By correlating traffic interventions (signal timing changes, lane configurations) with the resulting traffic flow data, cities can build evidence-based understanding of what management actions actually work in their specific network context.

The demonstration at Intertraffic Amsterdam 2026 serves both a technical validation and commercial signaling purpose — positioning the integrated platform to urban mobility decision-makers attending one of the industry's most important annual events.
