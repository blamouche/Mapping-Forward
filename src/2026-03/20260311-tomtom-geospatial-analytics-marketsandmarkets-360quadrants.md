# TomTom Recognized as a 'Star' in the Geospatial Analytics Market by 360Quadrants
**Source**: https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/blog/ICT/tom-tom-geospatial-analytics-360quadrants
**Date**: March 11, 2026
**Author**: MarketsandMarkets Research Private Ltd.
**Keywords**: TomTom, 360Quadrants, geospatial analytics, location intelligence, AI, machine learning, enterprise, autonomous driving, Overture Maps

## Elevator pitch
MarketsandMarkets' 360Quadrant analysis positions TomTom as a "Star" in geospatial analytics, citing its real-time traffic APIs, AI-driven map automation, and modular enterprise location intelligence platform.

## Takeaways
- TomTom was evaluated among 190+ companies on revenue, geographic reach, technology, and growth strategy
- Key capabilities include proprietary mapping data, real-time traffic APIs, geocoding, and spatial visualization
- The company serves insurance, retail, logistics, and urban planning verticals with location intelligence products
- AI-driven automated map production and participation in Overture Maps Foundation are highlighted as differentiators
- Strategic priorities span autonomous driving, ADAS, open ecosystem collaboration, and smart mobility

## Synthesis
The MarketsandMarkets 360Quadrant assessment provides a structured comparative analysis of TomTom's position in the geospatial analytics market. Evaluated against over 190 companies using a multi-dimensional framework — revenue scale, geographic presence, technology portfolio, and strategic growth trajectory — TomTom's "Star" designation indicates strong performance across these dimensions without necessarily claiming market leadership.

The assessment's emphasis on TomTom's API-driven service model reflects the company's deliberate repositioning away from consumer products toward developer and enterprise data services. The geocoding, spatial visualization, and mobility pattern analysis APIs form the commercial foundation of this model, enabling businesses in insurance, retail, logistics, and urban planning to integrate TomTom's location intelligence without building proprietary mapping capabilities. This B2B data-as-a-service approach is the engine of TomTom's long-term revenue thesis.

AI-driven map production automation receives specific mention as a differentiator. The ability to update global map data faster and more cost-effectively through machine learning applied to satellite imagery and sensor data is a genuine competitive advantage in a market where map freshness directly affects product quality. Traditional map update processes require significant human labor for verification and editing; automation shifts this economics while maintaining quality standards.

The Overture Maps Foundation membership is highlighted in the context of "open ecosystem collaboration" — a positioning choice that signals to enterprise customers that TomTom's data services are designed for interoperability with other geospatial standards and datasets. This matters for large organizations that cannot commit to proprietary lock-in and require their mapping data to integrate cleanly with GIS systems, data warehouses, and analytics platforms from multiple vendors.

The timing of this recognition, during TomTom's leadership transition, provides an external validation of the company's strategic direction that new CEO Mike Schoofs can leverage in enterprise sales conversations and investor relations.
