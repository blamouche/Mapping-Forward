# Google Maps : navigation 3D immersive en voiture et recherche en langage naturel sont en route
**Source**: https://www.igen.fr/app-store/2026/03/google-maps-navigation-3d-immersive-en-voiture-et-recherche-en-langage-naturel-sont-en-route-155203
**Date**: March 12, 2026
**Author**: Stéphane Moussie
**Keywords**: Google Maps, Immersive Navigation, Ask Maps, 3D navigation, natural language search, CarPlay, Android Auto, iOS, France

## Elevator pitch
Google Maps launches Immersive Navigation with 3D real-world rendering and Ask Maps with natural language search, extending from iOS and Android to CarPlay, Android Auto, and Google Built-in vehicles.

## Takeaways
- Immersive Navigation represents "the biggest evolution of driving directions in over a decade" using 3D technology
- The system highlights essential road information — traffic lanes, crosswalks — for complex maneuvers
- Ask Maps enables complex queries like "Are there any lit public tennis courts where I could play tonight?"
- Both features leverage Google's Gemini AI models for processing
- Full rollout includes iOS, Android, CarPlay, Android Auto, and Google Built-in vehicles; France launch timeline unannounced

## Synthesis
iGen.fr's coverage by Stéphane Moussie focuses particularly on the platform breadth of the Immersive Navigation rollout — a detail that other French tech publications give less emphasis. The explicit mention of CarPlay, Android Auto, and Google Built-in vehicles alongside iOS and Android reflects the significance of in-vehicle deployment: navigation improvements matter most when drivers are actually navigating, and the vehicle integration platforms are the primary navigation context for drivers with modern vehicles.

The "biggest evolution of driving directions in over a decade" framing from Google's own announcement is notable precisely because it acknowledges how limited navigation interface evolution has been since the transition from turn-by-turn text instructions to real-time traffic-aware routing. The core interaction model — voice prompts at decision points with a 2D overhead map — has remained fundamentally unchanged while the surrounding technology landscape transformed dramatically. Immersive Navigation represents the first substantive attempt to leverage the visual processing capabilities that modern devices now provide for genuinely different navigation presentation.

The Ask Maps example in this coverage — "Are there any lit public tennis courts where I could play tonight?" — is more specific than examples in other articles and illustrates the real-world complexity the feature is designed to handle. This query involves three simultaneous constraints: facility type (tennis court), condition requirement (lighting), and temporal availability (tonight). Standard keyword search would struggle with this; conversational AI processing against a location database with hours and amenity data can handle it coherently.

Moussie's note that no French launch timeline has been announced reflects both the technical reality of staged international rollouts and the regulatory context that influences Google's sequencing decisions for AI-enhanced features in European markets.
