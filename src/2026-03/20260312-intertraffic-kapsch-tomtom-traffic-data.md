# INTERTRAFFIC: Kapsch integrates TomTom traffic data
**Source**: https://www.traffictechnologytoday.com/news/event-news/intertraffic-kapsch-integrates-tomtom-traffic-data.html
**Date**: March 12, 2026
**Author**: Helen Norman
**Keywords**: Kapsch TrafficCom, TomTom, EcoTrafiX, floating car data, Intertraffic Amsterdam 2026, traffic management, smart cities

## Elevator pitch
At Intertraffic Amsterdam 2026, Kapsch TrafficCom showcased its EcoTrafiX platform enhanced with TomTom's floating car data for real-time traffic management without additional infrastructure.

## Takeaways
- TomTom's Floating Car Data and traffic services are integrated into Kapsch's EcoTrafiX Platform
- The integration delivers real-time insights into traffic flow, incidents, congestion patterns, and travel times
- Standard integration eliminates need for custom project-specific modifications, reducing deployment friction
- Multimodal movement data enables cities and road operators to make more informed mobility decisions
- The partnership was demonstrated live at Intertraffic Amsterdam 2026

## Synthesis
Traffic Technology Today's coverage of the Kapsch-TomTom partnership from Intertraffic Amsterdam 2026 provides trade publication context for an announcement that appeared across multiple industry channels simultaneously. The venue matters: Intertraffic is one of the primary gathering points for urban mobility professionals globally, making it the appropriate forum for demonstrating a partnership aimed at city traffic management authorities.

The editorial framing by Helen Norman emphasizes the operational simplicity of the integration — "eliminates need for custom project-specific modifications" — which is a significant selling point for public sector procurement. Municipal technology departments often lack the engineering resources to customize enterprise platforms significantly, so out-of-the-box integration with data services they already need is a practical procurement consideration, not merely a technical feature.

The multimodal movement data capability addresses a growing requirement in urban traffic management: understanding how people move through cities using combinations of modes — private vehicles, public transit, cycling, walking — rather than managing automobile flow in isolation. As cities pursue sustainability and equity goals, traffic management platforms that can see and optimize across modal boundaries become more valuable to progressive urban mobility programs.

The live demonstration at Intertraffic 2026 serves as both product validation and sales event. Decision-makers from city transportation departments attending the conference can evaluate the platform's capabilities directly, compressing the sales cycle for a product category where procurement decisions are complex and slow.
