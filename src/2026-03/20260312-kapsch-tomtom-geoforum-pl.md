# Kapsch TrafficCom wybiera TomTom Traffic do systemów zarządzania ruchem drogowym
**Source**: https://geoforum.pl/news/36817/kapsch-trafficcom-wybiera-tomtom-traffic-do-systemow-zarzadzania-ruchem-drogowym
**Date**: March 12, 2026
**Author**: Geoforum.pl
**Keywords**: Kapsch TrafficCom, TomTom Traffic, EcoTrafiX, traffic management, smart city, Poland, road traffic management systems

## Elevator pitch
Kapsch TrafficCom integrates TomTom Traffic into its EcoTrafiX Platform, providing Polish and global city authorities with real-time traffic data without additional infrastructure.

## Takeaways
- Kapsch TrafficCom integrates TomTom's data into EcoTrafiX for real-time traffic intensity, incidents, and travel times
- The partnership enables cities worldwide, including in Poland, to access multimodal traffic intelligence
- Standard integration removes the need for custom project modifications, simplifying city deployments
- Samuel Kapsch emphasized data's role in helping cities understand and combat congestion
- Mike Schoofs (TomTom CRO) highlighted enabling "faster and better decisions" through combined analytics
