# Waze, Google Maps: comment l'IA va révolutionner vos trajets (et votre façon de conduire)
**Source**: https://rmc.bfmtv.com/actualites/tech/waze-google-maps-comment-l-ia-va-revolutionner-vos-trajets-et-votre-facon-de-conduire_AV-202603260666.html
**Date**: March 26, 2026
**Author**: Anthony Morel
**Keywords**: Google Maps, Waze, AI, navigation, 3D maps

## Elevator pitch
Google Maps and Waze are shifting from turn-by-turn tools to AI-driven assistants, combining immersive 3D cartography with conversational search and trip planning.

## Takeaways
- Google Maps is rolling out a major visual redesign with 3D buildings, terrain, traffic lights, and crosswalk detail.
- Conversational AI will let users describe complex needs instead of navigating filters or menus.
- The platform relies on large-scale place data and community contributions to personalize results.
- New routing options emphasize fuel efficiency and driving compliance features.
- A French alternative, Roole Map, positions itself on pricing transparency and privacy.

## Synthesis
The article argues that navigation apps are entering their biggest shift in a decade by blending richer cartography with AI-driven interaction. Google Maps’ redesign is described as an immersive 3D experience that adds precise street-level detail—buildings, relief, traffic lights, and crosswalks—to make routes more readable and situationally aware. Alongside that visual upgrade, the core change is conversational AI: users will be able to describe scenarios in natural language, such as finding a late-night restaurant with parking or assembling a full weekend itinerary, and the app will interpret and plan accordingly. This pushes the GPS from a passive guide to a proactive assistant.

The piece highlights the scale of the underlying data and signals why AI is viable in this context: hundreds of millions of places and an even larger volume of user contributions to train recommendations and route optimization. It also emphasizes that the app learns from personal habits (frequent stops or preferences), indicating a move toward individualized routing and suggestions.

Beyond AI features, the article notes practical user functions that remain underused but valuable, such as saving parking locations, enabling fuel-efficient routes, and activating speed-related alerts. These are framed as immediate, actionable benefits while the larger AI transition unfolds. Finally, the text points to Roole Map as a rising French alternative, positioning itself on transparency and privacy by providing real-time fuel prices and trip cost estimates without collecting personal data. The takeaway is a competitive landscape where mainstream apps pursue AI-led personalization and immersive mapping, while challengers emphasize trust and cost control.
