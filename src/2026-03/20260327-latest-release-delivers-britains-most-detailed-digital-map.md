# Latest release delivers Britain's most detailed digital map
**Source**: https://www.ordnancesurvey.co.uk/news/latest-data-release-delivers-britains-most-detailed-digital-map
**Date**: March 27, 2026
**Author**: Press Office
**Keywords**: Ordnance Survey, OS NGD, national map, basemap, public sector

## Elevator pitch
Ordnance Survey expands the OS National Geographic Database with new datasets and enhancements, positioning it as Britain’s most detailed digital map and a core backbone for public‑sector services.

## Takeaways
- OS NGD now includes 16 data collections and 70 major enhancements.
- New coverage adds full GB bus and cycle lanes plus improved postcode areas.
- Retail areas and bridge data strengthen planning and emergency response use cases.
- OS is transitioning legacy products toward next‑gen basemaps like OS BaseMap Pro.
- Public‑sector access is enabled through the Public Sector Geospatial Agreement.

## Synthesis
Ordnance Survey announces a significant expansion of the OS National Geographic Database (NGD), describing it as the most detailed digital map of Great Britain. The update brings a larger, unified collection of authoritative datasets—16 collections and 70 enhancements—intended to improve access and attribution across public and commercial users. Notable additions include nationwide bus and cycle lane coverage, updated postcode unit boundaries, a new retail areas dataset, and improved structures data with third‑party bridge information to support emergency services.

The release is framed as a generational shift in OS data access and is tied to the Public Sector Geospatial Agreement, which enables thousands of public organizations to use the data for services such as transport management, healthcare planning, sustainability initiatives, and climate resilience. OS also outlines a product transition plan: older layers like OS MasterMap Topography will be retired in favor of OS BaseMap Pro, intended as a more modern, analytics‑ready basemap connected to NGD data. Overall, the announcement positions OS NGD as a foundational infrastructure for GB’s digital mapping ecosystem.
