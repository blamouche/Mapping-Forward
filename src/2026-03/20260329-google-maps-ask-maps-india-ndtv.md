# Google Maps' AI Feature 'Ask Maps' Comes To India: How Travellers Can Use It
**Source**: https://www.ndtv.com/travel/google-maps-ai-feature-ask-maps-comes-to-india-how-travellers-can-use-it-11290019
**Date**: March 29, 2026
**Author**: NDTV Travel
**Keywords**: Google Maps, Ask Maps, India, AI, conversational search, Gemini, travel planning

## Elevator pitch
Google Maps launches Ask Maps in India and the US simultaneously, enabling conversational AI-powered trip planning using 300 million locations, 500 million contributor insights, and real-time data for personalized recommendations.

## Takeaways
- Ask Maps available in English on Android and iOS in the US and India at launch
- Powered by Google's database of 300 million locations and 500 million contributor insights
- Supports complex contextual queries combining location, time, mood, and preferences
- Conversation history can be viewed and deleted by users for privacy control
- Alongside Ask Maps, Immersive Navigation with 3D visuals is also rolling out in select locations

## Synthesis
NDTV Travel covers the simultaneous India and US launch of Google Maps' Ask Maps feature — a notable choice by Google to expand this AI-powered capability to India at launch rather than treating it as a later-phase rollout. India's status as a co-launch market reflects both the strategic importance of the Indian Maps user base and Google's confidence in the feature's scalability.

Ask Maps represents a fundamental shift in how people interact with navigation apps. Instead of typing structured queries like "restaurants near me," users can now ask complex, contextual questions through text or voice. The feature draws on Google Maps' vast data ecosystem: 300 million locations worldwide, reviews and photos from 500 million contributors, traffic data, and Popular Times information. This combination enables genuinely personalized responses rather than generic search results.

NDTV provides a useful set of Google-approved example prompts that illustrate the feature's capabilities: planning a date night with specific ambiance requirements across locations between home and work; finding a quiet bar with particular characteristics within walking distance; identifying rooftop photo spots based on current sun position; planning a scenic running route to arrive at a landmark at sunset; generating three-day trip inspiration for a specific city.

These examples demonstrate that Ask Maps functions as a sophisticated intent interpreter — understanding not just "what" the user wants but "why," at "what time," and under "what constraints." This is qualitatively different from keyword-based search.

Privacy controls are specifically mentioned: conversation history can be viewed and deleted, giving users transparency and control over how the AI uses their interaction data. This addresses anticipated privacy concerns about conversational AI in a location context.

The simultaneous India launch, alongside immersive 3D navigation in select markets, positions India as a first-class participant in Google Maps' most ambitious feature expansion in years — rather than a recipient of delayed rollouts, as has historically often been the case.
