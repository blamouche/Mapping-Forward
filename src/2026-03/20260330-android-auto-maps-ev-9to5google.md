# Android Auto brings Google Maps EV trip planning to 350+ models from 16 brands
**Source**: https://9to5google.com/2026/03/30/android-auto-maps-ev-trip-planning/
**Date**: March 30, 2026
**Author**: 9to5Google
**Keywords**: Google Maps, Android Auto, EV trip planning, battery predictions, charging stops

## Elevator pitch
Android Auto now supports Google Maps EV trip planning for 350+ models from 16 brands, using AI and real-time Maps data to recommend charging stops, estimate arrival battery level, and update ETA.

## Takeaways
- Setup: Google Maps app > Settings > Your vehicles > Electric > Add vehicle details
- Adding current charge level unlocks charging stop recommendations and updated ETA
- Battery predictions combine AI energy models with real-time traffic, elevation, and weather
- Desired arrival battery level can be set for route adjustments
- Currently available in the US; more cars and markets expected soon

## Synthesis
9to5Google provides a clean, technically accurate summary of the Android Auto EV trip planning launch, structured to serve as a practical reference for readers who want to set up the feature. The article complements the feature announcement with specific step-by-step instructions.

The setup process as described: open Google Maps app on your phone, navigate to Settings > Your vehicles, set engine type to Electric, add vehicle details (make, model, year, trim), and confirm charging plug types. This configuration is a one-time setup that persists for future trips.

In-car usage: set a destination in Android Auto to see expected battery consumption for the trip. Add current charge level to receive recommended charging stops with timing, estimated arrival battery percentage, and an updated ETA that accounts for charging time. An additional preference allows setting a desired battery level at arrival — Google Maps will add charging stops as needed to meet this target.

9to5Google accurately describes the AI backend: "battery predictions are powered by Google AI and 'advanced energy models' that analyze vehicle weight and battery size" combined with real-time Maps data on traffic, elevation, and weather. This multi-factor approach distinguishes the feature from simple range calculations based solely on manufacturer specifications.

The article notes the complete list of brands supported at launch: Audi, BMW, Chevrolet, FIAT, Genesis, Hyundai, Jaguar, Kia, Lexus, Lucid, Mercedes-Benz, Nissan, Porsche, Subaru, Toyota, and Volkswagen. Support for more cars is described as coming soon.

This launch is positioned alongside other recent Google Maps upgrades — the 'Ask Maps' conversational AI feature and 'Immersive Navigation' — as part of a broader Maps modernization push in early 2026.
