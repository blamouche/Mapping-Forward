# Google Maps now predicts battery life for 350+ EVs
**Source**: https://www.androidpolice.com/google-maps-simplifies-battery-predictions-and-trip-planning-for-evs/
**Date**: March 30, 2026
**Author**: Karandeep Singh Oberoi, Android Police
**Keywords**: Google Maps, Android Auto, EV battery prediction, trip planning, Android Automotive

## Elevator pitch
Google Maps expands its EV charging features — previously exclusive to Android Automotive vehicles — to Android Auto, supporting 350+ models across 15+ brands with AI-powered battery predictions and charging stop recommendations.

## Takeaways
- Android Police was the first major publication to report on this update
- Feature extends Android Automotive's EV planning to the broader Android Auto ecosystem
- Setup requires vehicle details entered in Google Maps app; no direct vehicle communication
- Charging stop recommendations update based on driving and charging events
- Currently rolling out for US EVs across 15+ brands, more support expected soon

## Synthesis
Android Police was among the first publications to report on Google Maps' EV trip planning expansion to Android Auto, providing the technical setup instructions that became widely referenced across other coverage. The article clarifies an important industry distinction that helps contextualize this launch.

The key framing: "This isn't brand-new functionality. EV makers using Android Automotive have previously shipped this feature built in. The latest development essentially brings the feature to Android Auto (that runs off your phone), and expands it to over 350 car models." This distinction matters — Android Automotive is Google's full operating system embedded in the vehicle (Polestar, Volvo, Renault, GM models), whereas Android Auto is a phone projection interface. The EV planning feature has worked in Android Automotive for some time; this announcement extends it to the much larger Android Auto user base.

The setup process is straightforward: open Google Maps app, tap profile picture > Settings > Your vehicles, select Electric as engine type, add make/model/year/trim, confirm charging plugs, save. After setup, destinations set in Android Auto will display expected battery usage, and entering current charge level unlocks recommended charging stops, estimated arrival battery level, and an updated ETA.

One nuance not captured by all coverage: Android Police notes that after initial manual charge entry, "Google says the state of charge should update automatically based on your driving and charging stops." This suggests some dynamic updating is possible during the trip, though it relies on Maps' own models rather than live vehicle data.

The practical impact is significant: millions of Android Auto users with EVs from the 16 supported brands will gain access to integrated trip planning without switching to a specialized app. For a platform competing with Apple CarPlay and specialized EV routing apps, this is a meaningful capability upgrade.
