# TomTom selected by LOCUS to provide high-precision traffic data for location intelligence platform
**Source**: https://www.tomtom.com/newsroom/press-releases/general/160526227876208/tomtom-selected-by-locus-to-provide-high-precision-traffic-data-for-location-intelligence-platform/
**Date**: 2026-04-03
**Author**: TomTom Press Release
**Keywords**: TomTom, LOCUS, traffic data, location intelligence, logistics, last-mile delivery

## Elevator pitch
TomTom has been selected by LOCUS, a location intelligence platform for logistics, to provide high-precision traffic data that powers smarter last-mile delivery routing and fleet management.

## Takeaways
- LOCUS selected TomTom as its traffic data provider for its logistics intelligence platform
- High-precision traffic data improves route optimization and delivery time accuracy
- The partnership deepens TomTom's footprint in the logistics and last-mile delivery sector
- Location intelligence is increasingly central to supply chain efficiency
- TomTom continues to expand its B2B data partnerships beyond automotive

## Synthesis
TomTom has announced that LOCUS, a software platform serving logistics companies with location intelligence and route optimization, has selected TomTom as its provider of high-precision traffic data. While the press release provides limited detail, the announcement signals an important dimension of TomTom's commercial strategy: diversifying its data licensing business beyond automotive OEM partnerships into enterprise logistics.

LOCUS serves businesses with complex last-mile delivery operations — e-commerce fulfillment, field service management, and transportation logistics. For these use cases, traffic data accuracy directly translates to business value: more precise delivery time windows, better fleet utilization, and reduced fuel costs. High-precision, real-time traffic data is therefore a foundational input for any competitive logistics intelligence platform.

TomTom's traffic data product is built on a combination of proprietary probe data (GPS signals from connected vehicles and devices), third-party feeds, and machine learning models that infer traffic conditions across road segments lacking direct measurement. This multi-source approach gives TomTom strong global coverage, particularly in markets where public traffic data is sparse.

This partnership reflects a broader trend in the location intelligence market: as logistics companies face increasing pressure on delivery efficiency and sustainability, they are investing in more sophisticated spatial analytics platforms. Traffic data providers like TomTom, HERE, and Alphabet's traffic systems are competing to become the preferred data layer for these enterprise workflows.

For TomTom, this represents continued execution on its pivot from being primarily a consumer GPS device maker to a B2B location technology company — a transformation that has accelerated over the past several years as automotive and enterprise data licensing have grown in strategic importance.
