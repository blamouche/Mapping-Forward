# Explore Route 66 on Google Maps

**Date:** 29 April 2026
**Source:** Google Blog (The Keyword)
**Author:** Google
**Keywords:** Route 66, Google Maps, Street View, centennial, road trip, landmarks, navigation

---

In celebration of Route 66's 100th anniversary, Google Maps is rolling out two new ways to explore the iconic 2,448-mile highway stretching from Chicago to Santa Monica.

First, when searching for "U.S. Route 66" on Maps, users will now see the entire route mapped out on Android or iOS devices. Street View allows virtual visits to landmarks including Elmer's Bottle Tree Ranch in California and Cadillac Ranch in Texas.

Second, Google has published a shared list highlighting well-known landmarks, hidden gems, and the best local restaurants along the route. Whether driving the route or exploring from home, Maps now provides comprehensive coverage of the "Mother Road" for its centennial celebration.
