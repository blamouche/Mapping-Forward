# Mapping the most critical shipping lanes in the world

**Source:** [ABC News (Australia)](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-04-24/mapping-the-most-critical-shipping-lanes-in-the-world/106567522) / [FT maritime chokepoints interactive](https://ig.ft.com/maritime-chokepoints/)

## Elevator Pitch

About 90% of all traded goods are shipped by sea, passing through 13 critical choke points that handle roughly three-quarters of global maritime trade. With the Strait of Hormuz effectively blocked during the Iran war — and Iran charging tolls on passing vessels — decades of international shipping conventions are under threat. This article maps the five most critical straits and explores how the Hormuz precedent could reshape maritime trade, with Indonesia already floating the idea of tolls on the Malacca Strait.

## Key Points

- **Strait of Hormuz:** 20% of world oil output, 11% of all seaborne trade — effectively blocked by Iran during the war
- **Iran's toll precedent:** One ship reportedly paid $2M to pass; Indonesia suggested tolls on the Malacca Strait
- **Strait of Malacca:** 90,000+ ships/year, 30% of world traded goods, 80% of China's energy imports
- **13 key choke points globally** handle ~75% of maritime trade; 5 are considered most critical
- **UNCLOS** (UN Convention on Law of the Sea) protects transit rights through international straits, but Iran (non-signatory) has broken with decades of compliance
- **FT interactive** shows flows of crude, petroleum products, LNG, and container traffic through these chokepoints
- **Context:** Set against the broader Iran conflict timeline (Feb-May 2026)
