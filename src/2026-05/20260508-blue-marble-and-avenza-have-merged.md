# Blue Marble and Avenza Have Merged
**Source**: https://www.geoweeknews.com/news/blue-marble-and-avenza-have-merged
**Date**: May 8, 2026
**Author**: Eric van Rees
**Keywords**: GIS, merger, Blue Marble, Avenza, lidar, mapping, Global Mapper, Avenza Maps, offline maps

## Elevator pitch
Blue Marble Geographics and Avenza Systems have merged into a single unified brand, combining lidar processing, high-precision desktop mapping, and offline mobile GIS tools into an integrated workflow spanning both office and field operations.

## Takeaways
- The merger unites Global Mapper (desktop GIS/lidar) with Avenza Maps (offline mobile GPS), creating an end-to-end field-to-office geospatial workflow
- Global Mapper 27 was released immediately after the merger, integrating the GeoCalc geodetic SDK directly into the platform for coordinate intelligence
- New lidar validation and QA features include horizontal quality assurance, flight path alignment, and ASPRS density measurement compliance
- Patrick Stevenson joins as Chief Product Officer to accelerate innovation across the combined product suite
- The combined organization targets AEC, energy, natural resources, defense, and public sector markets with a single connected system

## Synthesis
The merger of Blue Marble Geographics and Avenza Systems represents a significant consolidation in the mid-market GIS software landscape, bringing together two established brands with complementary product lines. Blue Marble, founded in 1993 and headquartered in Hallowell, Maine, has long been known for Global Mapper — a versatile GIS application supporting over 250 spatial data formats — and Geographic Calculator, a specialized tool for geodetic coordinate conversion and datum transformation. Avenza brings to the table its flagship Avenza Maps mobile application, which enables offline map use on iOS and Android devices using geospatial PDF, GeoPDF, and GeoTIFF formats, serving both outdoor recreation and professional field work.

The strategic logic is clear: Blue Marble's desktop and processing capabilities (particularly in lidar and coordinate systems) pair naturally with Avenza's mobile field data collection tools. The end goal, as stated by the company, is to create a single connected system that reduces friction between field data capture and office-based analysis. This mirrors broader industry trends toward integrated workflows that eliminate the traditional gap between data collection and processing environments.

The immediate tangible output of the merger is Global Mapper 27, which integrates the GeoCalc SDK — a fully object-oriented geodetic class library — directly into the platform. This release also brings substantial lidar enhancements: horizontal quality assurance for flight path alignment and ground control point accuracy, guided breakline extraction using intelligent automation, support for evolving data standards including ASPRS density measurement methods, and expanded format support for E57 export, LAS 1.5 with GPS time offsets, TFADS-O, and GGF formats. These additions position Global Mapper 27 as a more complete single-environment solution for high-precision geospatial work, reducing the need for users to switch between multiple specialized tools.

The appointment of Patrick Stevenson as Chief Product Officer signals an intent to accelerate product integration rather than simply maintaining two separate product lines under one corporate umbrella. The challenge will be executing this integration without alienating existing user bases — Global Mapper users are typically desktop GIS professionals, while Avenza Maps users span a broader range from surveyors to hikers. Successfully bridging these communities while maintaining the distinct value propositions of each product will be key to realizing the merger's full potential.
