# TomTom dostarczy LOCUS precyzyjne dane o ruchu drogowym
**Source**: https://www.rynekinfrastruktury.pl/wiadomosci/drogi-i-autostrady/tomtom-dostarczy-locus-precyzyjne-dane-o-ruchu-drogowym-99448.html
**Date**: May 10, 2026
**Author**: Rynek Infrastruktury (LOCUS/TomTom press release)
**Keywords**: TomTom, LOCUS, traffic data, mobility analytics, smart cities, North America, AI, digital twins

## Elevator pitch
TomTom has partnered with LOCUS, a mobility analytics firm, to integrate its traffic data into LOCUS's AI-powered platform, supporting smarter cities, optimized mobility networks, and digital twin applications across North America.

## Takeaways
- TomTom Traffic data will be integrated into LOCUS's analytics and predictive modeling platform for public and private sector clients
- Applications include real estate/infrastructure planning, transport modeling, traffic management, and parking/facility management
- The partnership targets faster, data-driven decision-making that improves quality of life, reduces costs, and cuts emissions
- Mike Schoofs (TomTom CRO) emphasized the partnership supports mobility decisions based on data
- Moby Khan (LOCUS Head of Product) stressed that TomTom's high-quality traffic data is an "essential element" of their AI-powered location analytics platform

## Synthesis
This Polish infrastructure news outlet reports on a partnership between TomTom and LOCUS, a mobility analysis and location data firm focused on the North American market. The integration of TomTom Traffic with LOCUS's predictive analytics platform is positioned as a response to "growing demand for data-driven mobility systems" — a trend accelerated by smart city initiatives, digital twin projects, and the need for infrastructure resilience planning.

The partnership's scope is broad: real-time and historical traffic data will feed LOCUS's AI models across multiple verticals including transport modeling, traffic management, parking optimization, real estate development, and facility management. The promised outcome is "faster and more proactive decision-making" — moving from reactive traffic management to predictive optimization.

Notable is Mike Schoofs' title in this press release: Chief Revenue Officer, TomTom. Schoofs was recently promoted to CEO (as covered in a separate synthesis), suggesting this press release may have been drafted during the transition period. The partnership exemplifies TomTom's strategy of monetizing its traffic data beyond consumer navigation apps — positioning itself as a data infrastructure provider for the broader mobility ecosystem, including urban planning and commercial real estate.
