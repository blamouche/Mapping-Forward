# TomTom: Serverprobleme behoben, gespeicherte Inhalte wieder da
**Source**: https://www.iphone-ticker.de/tomtom-serverprobleme-behoben-verkehrswarner-zum-bestpreis-277724/
**Date**: May 11, 2026
**Author**: iphone-ticker.de (Chris)
**Keywords**: TomTom, server outage, data recovery, saved places, favorites, cloud sync, Tom by TomTom, traffic warning

## Elevator pitch
TomTom has resolved the server problems that caused user-saved places and favorites to disappear since May 5; data from before May 1 has been restored, though content created between May 1-9 may be permanently lost.

## Takeaways
- The root cause was a technical issue with account login and associated data synchronization
- TomTom confirmed all originally saved user data has been restored, but data from May 1-9 may not be recoverable
- The issue affected apps, online functions, and navigation devices with sync capabilities
- Some users still report missing data as of May 11 and need to log out/in or restart
- The article also covers a deal on "Tom by TomTom," a traffic warning device now priced at €59.99

## Synthesis
The iphone-ticker.de article provides the most detailed post-mortem of the TomTom cloud incident among the German-language coverage. According to TomTom's statement, the error was caused by a "technical problem related to logging into a TomTom user account and the associated data synchronization." The key finding: while pre-existing data has been restored from backups, any routes, favorites, or saved places created or modified between May 1 and May 9, 2026 may be permanently lost — suggesting the backup restoration point is several days before the outage began.

User comments on the article reveal the uneven nature of the recovery. While some report "everything back," others still see empty favorites lists as of May 11. Workarounds suggested include logging out and back in, restarting the device, and in one case, clearing the Safari browser history on iPhone to trigger a re-sync. The article also notes that German users have specific legal restrictions on radar warning devices — using such a device while driving carries a €75 fine and one point on the license.

The second half of the article shifts to product news: "Tom by TomTom," a traffic warning device that alerts drivers to speed cameras, traffic jams, and obstacles, is now available on Amazon Germany for €59.99 (down from €79.99 direct from TomTom). The device works as a companion to the TomTom app without requiring phone interaction, using Bluetooth proximity for alerts.
