# TomTom verliert Nutzerdaten – jetzt läuft die Datenrettung
**Source**: https://www.pcwelt.de/article/3135553/tomtom-verliert-nutzerdaten-jetzt-laeuft-die-datenrettung.html
**Date**: May 11, 2026
**Author**: PC-WELT
**Keywords**: TomTom, data loss, cloud outage, user data, saved places, favorites, data recovery, navigation

## Elevator pitch
German coverage of TomTom's server-side data loss incident that wiped user-saved places and favorites starting May 5, with recovery efforts underway by May 10-11.

## Takeaways
- The problem was confirmed as a technical issue related to account login and data synchronization
- Data created or modified between May 1 and May 9 may not be recoverable according to TomTom
- The issue affected all platforms: mobile apps, web portal, and dedicated navigation devices with sync capabilities
- TomTom acknowledged the problem and confirmed data restoration was in progress
- This is essentially duplicate coverage of the borncity.com report on the same incident

## Synthesis
This PC-WELT article provides additional German-language coverage of the TomTom cloud outage that began May 5, 2026. The article confirms technical details already reported by borncity.com and the TomTom forum thread: the incident was caused by a technical problem related to account authentication and cloud data synchronization. All platforms were affected — iOS and Android apps, the plan.tomtom.com web portal, and dedicated navigation devices with sync functionality.

One notable detail: TomTom stated that data created or modified between May 1 and May 9, 2026 "may not be recoverable," suggesting the backup/restore point predates the outage by several days. By May 10-11, most users reported their data had been restored, though the process appeared to be rolling out gradually rather than all at once. The incident highlights the dependency of modern navigation platforms on cloud infrastructure and raises questions about data resilience practices at a company that provides mission-critical mapping services to automakers and enterprises.
