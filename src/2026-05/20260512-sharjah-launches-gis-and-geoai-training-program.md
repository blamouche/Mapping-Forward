# Sharjah launches GIS and GeoAI training programme for government employees

**Date:** 12 May 2026
**Source:** ETHRWorld EMEA (Economic Times)
**Author:** Online Bureau
**Keywords:** Sharjah, GIS, GeoAI, training, government, digital transformation, Esri, ArcGIS, UAE

---

Sharjah Digital Department has launched a year-long specialised training programme focused on Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and GeoAI applications for government employees. The initiative, in collaboration with the Department of Human Resources, aims to enhance digital and geospatial capabilities using Esri technologies.

The programme includes foundational and advanced workshops centred around the ArcGIS platform, covering spatial analytics, geospatial data management, and smart dashboard applications aimed at improving data-driven decision-making across government operations.

Lamia Al Hosan Al Shamsi from Sharjah Digital Department stated that the programme enables government entities to leverage GIS and GeoAI technologies as strategic tools supporting decision-making and operational efficiency. The initiative forms part of broader Gulf-region efforts to strengthen digital skills, AI readiness, and data capabilities within the public sector as digital transformation strategies accelerate across the Middle East.
