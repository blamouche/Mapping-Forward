## Elevator pitch
TomTom GO Navigation brings professional-grade navigation to smartphones with offline maps across Europe, real-time traffic updates, and route customization for private users and small fleets.

## Takeaways
- TomTom GO Navigation offers full turn-by-turn navigation on Android and iOS with offline maps for multiple European countries
- Users can download maps in advance and navigate without active data connection, avoiding roaming costs
- The app provides real-time traffic updates, lane guidance, road closure information and alternative routes when connected
- Favorites and frequent destinations can be saved for quick access; routes can be customized by avoiding specific road types
- The app serves both private drivers and small business fleets, with regular map updates keeping route planning current

## Synthesis
TomTom GO Navigation brings the company's established navigation technology to smartphones and tablets, positioning itself as a dedicated alternative to the navigation features built into general-purpose mapping platforms. The app's core differentiator is its offline capability: users download maps for individual countries or regions in advance and can navigate without an active data connection, making it particularly practical for cross-border travel in Europe where roaming charges may apply.

The app provides turn-by-turn navigation with voice guidance, clear lane instructions, and dynamic rerouting based on live traffic conditions when a data connection is available. Road closures, traffic jams, and other disruptions are factored into route calculations. Users can customize navigation by avoiding specific road types and save frequent destinations like home, work, or regularly visited addresses for faster access.

In the German and European market, TomTom GO Navigation competes with the navigation features of major mapping platforms like Google Maps and Waze, differentiating itself through its specialized navigation focus and offline map functionality. The app targets both private users — commuters, business travelers, holiday drivers — and small commercial fleets where flexible, up-to-date navigation without dedicated hardware is valuable. For tradespeople and delivery services operating in dense urban areas, regular map updates and traffic-aware routing help make journey times more predictable.

The product sits within TomTom's broader strategy of offering navigation solutions across consumer and enterprise segments — from mobile apps for end users to embedded systems for automakers and traffic data services for governments. As a publicly listed company on Euronext Amsterdam (ISIN NL0000387058), TomTom's consumer app business represents one access channel to end customers alongside its B2B mapping and traffic data operations.
