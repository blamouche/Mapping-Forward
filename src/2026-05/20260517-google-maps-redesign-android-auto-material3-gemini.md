# Google Maps Redesign on Android Auto: Material 3 Expressive, 3D Navigation, and Gemini AI Integration
**Source**: https://www.numerama.com/tech/2254251-google-maps-change-de-look-sur-android-auto-encore-une-polemique-lodyssee-le-recap-de-la-semaine.html
**Date**: May 17, 2026
**Author**: Julien Lausson
**Keywords**: Google Maps, Android Auto, Material 3 Expressive, 3D navigation, Gemini AI, interface redesign, in-car UX

## Elevator pitch
Google Maps on Android Auto is rolling out a comprehensive redesign featuring Material 3 Expressive UI, 3D immersive navigation views, and Gemini AI contextual assistance — marking a major visual and functional evolution of the in-car navigation experience.

## Takeaways
- Android Auto adopts Material 3 Expressive design language, enabling a more dynamic and customizable interface compared to the current Material Design.
- Google Maps transitions to 3D immersive navigation for enriched cartographic rendering — buildings, landmarks, and terrain gain visual depth.
- Gemini AI integrates into Android Auto to provide contextual driving assistance, complementing the broader AI assistant push across Google's ecosystem.
- The redesign represents Android Auto's most significant visual overhaul to date.
- The broader context includes Apple CarPlay opening to third-party AI assistants via iOS 26.4, creating a competitive in-car AI landscape.
- This evolution turns car dashboards into full computing interfaces rather than simple screen-mirroring systems.

## Synthesis
Android Auto is undergoing its most substantial visual and functional transformation, with Google Maps at the center of the redesign. The new Material 3 Expressive design language brings dynamic, customizable interface elements that move beyond the restrained Material Design aesthetic that has defined Android Auto since its inception.

Three pillars define the update. First, the visual layer: Material 3 Expressive allows the interface to adapt more fluidly to different screen sizes and contexts, with richer color palettes and motion design. Second, navigation shifts to a 3D immersive mode that renders buildings, landmarks, and terrain with spatial depth — a move that brings Google Maps on Android Auto closer to the experience already present on phone apps and rival navigation platforms. Third, and most strategically, Gemini AI is being embedded directly into the driving context, offering contextual assistance that goes beyond turn-by-turn directions.

The timing is significant. As Apple has been forced by iOS 26.4 regulations to open CarPlay to third-party AI assistants — already welcoming ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Grok — Google's integration of Gemini into Maps on Android Auto is a defensive and offensive play simultaneously. It defends Android's automotive foothold by deepening Google's own AI integration, while also pushing the experience forward before Apple's next-generation CarPlay fully materializes.

The broader implication: car dashboards are becoming contested computing platforms. The redesign reflects a recognition that navigation is no longer just about maps — it's about the entire in-car digital experience, from entertainment to productivity to AI-powered concierge services. Google is positioning Maps + Gemini as the hub of that ecosystem.
