# TomTom brings intelligent location tech to Motor City
**Source**: https://www.itsinternational.com/daily-news/its-america-2026/tomtom-brings-intelligent-location-tech-motor-city
**Date**: May 19, 2026
**Author**: Unknown
**Keywords**: TomTom, ITS America 2026, intelligent location technology, traffic management, AI Traffic Agent, Detroit, smart cities

## Elevator pitch
At ITS America 2026 in Detroit, TomTom will demonstrate how its maps, real-time traffic insights, and AI Traffic Agent enable natural-language querying of road conditions, congestion patterns, and incident hotspots to support smarter transportation networks.

## Takeaways
- TomTom showcases intelligent location technology at ITS America 2026 to help transport systems become safer and more efficient.
- The AI Traffic Agent allows analysts to ask natural-language questions about real-time road conditions with historical context.
- Demonstrations target cities, road authorities, State DOTs, and mobility providers for improved traffic flow, safety, and incident response.
- Partner showcases will highlight how public agencies and technology providers collaborate to accelerate innovation.
- Craig Smith emphasizes moving beyond concepts to actual deployment at real scale in real cities.

## Synthesis
TomTom is positioning itself at ITS America 2026 in Detroit as a provider of practical, deployable location intelligence for transportation networks. The company's core message is a shift from conceptual discussions about smart infrastructure to tangible applications that cities and road authorities can use today. At Booth 3036, demonstrations will cover three main areas: maps, real-time traffic insights, and advanced location intelligence tools designed to support data-driven decision-making across multiple stakeholders — from municipal traffic managers to State Departments of Transportation and private mobility providers.

The standout technology being showcased is the TomTom AI Traffic Agent, which represents a significant evolution in how transportation data is accessed and analyzed. Rather than requiring analysts to navigate complex dashboards and manually correlate datasets, the AI Traffic Agent accepts natural-language queries and returns contextualized insights about real-time road conditions, historical congestion patterns, incident hotspots, and overall network performance. This interface lowers the barrier to data-driven traffic management, potentially democratizing access to sophisticated analytics that previously required specialized training.

TomTom's approach at the event emphasizes partnership and ecosystem development. The booth will feature multiple partner showcases, and conference sessions will explore how collaboration between public agencies, infrastructure partners, and technology providers is accelerating innovation in intelligent transport systems. Craig Smith, representing TomTom, framed the event as an opportunity to demonstrate how location technology can address current transport challenges while also supporting future needs. The emphasis on "real roads, in real cities, at real scale" underscores a pragmatic positioning that contrasts with more speculative smart-city narratives — TomTom is selling solutions that work now, built on its established mapping and traffic intelligence platform, rather than promising future capabilities.
