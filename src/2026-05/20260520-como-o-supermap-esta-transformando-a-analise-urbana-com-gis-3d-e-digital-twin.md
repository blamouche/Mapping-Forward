## Elevator pitch
SuperMap, Asia's leading GIS platform and the world's second-largest GIS vendor, is advancing urban analysis through integrated 3D GIS and Digital Twin technologies that automate spatial analysis, integrate heterogeneous data sources, and enable real-time decision-making for smart cities.

## Takeaways
- SuperMap iDesktopX provides advanced spatial data editing, analysis, and modeling in a robust desktop environment, while iServer enables high-performance geospatial service publishing and iPortal serves as a centralized hub for map and service governance.
- The platform creates highly detailed 3D digital representations of cities by integrating geospatial maps, 3D buildings, BIM models, satellite imagery, point clouds, and real-time sensor data.
- Automation of complex spatial analysis workflows — traditionally manual processes — delivers significant gains in productivity, precision, and speed for territory validation, data cross-referencing, and cartographic product generation.
- The combined 3D GIS and Digital Twin approach supports simulations, real-time monitoring, and advanced spatial analysis for urban planning, utilities, environment, transportation, and defense sectors.
- SuperMap positions itself at the intersection of AI, big geo-data, and cloud computing, targeting the growing demand for integrated territorial intelligence in smart city projects.

## Synthesis
This MundoGEO article, published as sponsored content by SuperMap, profiles the company's suite of geospatial tools and their application to urban analysis through 3D GIS and Digital Twin technology. Founded in 1997, SuperMap is recognized as the leading GIS software provider in Asia and holds the number-two position globally, serving public and private organizations across urban planning, utilities, environmental management, transportation, defense, and smart cities.

The article walks through three core components of the SuperMap platform. SuperMap iDesktopX is the desktop-side offering for advanced spatial data editing, analysis, and modeling in an intuitive environment. SuperMap iServer handles high-performance publishing of geospatial services and applications, facilitating data sharing and analysis in corporate and cloud environments. SuperMap iPortal functions as a centralized management platform for organizing and disseminating maps, services, and applications, promoting collaboration and data governance across organizations.

The core value proposition is the integration of 2D and 3D GIS with Digital Twin technology. The platform creates highly detailed digital representations of cities, infrastructure, and territories by combining geospatial base maps, 3D building models, BIM models, satellite imagery, point cloud data, real-time sensor feeds, and corporate databases. Beyond visualization, the Digital Twin approach enables simulations, monitoring, and advanced real-time spatial analysis — transforming the model from a static representation into a dynamic analytical environment.

The article particularly emphasizes the automation benefits. Traditional urban analysis workflows often involve complex manual geoprocessing, data validation, and cross-referencing across multiple sources. SuperMap's automation tools accelerate these processes with claimed improvements in productivity, error reduction, technical precision, and speed in approval and inspection workflows. The platform's ability to integrate heterogeneous data sources — GIS, BIM, remote sensing, IoT sensors, and enterprise databases — into a unified geospatial environment is presented as a key differentiator for enabling faster, more strategic decision-making.

The piece concludes by introducing Digimap Sistemas as SuperMap's partner in Brazil, noting SuperMap's confirmed presence as an exhibitor at the DroneShow, MundoGEO Connect, SpaceBR Show, and Expo eVTOL 2026 events in São Paulo (June 16-18). As sponsored content, the article naturally emphasizes product capabilities over critical analysis, but it provides a useful overview of SuperMap's platform architecture and its positioning in the increasingly competitive 3D GIS and urban digital twin market.
