# Google I/O 2026 : Gemini partout, Ask YouTube, Ask Maps et 900 millions d'utilisateurs

**Author**: Virginie Achouch

## Elevator pitch
Google I/O 2026 confirms a sweeping AI-first strategy: Gemini surpasses 900 million monthly users, Ask Maps receives its biggest update in a decade, and a new Universal Cart unifies shopping across all Google services.

## Takeaways
- Gemini reaches 900M monthly active users (up from 400M), processing 3.2 quadrillion tokens/month (7× increase).
- Ask Maps gets its biggest update in 10 years, allowing complex natural-language queries about routes and surroundings.
- Ask YouTube enables AI-powered video search delivering digestible clips in response to natural-language questions.
- Universal Cart unifies shopping across Search, Gemini, YouTube, and Gmail with automatic price tracking and compatibility checks.
- Gemini 3.5 Flash is 4× faster than comparable frontier models at half the cost; Gemini Omni enables multimodal generation (video, images, text).
- Android XR audio glasses developed with Samsung, Gentle Monster, and Warby Parker, with Gemini built in, launching autumn 2026.
- SynthID has watermarked 100 billion images/videos and 60,000 years of audio content; OpenAI and Kakao join the programme.

## Synthesis
Virginie Achouch's comprehensive recap of Google I/O 2026 reveals a company executing an aggressive "AI everywhere" playbook. The keynote, led by Sundar Pichai, showcased staggering growth numbers: Gemini's user base more than doubled year-over-year to 900 million, token processing multiplied sevenfold to 3.2 quadrillion monthly, and AI Overviews now serve 2.5 billion users.

For the mapping domain, the headline is Ask Maps — Google's biggest Maps update in a decade. The feature allows users to ask complex, contextual questions about routes, surroundings, and points of interest in natural language, transforming Maps from a navigation tool into a conversational local discovery platform. This aligns with Google's broader pattern of layering Gemini's reasoning capabilities onto its massive geospatial data assets.

The Universal Cart represents a particularly ambitious product: a cross-service shopping basket that works across Search, Gemini, YouTube, and Gmail, tracking prices, detecting product incompatibilities, and enabling checkout via Google Pay. It signals Google's intent to own the entire purchase journey — from discovery through AI recommendations to transaction.

On the model frontier, Gemini 3.5 Flash delivers frontier performance at half the price and quadruple speed, while Gemini Omni introduces multimodal output generation. The hardware play with Android XR audio glasses (partnering with Samsung and eyewear brands Gentle Monster and Warby Parker) shows Google learning from the Google Glass failure: design-first, fashion-partnered, with Gemini as the core OS. With $180-190 billion in planned capex for 2026, Google is betting at unprecedented scale that AI will be the connective tissue of its entire product ecosystem.
