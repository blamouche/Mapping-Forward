# TomTom erweitert seinen bildschirmfreien Fahrbegleiter "Tom"
**Source**: https://www.iphone-ticker.de/tom-tomtom-erweitert-seinen-bildschirmfreien-fahrbegleiter-278428/
**Date**: May 22, 2026
**Author**: Nicolas (iphone-ticker.de)
**Keywords**: TomTom, Tom, screen-free driving companion, traffic alerts, Pfingsten, firmware update

## Elevator pitch
TomTom expands its screen-free driving companion "Tom" with firmware updates addressing connectivity issues, as Pfingsten traffic data shows Hamburg experienced double the normal Friday congestion.

## Takeaways
- Tom is a display-free device providing audio traffic and hazard alerts via the TomTom smartphone app
- Pfingsten traffic data: Hamburg hit ~200% of normal Friday levels; Lower Saxony, Schleswig-Holstein, Baden-Württemberg also heavily affected
- Firmware updates since launch focused on iPhone connectivity reliability and alert delivery consistency
- New "RouteBar" feature in the TomTom app compactly displays key route information
- Device price reduced to €59 (from €79) for Pfingsten weekend, no subscription required

## Synthesis
TomTom is using the high-traffic Pfingsten weekend as a backdrop to promote its screen-free driving companion "Tom," a device launched in autumn 2025 that provides audio-based traffic and hazard alerts without requiring drivers to look at a screen. The article opens with TomTom traffic data showing Hamburg's congestion levels at roughly double a normal Friday during the Pfingsten travel wave, with multiple German states experiencing significantly elevated traffic.

"Tom" is positioned as a companion for commuters and drivers on familiar routes who don't need constant navigation but benefit from real-time hazard and traffic alerts. Unlike traditional navigation devices, it has no display — it communicates entirely through audio signals working alongside the TomTom smartphone app running in the background.

Since launch, TomTom has processed user feedback leading to several firmware updates addressing the two most common complaints: connection reliability between the device and iPhones, and inconsistent delivery of warning alerts. Volume levels and the duration of visual notifications in the companion app were also adjusted.

The companion TomTom app has been updated with a "RouteBar" feature that compacts key route information into a glanceable display, which TomTom reports is among its most popular features. The device, which requires no subscription, has been discounted from €79 to €59 for the holiday weekend.

The article reflects TomTom's strategy of expanding beyond traditional navigation into ambient driver assistance — a product category that bridges dedicated GPS devices and smartphone apps by providing continuous awareness without demanding visual attention.
