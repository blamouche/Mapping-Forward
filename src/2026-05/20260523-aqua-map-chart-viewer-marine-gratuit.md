# Aqua Map Chart Viewer, cartes marines gratuites en ligne
**Source**: https://www.yacht.de/fr/connaissance/navigation/navigation-aqua-map-chart-viewer-cartes-marines-gratuites-en-ligne-evenement-de-grand-voilier-comme-exemple-en-direct/
**Date**: May 23, 2026
**Author**: Hauke Schmidt
**Keywords**: Aqua Map, nautical charts, marine navigation, web chart viewer, NOAA, weather overlay, sailing

## Elevator pitch
Aqua Map launches a free browser-based nautical chart viewer covering most global waters with weather overlays, positioning itself as a planning companion for sailors ahead of the Sail4th 250 tall ship celebration in New York.

## Takeaways
- Free web-based viewer requires no registration or app installation; works in all modern browsers
- Covers Germany, Netherlands, England, Mediterranean, and US waters (Denmark excluded due to licensing)
- Weather overlays: NOAA HRRR wind model (3km resolution, US coasts), Wavewatch III (global), ocean currents (global)
- 48-hour forecast slider and 7-day weather forecast by tapping on the map
- Route planning and mobile sync require paid subscription (~€25 premium + €20 for German charts)

## Synthesis
Aqua Map has introduced a free, browser-based nautical chart viewer that allows sailors to consult marine charts and weather data without installing any application or creating an account. The Chart Viewer positions itself as a planning tool for pre-voyage preparation on desktop computers, complementing the existing Aqua Map mobile navigation app for iOS and Android.

The free tier covers most global maritime zones — Germany, Netherlands, England, the Mediterranean, and the United States — with a notable exception: Denmark blocks online display of its nautical charts for licensing reasons, affecting North Sea and Baltic passage planning. Weather integration is the standout feature: wind data from NOAA's HRRR model (3km resolution, hourly updates for US coastal waters), wave data from Wavewatch III (global), and ocean currents from the Global Real-Time Ocean Forecast System. A 48-hour forecast slider and color-coded intensity display make conditions immediately readable.

The free version is deliberately limited to consultation: no route planning, no synchronization with the mobile app, and no offline functionality. Full capabilities — creating routes on the web viewer and syncing them to mobile devices — require a paid subscription (approximately €25 for premium access plus €20 for German charts).

A practical demonstration of the platform's capabilities comes from the Sail4th 250 Celebration (July 3-8, 2026, New York/New Jersey), where Aqua Map serves as the official navigation partner. A specially expanded version of the Chart Viewer will allow live tracking of the largest tall ship fleet in US history, including the German training ship "Gorch Fock," providing real-time fleet tracking for spectators and participants.
