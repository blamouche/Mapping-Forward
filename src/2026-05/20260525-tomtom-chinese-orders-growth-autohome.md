# TomTom中国订单增长10倍 将助力更多的中国车厂出海
**Source**: https://chejiahao.autohome.com.cn/info/25552936
**Date**: May 25, 2026
**Author**: CNMO科技 (via 汽车之家 车家号)
**Keywords**: TomTom, Chinese automakers, overseas expansion, ADAS, navigation, Huawei Petal Maps, automotive globalization, 10x orders

## Elevator pitch
CNMO科技 reports on TomTom's 10x surge in China-related orders and the company's role as the overseas mapping infrastructure enabling Chinese automakers' global intelligent driving push.

## Takeaways
- TomTom's China-related orders grew tenfold from 2024 to 2025, reflecting accelerating demand from Chinese OEMs expanding globally.
- The company positions itself as the "overseas version of AutoNavi" with full-stack map data, flexible delivery models (data-only, services-only, or turnkey), and coverage across 235 countries.
- TomTom's Greater China team achieved six-month mass production on a Chinese client project, demonstrating rapid integration capabilities.
- Traffic light countdown recognition co-developed with Huawei Petal Maps completed Madrid pilot testing, with European scale-up planned for H2 2026.
- Core goals: improving user experience perception and securing additional orders to support more Chinese automakers in overseas markets.

## Synthesis

CNMO科技's article on 汽车之家's 车家号 platform summarizes the 36Kr interview with TomTom executives, focusing on the European mapping company's explosive growth in the Chinese market. The headline metric — a tenfold increase in China-related orders from 2024 to 2025 — anchors the narrative that TomTom has become a strategic enabler for Chinese automotive globalization.

The piece frames the shift in Chinese automotive exports from hardware competitiveness (cost, electrification) to software intelligence as the defining factor. Smart cockpits and ADAS are identified as the key differentiators Chinese brands need to compete in premium overseas segments. This creates structural demand for map providers like TomTom, who possess the independently controlled global data bases, regulatory certifications, and local-market adaptation capabilities that Chinese OEMs lack outside their home market.

TomTom's flexibility is emphasized: the company can act as a pure data supplier, a standalone service provider, or a full tier-1 solution integrator, adapting to different automakers' strategies and internal capabilities. Specific operational evidence includes a six-month mass production delivery milestone and the Madrid pilot for traffic light countdown recognition — a Huawei Petal Maps co-development set for European deployment in late 2026. While the article adds limited new information beyond the original 36Kr report, its publication on 汽车之家, China's largest automotive portal, signals the domestic auto industry's intense interest in the mapping infrastructure underpinning overseas expansion strategies.
