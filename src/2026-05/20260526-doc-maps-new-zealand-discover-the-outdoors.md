## Elevator Pitch

The New Zealand Department of Conservation's **"Discover the Outdoors" (DTO)** is an interactive web mapping platform for exploring **conservation land and recreational activities** across Aotearoa. It shows walking/tramping tracks, campsites, huts, hunting areas, and other recreational infrastructure, with elevation profiles covering ~60% of tracks. The platform is backed by a rich **open spatial data portal** (ArcGIS Open Data) offering downloadable layers in multiple formats (SHP, GDB, KML, GPX, GeoJSON, ESRI REST APIs). A major platform upgrade is underway in 2026, phasing out the old viewer. DOC's award-winning Geospatial Services Team supports the entire geospatial ecosystem.

## Relevance to Mapping Forward

DOC Maps is a premier example of **conservation geospatial infrastructure**. It operates at the intersection of public access, land management, and open data. The platform's elevation profiles are particularly notable — they transform raw track geometry into decision-support tools for hikers ("is this track right for my fitness level?"). The **open spatial data portal** makes authoritative conservation data available for third-party app developers, researchers, and community groups. The platform also demonstrates the lifecycle of government GIS — the transition from legacy viewers to modern platforms, with careful change management for users.

## Key Details

- **Primary tool:** Discover the Outdoors (DTO) — [doc.govt.nz/map/index.html](https://www.doc.govt.nz/map/index.html)
- **Old viewer being discontinued:** May 2026 — users directed to DTO
- **Open data portal:** [doc-deptconservation.opendata.arcgis.com](https://doc-deptconservation.opendata.arcgis.com/)
- **Data formats:** SHP, GDB, KML, GPX, GeoJSON, ESRI REST APIs
- **APIs for developers:** Tracks, huts, campsites APIs at [api.doc.govt.nz](https://api.doc.govt.nz/)
- **Elevation profiles:** Available on ~60% of tracks, with ongoing improvements
- **Additional apps:** Pesticide summaries, PF2050 predator control, Trapping app, Whio (blue duck) monitoring
- **Geospatial team:** Award-winning team providing field support, analysis, training
- **Projection:** NZTM (New Zealand Transverse Mercator 2000)
