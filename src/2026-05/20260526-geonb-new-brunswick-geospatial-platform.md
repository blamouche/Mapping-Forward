## Elevator Pitch

**GeoNB** is New Brunswick's provincial **Spatial Data Infrastructure (SDI)** — a "one-stop shop" for all geographic information about the Canadian province. Built and maintained by Service New Brunswick, it provides a **free, bilingual (EN/FR)** geospatial portal that combines an interactive map viewer, 35+ downloadable datasets (topographic layers, orthophotos, property maps), value-added applications (flood mapping, wetland conservation, coordinate transformation), and a GIS Developer's Corner with live ArcGIS map services. The platform won an **Esri Canada Award of Excellence** and serves as a model of intelligent government GIS service delivery, attracting 13,000+ weekly visitors.

## Relevance to Mapping Forward

GeoNB is a textbook example of **government-led SDI done right**. It successfully bridges the gap between professional GIS users (who access raw data and ArcGIS services) and the general public (who use the map viewer without needing GIS software). Its bilingual design is essential for New Brunswick's officially bilingual context. The platform demonstrates how a provincial government can become a **geospatial data utility** — reducing duplication across departments, enabling private-sector innovation, and empowering citizens with geographic information about their properties, flood risks, and natural resources.
