# India's Airspace Map for Drone Operations — DigitalSky Platform

- **Date:** 2021-09-24 (original press release); platform remains active as of 2026
- **Source:** [digitalsky.aai.aero/airspace-map](https://digitalsky.aai.aero/airspace-map)
- **Author:** Ministry of Civil Aviation, Government of India (via DGCA — Directorate General of Civil Aviation)
- **Keywords:** digital sky, drone, airspace map, India, DGCA, green zone, yellow zone, red zone, UAS, unmanned aircraft, geofencing, aviation, Atma Nirbhar Bharat

## Elevator Pitch

The Indian government's **DigitalSky platform** hosts a publicly accessible, interactive **airspace map for drone operations** across the entire country. Launched under the liberalised Drone Rules 2021, the map divides Indian airspace into **green, yellow, and red zones**, drastically simplifying where drones can fly. Green zones (up to 400 ft, not near airports) require no permission for drones up to 500 kg. Yellow zones (near airports, 5–12 km) need ATC clearance. Red zones are no-fly areas requiring central government permission. The yellow zone buffer was slashed from 45 km to 12 km from airport perimeters. This is a landmark geospatial policy tool enabling India's drone economy, projected to create 500,000+ jobs and ₹30,000+ crore in services over three years.

## Relevance to Mapping Forward

This is a prime example of **geospatial data infrastructure as public policy tool**. The airspace map isn't just a map — it's a regulatory interface that translates complex airspace rules into an accessible visual layer. It connects geospatial technology (GIS, zone mapping), government regulation (DGCA/AAI), and economic development (drone manufacturing, services). It's also a model of **open government geospatial data** — freely available without login, with APIs and zone data layers that developers and drone operators can integrate into flight planning apps.

## Key Details

- **Platform:** DigitalSky (DGCA), accessible at [digitalsky.dgca.gov.in](https://digitalsky.dgca.gov.in)
- **Three zones:** Green (no permission needed), Yellow (ATC permission required), Red (Central Govt permission)
- **Yellow zone reduction:** From 45 km to 12 km from airport perimeter
- **Policy context:** Drone Rules 2021, PLI scheme for drones, Geospatial Data Guidelines 2021
- **Economic projections:** ₹5,000+ crore investment, 10,000+ direct jobs, ₹30,000+ crore services industry

## Connections
