## Elevator Pitch

Mergin Maps is an open-source field mapping platform that integrates with QGIS for mobile geospatial data collection. Available on iOS and Android, it supports offline data capture, real-time team synchronization, and automatic conflict-free merge of edits from multiple surveyors. Trusted by 100,000+ users across 190 countries, it serves industries from agriculture and ecology to telecom, construction, and water utilities.

## Key Points

- **Core workflow:** Create projects in QGIS → capture data in field via mobile app → sync back to QGIS
- **Capture capabilities:** Points, lines, areas, photos, barcode/QR scanning
- **Sync model:** Cloud-based, one-click sync, no cables, automatic merge of concurrent edits
- **Offline-first:** Works without connectivity, syncs when back online
- **Open formats:** Compatible with existing QGIS projects and GIS infrastructure
- **Pricing tiers (yearly):** Individual €11.90/mo, Professional €65/mo, Team €149/mo
- **Industries served:** Agriculture, telecom, construction, environmental protection, water utilities, forestry, government, transportation, natural resources
- **External GNSS support** for high-accuracy positioning

- **Source code:** Open source on GitHub
