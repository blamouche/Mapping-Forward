# TomTom Partners with Transit Technologies to Enhance US Fleet Operations

**Date**: 2026-07-07

TomTom and Transit Technologies announce a strategic partnership to integrate TomTom's Orbis Maps and Live Traffic into Transit Technologies' vehicle management platform, improving real-time mapping and traffic data for fleet operations across the United States and Central America.

## Synthesis
TomTom has announced a strategic partnership with Transit Technologies, a US-based vehicle management platform provider, to integrate TomTom's Orbis Maps and Live Traffic services into Transit Technologies' fleet management solutions. The collaboration aims to improve the efficiency of fleet operations across the United States and Central America by combining TomTom's real-time traffic and mapping capabilities with Transit Technologies' fleet management software.

The partnership leverages TomTom's Orbis Maps platform, which combines open data from the Overture Maps Foundation with TomTom's proprietary content including address points, speed limits, and traffic restrictions. The Live Traffic service processes more than 5 billion kilometers of driving data daily, refreshed every 30 seconds across 80+ countries, providing real-time flow and incident information critical for fleet routing and optimization.

For Transit Technologies, the integration enhances its vehicle management platform with high-quality, continuously updated mapping and traffic data. Fleet operators benefit from improved route planning, real-time traffic awareness, and more accurate ETA calculations. The partnership reflects TomTom's broader strategy of expanding Orbis Maps adoption in the enterprise and B2B fleet management segments, where reliable location data directly impacts operational efficiency and cost management.

This collaboration is part of a series of TomTom partnerships aimed at embedding Orbis Maps into vertical industry platforms, following the platform's general availability launch with six new APIs and an MCP Server for AI agent integration.
