# This little-known trick finally unlocks speed camera alerts on your phone

## Elevator pitch
TomTom AmiGO can overlay speed camera alerts on top of Google Maps on Android, using the operating system's overlay functionality to provide audio and visual warnings without replacing the primary navigation app.

## Takeaways
- TomTom AmiGO can display a floating overlay on top of Google Maps, providing speed camera alerts, current speed, and speed limit information
- The overlay uses Android's system-level overlay functionality, meaning this feature is not available on iPhones in the same way
- Voice alerts reduce the need to look at the screen while driving, enhancing safety
- French regulations require apps to warn about "danger zones" rather than precise enforcement locations; laws vary by country
- Battery optimization may need to be disabled for the app to run reliably in the background

## Synthesis
A practical tip from Futura-Sciences highlights how Android users can augment Google Maps with speed camera alerts using TomTom's free AmiGO app. Rather than replacing Google Maps entirely, AmiGO leverages Android's overlay functionality to display a floating widget on top of the active navigation app, showing current speed, speed limit, and hazard alerts.

The setup is straightforward: users download TomTom AmiGO from the Google Play Store, grant permissions including display-over-other-apps access, enable voice guidance, and then launch Google Maps as usual. The overlay coexists with Google Maps' own interface, adding a layer of community-reported speed enforcement and hazard data that Google Maps' native coverage may not comprehensively provide.

The article notes important legal considerations: French regulations require navigation apps to warn about "danger zones" rather than displaying precise enforcement device locations. Users driving abroad should verify local laws, as restrictions on speed camera warning apps vary significantly by country.

iPhone users can use TomTom AmiGO as a standalone navigation app, including through Apple CarPlay, but cannot replicate the overlay experience due to iOS's more restrictive window management. Android users may also need to disable battery optimization for the app to ensure it continues running in the background during long drives, a common issue with manufacturer-specific power management features.
