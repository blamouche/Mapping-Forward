## Elevator pitch
The Vietnam Women's Museum hosts "Giao thời" (Transition), a multisensory exhibition using 3D mapping technology and interactive sensors to immerse visitors in Vietnamese cultural heritage across three experiential spaces — nature, culture, and patrimony.

## Takeaways
- The exhibition "Giao thời" (Transition) at the Vietnam Women's Museum uses 3D mapping technology combined with interactive sensor systems to create an immersive cultural experience
- Visitors journey through three sequential spaces: "Khởi lưu" (Origin/Source), "Cội thiêng" (Sacred Roots/Cultural Current), and "Sắc thời" (Eclats of Time/Heritage Current)
- 3D mapping and digital infographics transform familiar Vietnamese folk art images into living, contemporary forms, bridging traditional values and modern creativity
- The combination of light, sound, and projection technology creates a "visual immersion" into the cultural flow from past to present
- The exhibition is organised by the Vietnam Women's Museum in collaboration with VietArt, LumiPark, and LumiTech, running until July 31, 2026

## Synthesis
On July 13, 2026, VietnamPlus reported on a groundbreaking cultural exhibition at the Vietnam Women's Museum in Hanoi. The exhibition, titled "Giao thời" (Transition), represents a significant application of 3D mapping technology — more commonly associated with entertainment and commercial events — in the cultural heritage and museum sector.

The exhibition guides visitors through three successive experiential spaces, each corresponding to a fundamental source of Vietnamese cultural identity. The first space, "Khởi lưu - Mạch nguyên sinh" (Origin - First Source), opens a universe of life's origins where lighting effects and digital images allow visitors to feel the vitality of nature and the close relationship between humans and their environment. The second space, "Cội thiêng - Mạch văn hóa" (Sacred Roots - Cultural Current), delves into the depths of national history and the different strata of Vietnamese culture. The third, "Sắc thời - Mạch di sản" (Eclats of Time - Heritage Current), is dedicated to heritage values transmitted across generations.

The technological approach uses digital infographic language and 3D mapping to present familiar images of Vietnamese folk art in living, contemporary forms. This creative use of projection technology fosters a connection between traditional values and contemporary creativity, making cultural heritage more accessible — particularly to younger audiences. The combination of light, sound, and projection technologies offers visitors a "visual immersion" into the cultural current flowing from past to present.

The exhibition demonstrates that when used creatively and responsibly, technology becomes a means for the public to access heritage in more original and attractive forms. Rather than limiting heritage to the conservation of objects or transmission of past narratives, 3D mapping technology contributes to maintaining these values in contemporary life and inspiring future generations. The exhibition runs until July 31, 2026, organised in collaboration with VietArt, LumiPark, and LumiTech.
