## Synthesis
Esri has announced the launch of the Power of Where Collection, a new series of books that builds on the ideas introduced in Esri president Jack Dangermond's 2024 book "The Power of Where: A Geographic Approach to the World's Greatest Challenges." The collection will debut at the 2026 Esri User Conference in July, expanding the foundational concepts of geographic information systems (GIS) into focused, real-world applications that demonstrate the growing impact of spatial technology across industries.

The first two titles in the collection are "Geowater: A Geographic Approach to Water Data and Forecasting" and "Parcels: How Property Shapes Geography." Together, these books illustrate how spatial thinking and GIS are transforming how organizations understand systems, manage resources, and plan for the future. "Geowater" addresses water sustainability and climate resilience through the lens of geographic data, while "Parcels" explores how landownership and property boundaries shape governance, economic development, and community planning.

Dangermond describes the collection as demonstrating how GIS "connects data, people, and place to create a more sustainable and resilient future." The books will be featured at the Esri User Conference through author presentations, book signings, and at the Esri Press stand. The collection reflects Esri's broader strategy of positioning GIS not merely as a technical tool but as a framework for understanding and addressing the world's most pressing challenges — from water scarcity and climate adaptation to land governance and economic development. Future titles will expand the collection to bring the geographic approach to additional industries and disciplines.
