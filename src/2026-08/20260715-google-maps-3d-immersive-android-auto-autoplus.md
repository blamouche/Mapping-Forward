# L'une des fonctions les plus attendues de Google Maps arrive enfin sur Android Auto
**Source**: https://www.autoplus.fr/actualite/lune-des-fonctions-les-plus-attendues-de-google-maps-arrive-enfin-sur-android-auto-1455924.html
**Date**: 2026-07-15
**Author**: Matteo Mercier, Auto Plus
**Keywords**: Google Maps, Android Auto, 3D navigation, immersive navigation, Gemini AI, buildings, relief, CarPlay, deployment

## Elevator pitch
Google Maps has launched its immersive 3D navigation on Android Auto, using Gemini AI to render buildings, road markings, and signage in realistic detail — but the feature is currently limited to the United States with no timeline for Europe.

## Takeaways
- Google Maps abandons the flat top-down view for a 3D model with buildings, vegetation, and precise road markings
- Gemini AI processed millions of Street View and aerial images to reconstruct real-world geometry
- The system makes blocking buildings semi-transparent in narrow streets to maintain route visibility
- Vocal guidance has been upgraded to use natural, environment-based instructions ("turn after the restaurant with the red sign")
- Deployment is server-side and currently US-only; Europe has no announced timeline

## Synthesis
Google Maps has begun rolling out its most significant Android Auto visual overhaul: a fully immersive 3D navigation experience that replaces the flat, schematic top-down view with a realistic three-dimensional rendering of the driving environment. The update, which Google describes as the biggest navigation evolution in a decade, uses the company's Gemini AI to process millions of Street View and aerial images, automatically reconstructing the geometry of the real world.

The result is a navigation interface where buildings rise alongside the route, vegetation appears on roadsides, and road markings gain exceptional precision. At complex interchanges, the system displays stop signs, traffic lights, and exact lane widths so drivers know precisely where to position their vehicle. A particularly clever feature addresses the problem of tall buildings blocking the view in narrow streets: the interface makes obstructing structures semi-transparent in real-time, maintaining clear visibility of the route ahead.

The audio experience has been similarly upgraded. The robotic voice that previously announced turns "in 400 meters" has been replaced with natural-language instructions that reference visual landmarks — for example, "turn after the restaurant with the large red sign" or "bear right before the stone viaduct." The map scale also adapts dynamically, zooming out on highways to anticipate distant traffic conditions and zooming in at roundabouts to ensure no exit is missed. Arrival at the destination now includes a Street View preview of the exact building entrance.

The deployment is entirely server-side, meaning users cannot trigger it by updating the app or changing settings. The feature launched exclusively in the United States with the Android Auto 17.3 update deployed the weekend of July 12, following its earlier debut on Apple CarPlay. Google has not communicated any timeline for the feature's arrival in Europe, citing the need for sufficient Street View and aerial imagery coverage city by city. The gradual rollout is consistent with Google's typical strategy of launching in the US first, then expanding region by region.
