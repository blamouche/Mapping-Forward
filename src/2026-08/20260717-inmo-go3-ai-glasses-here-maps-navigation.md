# The INMO GO3 are lightweight AI glasses with features built for everyday use
**Source**: https://www.androidauthority.com/inmo-go3-ai-glasses-3687255/
**Date**: 2026-07-17
**Author**: Ankit Banerjee, Android Authority
**Keywords**: INMO GO3, AI glasses, HERE Maps, AR navigation, smart glasses, wearable navigation, heads-up display, translation

## Elevator pitch
The INMO GO3 smart glasses integrate HERE Maps for hands-free walking and cycling navigation displayed directly in the user's line of sight, offering a safer alternative to phone-based navigation alongside AI translation and teleprompter features.

## Takeaways
- INMO GO3 smart glasses feature built-in HERE Maps for walking and cycling directions in a heads-up display
- Navigation directions appear directly in the user's line of sight, eliminating the need to look down at a phone
- The glasses weigh approximately 58 grams, similar to normal eyeglasses, with an anti-leakage display design for privacy
- Supports 98 languages for real-time translation and 77 for conversational translation
- HERE Maps integration provides turn-by-turn navigation without pulling out a phone at every corner

## Synthesis
The INMO GO3 represents a notable integration of professional mapping services into the emerging category of AI-powered smart glasses. At approximately 58 grams, the GO3 is designed to look and feel like normal eyeglasses while packing AR display technology, AI translation, and — importantly for the mapping ecosystem — built-in HERE Maps navigation.

The HERE Maps integration is particularly significant as it brings a major commercial mapping platform into the wearable space. Walking and cycling directions appear directly in the user's line of sight, enabling navigation without the safety risks of staring at a phone screen while moving. The anti-leakage display design ensures that navigation information is invisible to people around the user, addressing privacy concerns that have plagued smart glasses since the category's inception.

The GO3's navigation capability is part of a broader feature set that includes real-time translation in 98 languages, a teleprompter mode for presentations, and an AI voice assistant with internet search and context memory. The glasses support custom prescription lenses, making them practical for everyday use by people who already wear glasses.

INMO's choice of HERE Maps over Google Maps or Apple Maps is notable — HERE's strong pedestrian and cycling routing, combined with its licensing model for embedded integration, makes it a natural fit for wearable platforms where Google's and Apple's embedded licensing terms may be less favorable. This integration demonstrates how the mapping platform market is expanding beyond smartphones and automotive into the wearable computing space.
