# XPeng, 1er constructeur chinois à intégrer Google Maps Auto SDK pour une raison bien précise
**Source**: https://www.mac4ever.com/auto/197183-xpeng-1er-constructeur-chinois-a-integrer-google-maps-auto-sdk-pour-une-raison-bien-precise
**Date**: 2026-07-18
**Author**: Mac4Ever
**Keywords**: XPeng, Google Maps Auto SDK, automotive navigation, Chinese automaker, NGP, autonomous driving, CarPlay, Android Automotive

## Elevator pitch
XPeng becomes the first Chinese automaker to integrate Google Maps Auto SDK in its L03 model, using Google's traffic data, POIs, and route planning while retaining its own navigation interface, as a strategic step toward launching its NGP autonomous driving system in international markets.

## Takeaways
- XPeng is the first Chinese automaker to integrate Google Maps Auto SDK, debuting in the L03 model
- Google Maps Auto SDK lets automakers keep their own navigation UI while using Google's traffic data, POIs, and route planner
- Rivian was actually the first automaker globally to use the SDK (July 2025); XPeng is the first in Asia-Pacific
- The integration is strategic: XPeng needs reliable map data covering dozens of countries before launching its NGP (VLA 2.0) driver-assistance system in Europe
- Other XPeng models (G6, G9) could potentially receive the SDK via OTA update if hardware permits

## Synthesis
XPeng's integration of Google Maps Auto SDK in its new L03 model marks a significant milestone in the convergence of automotive navigation and big-tech mapping platforms. Until now, getting Google Maps in a car required either smartphone mirroring (CarPlay or Android Auto) or a vehicle running Android Automotive with Google services. The Auto SDK offers a middle path: automakers retain full control over their navigation interface, rendering, and user interactions, while Google provides the underlying traffic data, points of interest, and route planning engine.

Jorgen Behrens, VP and GM of Google Maps Automotive, noted that over 2 billion users rely on Google Maps monthly, framing the SDK expansion as bringing Google's technology to more drivers in Europe and beyond. However, the real motivation is strategic: XPeng cannot launch its NGP (Next Generation Pilot, VLA 2.0) autonomous driving system in foreign markets without a reliable cartographic layer covering dozens of countries. Rather than building maps country by country as Tesla has done, XPeng chose to outsource this component to Google.

The L03 is the only model currently equipped with the SDK, though XPeng's other models (G6, G9) could potentially receive it via OTA update. This integration highlights how mapping infrastructure is becoming a critical enabler for the automotive industry's shift toward software-defined vehicles and autonomous driving, with Google positioning itself as a key provider of the spatial data layer that underpins these systems.
