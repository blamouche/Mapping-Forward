# BMC Approves AI, Drone, Satellite Tech To Detect Illegal Constructions And Encroachments Across Mumbai
**Source**: https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/bmc-approves-ai-drone-satellite-tech-to-detect-illegal-constructions-and-encroachments-across-mumbai
**Date**: 2026-07-21
**Author**: Free Press Journal
**Keywords**: BMC, AI, drone survey, satellite imagery, GIS, illegal construction, encroachment detection, geo-tagged complaints, Mumbai, remote sensing

## Elevator pitch
Mumbai's Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation has approved a technology-driven plan combining AI detection, drone surveys, satellite imagery, and GIS monitoring to identify illegal constructions and encroachments early, supplemented by a GPS-enabled citizen complaint app.

## Takeaways
- The BMC General Body approved a proposal under Rule 59 to combat illegal constructions using advanced technology
- The plan includes AI-based detection systems, drone surveys, satellite imagery, GIS monitoring, and remote sensing
- A GPS-enabled mobile app and online portal will allow citizens to submit geo-tagged complaints with precise locations
- The Municipal Administration is directed to develop a time-bound action plan with an implementation report to the House
- BJP corporator Tajinder Singh Tiwana highlighted that illegal constructions adversely affect urban planning, public safety, traffic management, and environmental protection

## Synthesis
The General Body of the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC) has approved a comprehensive technology-driven proposal to curb illegal constructions and encroachments across Mumbai. The plan, moved under Rule 59, aims to supplement conventional inspection methods with a multi-layered technological approach combining Artificial Intelligence, drone surveys, satellite imagery, Geographic Information Systems (GIS), remote sensing, and real-time monitoring systems to detect unauthorised structures at an early stage.

A key component of the initiative is the launch of a GPS-enabled mobile application and an online citizen complaint portal. These tools will enable residents to submit geo-tagged complaints, ensuring that the exact location of reported violations is immediately available to concerned authorities for prompt enforcement action. The General Body has directed the Municipal Administration to develop an AI-based Illegal Construction Detection System, conduct regular drone and satellite monitoring, introduce a digital public complaint mechanism, and prepare a time-bound action plan with an implementation report submitted to the House.

The decision reflects growing recognition that traditional inspection methods alone are insufficient to address the rapid rise in illegal constructions in one of the world's most densely populated cities. By leveraging geospatial technology, remote sensing, and citizen participation through geo-tagged reporting, Mumbai is establishing what could become a model for other Indian megacities facing similar urban governance challenges. The integration of AI-based detection with drone and satellite monitoring represents a significant shift from reactive to proactive enforcement in urban planning.
