## Synthesis
On July 28, 2026, Geo Week News published an industry press release announcing that Maptitude, developed by Caliper Corporation, had been named one of the six best GIS software tools for mapping in 2026 by G2, the world's largest software marketplace. The recognition came from G2's buyer's guide titled "I Compared the 6 Best GIS Software Tools for Mapping (2026)," which evaluated platforms across spatial analysis, mapping capabilities, geospatial data management, ease of implementation, scalability, integrations, and overall user experience.

Maptitude stood out in several key metrics. It earned the highest geocoding score in the lineup at 98%, indicating exceptional reliability in turning address lists and location-based datasets into accurately plotted maps. It also scored 97% on data capture. Its 94% Likelihood to Recommend score was the highest among the six compared platforms, reflecting strong user satisfaction and advocacy.

The guide identified territory mapping and management as the capability driving the majority of positive Maptitude reviews. Users described how they build, adjust, and balance sales territories, service zones, and operational regions using purpose-built tools. The guide also highlighted Maptitude's bundled demographic and geographic data—a significant differentiator that eliminates the need for costly third-party data purchases—and its site selection and market analysis tools used across retail, real estate, and franchise development.

A key theme in the recognition is accessibility. Stewart Berry, Vice President at Maptitude, emphasized that the platform was "built to give business teams the full power of a professional GIS platform without the complexity or the cost that usually comes with it." The guide noted that Maptitude makes sophisticated spatial analysis accessible to business analysts who do not have deep GIS expertise—a critical advantage as location intelligence becomes increasingly central to business decision-making across industries.

Maptitude is available in both desktop and cloud-based editions (Maptitude Online), giving organizations flexibility in how they build and share maps. The platform is used by Fortune 500 companies, government agencies, and nonprofits worldwide, and supports educational initiatives with free licenses for students and educators.
