# Ce réglage très simple sur Google Maps va sauver votre forfait mobile cet été
**Source**: https://www.journaldesfemmes.fr/maman/techno/3273669-ce-reglage-tres-simple-sur-google-maps-va-sauver-votre-forfait-mobile-cet-ete/

**Author**: Journal des Femmes
**Keywords**: Google Maps, offline maps, data consumption, mobile data saving, Waze, Apple Plans, navigation data usage, vacation navigation

## Elevator pitch
Downloading offline maps in Google Maps before leaving home can dramatically reduce mobile data consumption during vacation navigation, as standard online navigation consumes 2-15 MB/hour and satellite view can exceed 100 MB/hour.

## Takeaways
- Google Maps' standard navigation consumes approximately 2 MB/hour, but can reach 15 MB with frequent recalculations, and satellite view exceeds 100 MB/hour
- Offline maps are activated via profile photo > "Plans hors connexion" > "Sélectionner votre propre plan" — frame the area and download over Wi-Fi

## Synthesis
Published on August 3, 2026, in Journal des Femmes, this article explains how to save mobile data while using Google Maps during summer travel. Google Maps is the dominant navigation app in France with over 42 million monthly users, but its data consumption can become a "gouffre" (bottomless pit) on long vacation routes or when exploring unfamiliar areas.

The article details the data costs: standard navigation consumes about 2 MB per hour, rising to 15 MB with frequent route recalculations. Activating satellite view explodes consumption to over 100 MB per hour. For travelers on limited data plans, this can lead to bill overage charges. The solution is Google Maps' offline maps feature, which allows users to download a geographic area over Wi-Fi before departure and navigate without consuming mobile data.

The setup is straightforward: tap the profile photo in the top right, select "Plans hors connexion" (Offline maps), then "Sélectionner votre propre plan" (Select your own map). Frame the desired geographic zone and download. Users should verify that "Wi-Fi only" downloads are enabled in settings to prevent the download itself from consuming mobile data, and ensure sufficient storage space.

The article compares data consumption across apps: Waze is less data-intensive than Google Maps but limited to road traffic — it can't help find bakeries or shops. Apple Plans, the default iPhone app, is the most data-hungry, consuming more than twice Google Maps' data, but offers the same offline feature. Offline mode has limitations: no real-time traffic for avoiding jams, and no pedestrian, cycling, or transit routing. The article recommends periodically reactivating data for traffic updates, then returning to offline mode for the rest of the journey.
