# Waze et Google Maps : combien de gigas votre GPS consomme-t-il sur un long trajet ?
**Source**: https://www.jechange.fr/telecom/mobile/news/waze-google-maps-consommation-data-gps-long-trajet
**Date**: 2026-08-03
**Author**: Florian Tostivint, JeChange
**Keywords**: Google Maps, Waze, data consumption, GPS navigation, mobile data, offline maps, streaming comparison, data budget

## Elevator pitch
GPS navigation consumes surprisingly little data — Google Maps uses 3-5 MB/hour and Waze 20-30 MB/hour — meaning an 8-hour trip costs at most 300 MB, far less than a single minute of HD video streaming.

## Takeaways
- Google Maps consumes 3-5 MB/hour; Waze 20-30 MB/hour (up to 40 MB in dense urban areas) due to real-time traffic and incident refreshing
- An 8-hour drive costs at most 300 MB with Waze, often less than 40 MB with Google Maps — negligible against typical multi-GB data plans
- The real data drain is background usage: Spotify at 45 MB/hour, Netflix at ~1 GB/hour, YouTube HD at 1.5-2 GB/hour
- One minute of HD YouTube video (~30 MB) equals nearly an hour of Waze navigation
- Downloading offline maps reduces GPS data to zero and works even without signal in mountains or gorges

## Synthesis
Published on August 3, 2026, in JeChange, this article by Florian Tostivint demystifies GPS data consumption with concrete numbers. The common assumption that hours of GPS navigation devours mobile data is unfounded — navigation primarily transmits position data and receives route traces plus traffic information, which is very lightweight.

The article provides precise figures: Google Maps uses approximately 3-5 MB per hour of driving, while Waze consumes 20-30 MB per hour (up to 40 MB in dense urban areas) because it continuously refreshes traffic conditions and user-reported incidents. For context, an 8-hour long drive represents at most 300 MB with Waze and often less than 40 MB with Google Maps — a drop in the bucket compared to typical mobile data plans of several tens of gigabytes.

The article then shifts focus to what actually consumes data during a trip. A comparison table shows that while Google Maps uses 3-5 MB/hour and Waze 20-40 MB/hour, music streaming (Spotify) consumes about 45 MB/hour, standard-definition video streaming (Netflix) about 1 GB/hour, and HD YouTube up to 1.5-2 GB/hour. The striking comparison: a single minute of HD YouTube video (approximately 30 MB) equals nearly an hour of Waze navigation. The real data drain is passenger entertainment and background app updates, not the map.

To reduce data to near zero, the article recommends downloading offline itineraries before departure (GPS then works without consuming a byte), saving playlists offline, disabling automatic updates on mobile data, and downloading films at home over Wi-Fi. Offline maps also provide continued navigation in signal-dead zones like mountains and gorges.
