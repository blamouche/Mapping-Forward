## Synthesis
Published on August 5, 2026, in Supply Chain Digital, this article by Louise Collins presents a top 10 ranking of fleet telematics providers — companies that use GPS location, engine diagnostics, and driver behavior analysis to track, monitor, and manage commercial vehicle fleets. The ranking highlights how telematics has evolved from simple vehicle tracking into a sophisticated ecosystem combining AI, IoT, and real-time analytics.

At the top of the ranking is Geotab, an Ontario-based company founded in 2000. With over 25 years of fleet experience, Geotab processes more than 100 billion data points daily to generate informed predictions and insights. Over 54,999 companies use the platform, which offers custom reports, automated compliance, and critical asset management on a single platform.

Samsara (San Francisco, #2) distinguishes itself with comprehensive visibility — monitoring not just vehicles but powered assets, unpowered assets, temperature, road operations, and driver actions. Its data-driven insights help improve fuel usage, fleet electrification, route performance, and vehicle diagnostics through personalized, app-based workflows.

Verizon Connect (#3) focuses on coordinated daily operations with near-time vehicle visibility, helping spot and reduce fuel waste and idling. Its GPS fleet tracking includes vehicle history data points for route replay and proof-of-operations verification.

The ranking also highlights specialized capabilities: Lytx's AI-powered Fatigue Detection Technology using machine vision and human intelligence; Motive's detection of 20+ unsafe driver behaviors at up to 99% accuracy; PowerFleet's Unity platform combining AI and IoT across fleets and warehouses; and Trimble's TMT Fleet Maintenance digitizing the entire vehicle lifecycle.

TomTom's Webfleet (#10) offers vehicle tracking across multiple vehicle types with real-time tracking, driving style monitoring, and fuel/battery usage insights, maintaining TomTom's presence in the fleet management space alongside its navigation products.
