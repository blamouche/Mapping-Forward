# Ask Maps Gets More Helpful in India: Hindi Support, Personal Intelligence, and Live Transit
**Source**: https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/ask-maps-gets-more-helpful-in-india/
**Date**: 2026-08-06
**Author**: Google India Team
**Keywords**: Google Maps, Ask Maps, Gemini, Hindi, Personal Intelligence, Gmail integration, real-time transit, India

## Elevator pitch
Google is expanding Ask Maps in India with Hindi language support, Personal Intelligence that securely connects to Gmail for personalized travel suggestions, real-time transit information, and the ability to resume past conversations.

## Takeaways
- Ask Maps is now available in Hindi, allowing users to discover places, plan routes, and get local insights in India's most widely spoken language
- Personal Intelligence lets users optionally connect Ask Maps to Gmail (off by default) so Maps can factor in flight or dinner reservations to provide relevant suggestions
- Ask Maps remembers past conversations when history settings are enabled, letting users resume trip planning where they left off
- A new live transit widget in Ask Maps provides real-time schedules and delays for buses, metros, and trains
- Ask Maps combines Gemini AI capabilities with Google's map data to answer natural-language questions about places, traffic, weather, and busyness levels

## Synthesis
On August 6, 2026, Google announced significant updates to Ask Maps in India, building on what it describes as "Google Maps' biggest transformation in over a decade." Ask Maps combines Gemini model capabilities with Google's map database to let users ask natural-language questions about real-world places and get conversational, contextual answers. Since launching in India and the U.S., Google reports that users are finding entirely new ways to use Maps—from finding quiet restaurants for conversation to organizing tailored itineraries.

The first major update is Hindi language support. Starting from the announcement date, Ask Maps can understand and respond naturally in Hindi, making it easier for Hindi-speaking users to discover places, plan routes, and get local insights. This is a significant localization move for the Indian market where Hindi is spoken by hundreds of millions.

The second update, Personal Intelligence, is particularly notable. Users can optionally connect Ask Maps to Gmail (off by default, with the user in control) so that Maps can automatically factor in upcoming travel plans. For example, a traveler can ask for ideas on how to spend a few hours near their hotel before a flight, and Maps will use the hotel reservation from Gmail to provide nearby recommendations along with tips for getting to the airport on time. This integration with Calendar is also planned.

Ask Maps also now remembers past conversations when history settings are enabled. Users can ask Maps to remind them of previously suggested activities for a trip, pulling up prior ideas without starting from scratch. Additionally, a new live transit widget provides up-to-the-minute schedules and delays for buses, metros, and trains, alongside existing real-time information about traffic conditions, weather, and place busyness.
