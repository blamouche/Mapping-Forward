## Synthesis
On August 6-7, 2026, The Arunachal Times and multiple Indian news outlets reported that Arunachal Pradesh Governor Lt Gen (Retd) KT Parnaik declared that Geographic Information System (GIS) technology must become a cornerstone of governance in the state. The governor made these remarks while addressing the 'Geo Vision Itanagar' seminar organized by Esri India, a leading geospatial technology company.

Governor Parnaik emphasized that Arunachal Pradesh's unique geographical profile—characterized by diverse terrain and sensitive border areas—requires technology-driven governance approaches. He urged all government departments to adopt GIS-based systems for data-driven decision-making, arguing that the state's geographical challenges demand modern geospatial tools rather than traditional administrative methods.

The governor specifically advocated for drone-based GIS mapping to address the state's terrain challenges and support border development initiatives. He also called for a unified geospatial platform that would integrate data across departments, eliminating the siloed decision-making that currently limits effective governance. The seminar, organized by Esri India, brought together government officials and geospatial technology experts to discuss how GIS can transform governance in the northeastern state.

The announcement reflects a broader trend of Indian state governments embracing GIS technology for governance, from urban planning and land management to disaster response and border security. The involvement of Esri India as the seminar organizer highlights the company's strategic role in promoting GIS adoption among Indian state governments and institutions. Multiple news outlets covered the event, including India Today NE, ET Government, PTI, The Sentinel, and Social News XYZ, indicating significant media attention to GIS-driven governance in India's northeast.
