# How First American Data and Analytics Is Bringing Property Intelligence Into the GIS World
**Source**: https://www.geoweeknews.com/articles/how-first-american-data-and-analytics-is-bringing-property-intelligence-into-the-gis-world/
**Date**: 2026-08-12
**Author**: Geo Week News
**Keywords**: GIS, ArcGIS, property data, Esri, parcel mapping, real estate, property intelligence, data integration

## Elevator pitch
First American Data and Analytics now delivers its comprehensive property data as native ArcGIS feature services, enabling spatial professionals to query parcel-level property intelligence—ownership, valuation, transactions—directly within their GIS workflows.

## Takeaways
- First American now delivers property data via feature service directly inside ArcGIS, after five years of Esri partnership with only bulk/API delivery
- The company has standardized property data from all 3,143 US counties around a common property identifier, overcoming the fragmentation of county-level data schemas
- An AI-powered app on Esri's platform enables natural-language queries like "Find every vacant parcel in this ZIP code owned by an out-of-state resident"
- First American is developing AI to scan archived document images and identify human signatories behind LLC-owned properties (40–50 million US properties held by corporate entities)
- The company's data quality is existential to its business: its parent company uses the same data to underwrite title insurance

## Synthesis
First American Data and Analytics has reached an inflection point in its relationship with the GIS community. After five years as an Esri partner where their property data was accessible only through bulk delivery, APIs, and their SaaS platform, they now deliver data as native ArcGIS feature services—the format spatial professionals have been requesting for years. This integration eliminates the workaround culture that frustrated GIS analysts who needed property intelligence embedded in their maps rather than bolted on from external systems.

The core challenge First American solved is the fundamental fragmentation of American property data. The country's 3,143 counties never agreed on a standard way to collect or share property information, creating a patchwork of schemas, identifiers, and document formats. First American spent decades collecting this data—sometimes online, sometimes in person, sometimes via CDs mailed by county clerks—and standardizing it around a common property identifier that maintains historical continuity even when street names change (as happened in Phoenix during a street renaming project).

The use cases enabled by this GIS-native integration are extensive. Cities can map every parking lot alongside ownership, value, and zoning data to identify housing infill opportunities. Builders can track competitor land acquisitions. Investors can identify vacant parcels as alternative assets. First American is building an AI-powered natural-language query interface on Esri's platform and developing AI tools to scan archived document images to identify human signatories behind the estimated 40–50 million US properties held by corporations or LLCs. The company's unique position as both a data manufacturer and a title insurance underwriter creates a data quality incentive that marketing claims cannot replicate.
