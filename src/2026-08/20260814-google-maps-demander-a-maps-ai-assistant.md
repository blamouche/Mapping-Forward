# Google Revamps Maps with "Demander à Maps" AI Feature
**Source**: https://www.journaldemontreal.com/2026/08/14/google-revampe-maps-avec-la-fonction-demander-a-maps
**Date**: 2026-08-14
**Author**: Journal de Montréal
**Keywords**: Google Maps, Demander à Maps, Ask Maps, AI assistant, itinerary planning, real-time pricing, route recommendations

## Elevator pitch
Google has launched "Demander à Maps" (Ask Maps), an AI-powered feature in Google Maps that acts as a conversational assistant for itinerary planning, comparing prices in real time and checking availability to help users find the ideal destination.

## Takeaways
- "Demander à Maps" is an AI-powered conversational feature integrated into Google Maps
- The assistant can be queried for advice and options on itineraries and destinations
- Compares prices in real time and checks availability to help users find the ideal location
- Can suggest restaurants and hotels and even prepare actions on the user's behalf
- Represents Google's push to transform Maps from navigation tool to AI-powered travel assistant
- Available as part of Google's broader revamp of the Maps platform

## Synthesis
Google has introduced "Demander à Maps" (Ask Maps), a new AI-powered feature that transforms Google Maps from a pure navigation application into a conversational travel assistant. The feature allows users to interrogate Google Maps with natural language queries to obtain advice and choices about their itineraries, going beyond simple route planning to offer contextual recommendations.

The AI assistant can compare prices in real time and verify availability for restaurants, hotels, and other venues, enabling users to find the ideal destination without leaving the Maps interface. According to the Journal de Montréal report, the feature can chain together suggestions for restaurants and hotels, and even prepare actions on the user's behalf, effectively turning Google Maps into a proactive planning tool rather than a passive mapping utility.

This revamp is part of Google's broader strategy to integrate generative AI across its products. By embedding conversational AI directly into Maps, Google aims to differentiate its mapping platform from competitors by offering personalized, context-aware recommendations that leverage its vast database of business listings, real-time traffic data, and user reviews. The feature positions Google Maps as a comprehensive travel and lifestyle assistant, not just a tool for getting from point A to point B.
