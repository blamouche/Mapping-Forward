## Synthesis
The United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA) launched the Population Intelligence Hackathon (PopIntel Hack) in Nairobi, bringing together experts from public health, population modeling, artificial intelligence, and geospatial technology. The goal is to create tools that can turn scattered data into usable population intelligence for humanitarian and governmental decision-making. While collecting population data is straightforward, pulling it into a single tool that can be used when decisions need to be made quickly is far more challenging.

Esri serves as a geospatial technology partner for the event. Using ArcGIS, participants can bring population, climate, health, mobility, and risk layers into the same maps, dashboards, and applications at district and country scale. The ArcGIS ecosystem—including ArcGIS Online, Living Atlas of the World, Experience Builder, Dashboards, and Instant Apps—enables teams to build tools that show overlapping risks, surface population exposures, and answer logistical questions. The hackathon is organized in three tracks: Climate Pulse focuses on climate-demographic risk and resilience; Safe Futures covers sexual and reproductive health, maternal health, gender-based violence prevention, and harmful practices; and the Trust Layer handles country validation and ensures responsible use of AI tools. Geography runs through the heart of all three tracks, demonstrating how location intelligence can turn population data into actionable humanitarian response.
