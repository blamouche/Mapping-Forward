# Mapy.com Redraws Austria's Hiking Trails, Adding Tens of Thousands of Kilometres
**Source**: https://www.letemsvetemapplem.eu/fr/2026/08/21/mapy-com-prekreslily-turisticke-trasy-v-rakousku-pribyly-desitky-tisic-kilometru/

**Author**: Letem svetem Applem

## Elevator pitch
Czech mapping service Mapy.com has completely rebuilt Austria's hiking trail network from scratch, replacing incomplete OpenStreetMap data with its own verified database that now covers approximately 149,000 km of marked trails — up from the previous 95,000 km.

## Takeaways
- Mapy.com has redrawn Austria's entire hiking trail network, increasing coverage from ~95,000 to ~149,000 km of marked trails
- The team replaced OpenStreetMap data with its own verified database after finding OSM data incomplete and unverified
- For each trail, editors verified existence, actual routing, and type of marking before integrating into the database
- Austria was chosen as a priority destination because it is a top tourist destination for Czech travellers
- The update is part of Mapy.com's strategy to differentiate through superior tourist mapping for outdoor use

## Synthesis
Mapy.com, the Czech mapping service owned by Seznam, has undertaken a comprehensive rebuild of Austria's hiking trail network — one of the most significant data quality improvements in outdoor mapping. The previous trail data, sourced from OpenStreetMap, covered approximately 95,000 km of marked hiking trails but was found to be incomplete in many areas and contained routes whose trail marking could not be verified on the ground.

Rather than incrementally patching the OSM data, Mapy.com's editorial cartography team rebuilt the network from scratch. For each trail, they verified its existence, its actual routing, and the type of marking before integrating it into their own proprietary database. The result is approximately 149,000 km of marked trails — a 57% increase in coverage that represents not new trails but rather more complete and accurate documentation of existing ones.

The choice of Austria is strategic: it is one of the most popular tourist destinations for Czech travellers, and outdoor/tourist mapping is a key sector for Mapy.com's international expansion. The article's author argues that in mountain environments, the value of a mapping application lies in the accuracy and detail of its tourist information rather than in universal geographic coverage. A hiker who discovers on the trail that a proposed route is completely different or non-existent loses trust in the application. By investing in verified, editorial-quality trail data, Mapy.com differentiates itself from competitors that rely on crowdsourced data of varying reliability.
