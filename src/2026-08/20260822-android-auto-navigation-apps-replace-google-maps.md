# I Tested the Top 3 Android Auto Navigation Apps to Replace Google Maps
**Source**: https://www.androidpolice.com/tested-3-android-auto-navigation-apps-to-replace-google-maps-one-was-clear-winner/
**Date**: 2026-08-17
**Author**: Parth, Android Police
**Keywords**: Android Auto, Waze, OsmAnd, HERE WeGo, Google Maps, navigation, Gemini, community reporting

## Elevator pitch
After testing Waze, OsmAnd, and HERE WeGo on Android Auto, one app emerged as the clear Google Maps replacement thanks to its clean interface, community reporting, and surprisingly deep Gemini integration.

## Takeaways
- Waze, OsmAnd, and HERE WeGo were tested as Google Maps replacements on Android Auto
- OsmAnd offers extensive customization and offline maps but its UI feels like overkill for everyday use
- HERE WeGo is straightforward with strong offline support but lacked a compelling differentiator
- Waze won with its clean interface, lightweight performance, and real-time community hazard reporting
- Gemini integration gives Waze multi-app voice commands, rivaling Google Maps' assistant advantage
- Waze's community reports (traffic, crashes, hazards) proved useful even on familiar routes

## Synthesis
An Android Police writer conducted a hands-on comparison of three navigation apps as potential Google Maps replacements on Android Auto: Waze, OsmAnd, and HERE WeGo. The motivation was Google Maps' occasional lag on Android Auto, battery drain, and a busy interface that felt cluttered on an 8-inch car display.

OsmAnd impressed immediately with its level of control, offering extensive options for offline maps, route customization, map detail, driving profiles, and screen content. However, its UI felt designed for users who enjoy tweaking every setting, making it overkill for everyday commuting. HERE WeGo was much easier to settle into, with a straightforward interface, solid navigation, and strong offline map support. But nothing about it created a strong pull to switch permanently.

Waze emerged as the clear winner for several reasons. Its interface is clean with easy-to-recognize icons, and important information like turns, ETA, and road alerts is presented without crowding the screen. It feels lightweight in day-to-day use with fewer moments of hesitation. The real advantage was Waze's community reporting, which delivered useful warnings about traffic, crashes, stopped vehicles, and road hazards before the driver reached them, adding value even on familiar routes. Perhaps most surprisingly, Gemini integration works well with Waze, enabling multi-app voice commands like retrieving a location from Google Keep and starting navigation, or sending an ETA via Messages. This assistant integration, previously assumed to be a Google Maps advantage, proved to be accessible through Waze as well, making it the most compelling alternative.
