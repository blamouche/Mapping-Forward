## Synthesis
The Indian state of Chhattisgarh has signed two key Memorandums of Understanding to strengthen the implementation of PM GatiShakti, the national infrastructure master plan that uses GIS-based planning for coordinated infrastructure development. The state has made significant progress in integrating geospatial data across government departments.

Over 35 departments have been integrated into the PM GatiShakti platform, with 41 mandatory data layers and more than 600 additional data layers mapped. This comprehensive data integration enables cross-departmental coordination for infrastructure planning, reducing delays and cost overruns that typically result from siloed project execution. The state has also developed 45 use cases and 30 analytical tools under the platform, demonstrating practical applications of the geospatial infrastructure for governance.

PM GatiShakti is India's national initiative to create a unified digital platform for infrastructure planning, using GIS mapping to coordinate projects across railways, roads, ports, airports, and other sectors. By bringing all infrastructure data onto a single geospatial platform, the program aims to prevent the kind of overlapping and conflicting projects that have historically plagued Indian infrastructure development. Chhattisgarh's progress with 600+ data layers and 45 use cases positions it as one of the more advanced states in GatiShakti adoption, leveraging geospatial intelligence for evidence-based infrastructure decision-making.
