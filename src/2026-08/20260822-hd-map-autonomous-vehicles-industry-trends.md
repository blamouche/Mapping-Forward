## Synthesis
The fast development of autonomous and connected mobility has elevated the role of digital maps from simple navigation aids to critical safety infrastructure. SNS Insider's analysis of the HD Map for Autonomous Vehicles market reveals a sector experiencing explosive growth: from USD 2.59 billion in 2024 to a projected USD 22.97 billion by 2032, representing a compound annual growth rate of 31.48%. The US market alone is expected to grow from USD 0.73 billion to USD 6.38 billion during the same period.

HD mapping solutions differ fundamentally from standard navigation maps. They provide accurate data about road geometry, lane markings, traffic infrastructure, road borders, and other environmental elements that enable autonomous vehicles to operate in challenging driving conditions. This lane-level positioning and localization capability makes them indispensable for Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) and fully autonomous driving.

The competitive landscape features six major players with distinct strategies. TomTom leverages cloud solutions, AI, and sensor information, recently partnering with Mitsubishi Electric. Waymo has been mapping Tokyo streets since 2025 using manually driven vehicles to create high-quality 3D representations. HERE Technologies has deepened its automotive alliances, notably integrating HD map data into BMW vehicles. NVIDIA provides the AI and accelerated computing platform that processes sensor and mapping data for autonomous driving. Baidu dominates in the Asia Pacific with smart city and autonomous driving solutions, while Mapbox offers scalable cloud-based mapping for dynamic location information in semi-autonomous vehicles.
