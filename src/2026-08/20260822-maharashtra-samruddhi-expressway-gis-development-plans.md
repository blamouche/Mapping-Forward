# Maharashtra's 701-Km Samruddhi Expressway Set For GIS-Led Development Around 20+ Interchanges

## Elevator pitch
The Maharashtra State Road Development Corporation has floated global tenders for a consultant to prepare GIS-based development plans for the zones surrounding 20+ interchanges on the 701-km Nagpur-Mumbai Samruddhi Expressway.

## Synthesis
On July 25, 2026, Swarajya reported that the Maharashtra State Road Development Corporation (MSRDC) had floated global tenders to engage a consultant for preparing Geographic Information System (GIS)-based development plans for the zones surrounding interchanges on the Nagpur-Mumbai Samruddhi Expressway. The 701-km access-controlled corridor, also known as the Samruddhi Mahamarg, features over 20 interchanges, each spanning more than 40 acres.

The selected consultant will be tasked with preparing GIS base maps, conducting Existing Land Use (ELU) surveys, and drafting comprehensive GIS-based development plans for the influence areas around each interchange. This spatial planning framework is intended to guide orderly urban growth in pockets likely to experience rapid economic and industrial expansion as a direct consequence of improved connectivity along the expressway.

The tender process follows a structured timeline. A pre-bid meeting is scheduled for August 12, 2026, at MSRDC headquarters in Kohinoor Square, Dadar West. Bidders must submit their proposals online by 3 pm on September 15, 2026. An Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) of Rs 10 lakh has been stipulated as part of the tender conditions.

This initiative reflects a growing trend in India of leveraging GIS technology for infrastructure-driven urban planning. By establishing a detailed spatial planning framework before development pressure intensifies, MSRDC aims to steer sustainable land use, infrastructure planning, and future urban development along the corridor. The Samruddhi Expressway is anticipated to become a key economic axis connecting Mumbai and Nagpur, and the GIS-based plans for interchange influence zones are designed to ensure that growth occurs in a structured, planned manner rather than through ad-hoc development.
