## Synthesis
On July 27, 2026, AVMDergi reported that Next Geo, a Turkish company specializing in location analytics and AI-powered geographic data solutions, had been selected for the "Global Top 100 Geospatial Companies – 2026 Edition" list published by Geoawesome, a global geospatial publication and community platform. Next Geo was the only Turkish company to make the list, joining the ranks of global giants including Google, Mapbox, and HERE Technologies.

The 2026 edition of the list differed from previous years in its selection methodology. Rather than being purely editorial, Geoawesome incorporated community participation, with candidate companies evaluated and voted on by geospatial industry professionals. The list does not rank companies hierarchically but aims to make visible the organizations shaping the geospatial technology ecosystem.

Next Geo's technology portfolio spans several domains of applied geographic intelligence. The company develops solutions for location analytics, investment site selection, target audience analysis, sales forecasting, regional optimization, and AI-powered decision support systems. Its platform integrates diverse data sources — demographic data, consumer profiles, lifestyle segments, commercial density, competitive landscapes, mobility data, and enterprise customer data — into a unified geographic framework that produces actionable business insights.

The company serves multiple sectors including retail, food and beverage, finance, real estate, fast-moving consumer goods, fitness, and government. Next Geo CEO Fevzi Babalık emphasized the company's mission of transforming geographic data from mere map-displayed information into a strategic asset that guides investment, growth, and operational decisions. The recognition by Geoawesome validates the company's GeoAI approach and signals its ambition to expand internationally, targeting markets in the Middle East, Europe, and neighboring regions with its scalable software infrastructure and AI-driven decision models.
