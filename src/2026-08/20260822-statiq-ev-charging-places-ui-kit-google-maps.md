# Statiq: Improving User Experience and Increasing EV App Usage with Places UI Kit
**Source**: https://mapsplatform.google.com/resources/blog/statiq-improving-user-experience-and-increasing-ev-app-usage-with-places-ui-kit/
**Date**: August 22, 2026
**Author**: Raghav Arora (Statiq) / Google Maps Platform
**Keywords**: Google Maps Platform, Places UI Kit, Statiq, EV charging, India, Directions API, navigation, user experience, location intelligence

## Elevator pitch
India's largest EV charging network Statiq integrated Google Maps Platform's Places UI Kit directly into its app, enabling an end-to-end charging experience that produced a 13% increase in navigation starts, 15% faster session completion, and 9% more repeat charging sessions within three months.

## Takeaways
- Statiq is India's largest EV charging network with 2000+ owned stations, 80% of all stations listed, and over 1 million app downloads
- Places UI Kit integration took just three weeks from project start to product launch using ready-made components
- After relaunching in January 2026: 13% increase in navigation starts, 15% reduction in time to successful session, 7% increase in charger discovery interactions
- Revenue impact: 9% increase in repeat sessions, 8% increase in session frequency, 8% reduction in abandoned searches, 3% uptick in download-to-charge conversion
- 10% improvement in overall user satisfaction measured through in-app ratings and post-session feedback

## Synthesis
Statiq's integration of Google Maps Platform's Places UI Kit illustrates a strategic shift from infrastructure-led growth to experience-led growth in the EV charging sector. The core insight is that EV drivers need more than just the location of a charging station — they want to know about nearby amenities like restaurants, cafés, and parking while their vehicle charges for what can be up to an hour.

Before the integration, Statiq customers would use the Statiq app to find a charging station, then separately open Google Maps to navigate there. The Places UI Kit enabled Statiq to embed Google Maps directly into their app interface, offering an end-to-end experience: locate stations, view nearby amenities with user-contributed photos and reviews, navigate, charge, and pay — all within the Statiq app. The implementation used a horizontal scroll view in card format to display Places data including establishment names, images, and distance calculated through the Directions API.

The measurable results validate the experience-led approach. The 13% increase in navigation starts and 15% reduction in time-to-session indicate that reducing friction in the discovery-to-charge workflow directly drives engagement. The 9% increase in repeat sessions and 8% increase in session frequency translate to tangible revenue growth. Statiq plans to extend the experience with Google Street View integration, enabling drivers to virtually preview charging stations before arrival.
