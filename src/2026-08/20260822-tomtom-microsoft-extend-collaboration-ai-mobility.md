# TomTom and Microsoft Extend Collaboration on AI-Powered Mobility Solutions
**Source**: https://www.itsinternational.com/tomtom-and-microsoft-extend-collaboration/
**Date**: 2026-08-18
**Author**: Adam Hill, ITS International
**Keywords**: TomTom, Microsoft Azure, Azure Maps, AI, Overture Maps, automotive, logistics, mobility, Orbis Maps

## Elevator pitch
TomTom and Microsoft have extended their decade-long collaboration to develop AI-powered solutions for automotive, logistics, and broader mobility domains, with TomTom Orbis Maps continuing to power Azure Maps and Microsoft 365 location features.

## Takeaways
- TomTom and Microsoft have collaborated since 2016, including through the Overture Map Foundation
- Microsoft Azure remains TomTom's preferred cloud provider, and TomTom powers Azure Maps
- Bing, Microsoft Power BI, and Microsoft 365 all use TomTom maps and location services
- The companies will develop generative AI-powered automotive solutions and an in-vehicle digital cockpit
- TomTom CEO Harold Goddijn highlighted Orbis Maps powering Microsoft's traffic and location data
- Microsoft is already rolling out TomTom Traffic based on the new TomTom Orbis Maps platform

## Synthesis
Location specialist TomTom is extending its long-running collaboration with Microsoft, a partnership that dates back to 2016 and includes joint work through the Overture Map Foundation. The extended agreement focuses on developing AI-powered solutions for the automotive, logistics, and broader mobility domains.

The infrastructure layer of this partnership is significant: TomTom powers Microsoft's Azure Maps, which enables developers to incorporate mapping and location capabilities into solutions ranging from spatial analytics to Internet of Things and asset tracking. Beyond Azure, TomTom's maps and services support location-based features across Bing, Microsoft Power BI, and Microsoft 365. Microsoft is currently rolling out TomTom Traffic, based on the new TomTom Orbis Maps platform, promising fresher and more accurate location data across Microsoft's ecosystem.

TomTom CEO Harold Goddijn emphasized that the collaboration will create innovative, AI-based products designed to deliver customer value. Nicholas Lee, Corporate Vice President of Microsoft Maps & Local, noted that hosting TomTom's services in Microsoft Azure enables seamless integration with Microsoft's services across industries. The two companies are also collaborating on automotive-specific solutions, including generative AI-powered automotive solutions and an in-vehicle digital cockpit and infotainment system with cloud analytics. This partnership positions TomTom as a key mapping infrastructure provider within the Microsoft cloud ecosystem, competing with Google's in-house mapping capabilities and Amazon's partnerships with HERE Technologies.
