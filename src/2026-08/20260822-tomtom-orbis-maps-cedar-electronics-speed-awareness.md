# Cedar Electronics Selects TomTom Orbis Maps to Enhance Speed Awareness Across the United States
**Source**: https://www.tomtom.com/newsroom/press-releases/general/210397097400908/cedar-electronics-selects-tomtom-orbis-maps-to-enhance-speed-awareness-across-the-united-states/
**Date**: 2026-08-19
**Author**: TomTom Newsroom
**Keywords**: TomTom, Orbis Maps, speed limit data, Cedar Electronics, radar detectors, ADAS, probe data

## Elevator pitch
TomTom partners with Cedar Electronics to supply Orbis Maps speed limit data for Escort and Cobra radar detectors, leveraging multi-source probe intelligence to deliver accurate, frequently refreshed speed awareness alerts across the US.

## Takeaways
- Cedar Electronics selected TomTom Orbis Maps for integration into its Escort and Cobra radar detector product lines
- TomTom's speed intelligence combines official government datasets, field-verified surveys, and anonymized connected-vehicle probe data
- The multi-source approach validates and enriches speed information even on roads where signing is incomplete or inconsistent
- The partnership aims to improve real-time speed awareness alerts for drivers across the United States
- This deal signals that probe-based speed intelligence is becoming standard for automotive safety products

## Synthesis
TomTom announced on August 19, 2026 that Cedar Electronics, a leading provider of automotive safety and driver awareness solutions, has selected TomTom Orbis Maps for its Escort and Cobra radar detector products. The integration leverages TomTom's frequently refreshed speed limit data to help drivers stay informed of posted speed limits and changing road conditions across the United States.

What makes this announcement significant is TomTom's speed intelligence methodology. Rather than relying on a single data source, TomTom builds its speed data from multiple complementary inputs: official government and municipal datasets, field-verified survey information, and anonymized probe data from connected vehicles, navigation devices, and apps. This layered approach enables the company to validate, enrich, and frequently refresh speed information across the entire road network, including roads where speed limit information may be incomplete, inconsistently signed, or actively changing. The result is highly reliable speed data at scale.

Jeff Stevens, Head of R&D at Cedar Electronics, emphasized that customers rely on the products for accurate, real-time information to make safer driving decisions. Benoit Joly, Head of Sales at TomTom, noted that reliable speed limit information is an essential component for more informed driving experiences. The partnership reflects a broader industry trend: probe-based speed intelligence is becoming standard for real-time automotive safety products, and mapping companies that can combine authoritative sources with crowd-sourced data are positioning themselves as critical infrastructure providers for ADAS and driver awareness ecosystems.
