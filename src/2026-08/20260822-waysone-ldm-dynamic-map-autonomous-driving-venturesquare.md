# Maps That Read Real-Time Road Changes: WaysOne Unveils LDM Technology
**Source**: https://www.venturesquare.net/en/1107749/
**Date**: 2026-08-22
**Author**: Venturesquare
**Keywords**: Local Dynamic Map, LDM, autonomous driving, V2X, high-precision maps, smart transportation, Korea

## Elevator pitch
Korean startup WayzOne has unveiled a Local Dynamic Map (LDM) operating system that extends autonomous vehicle perception beyond sensor range by integrating real-time road conditions into high-precision maps, with government policy support extended through 2027.

## Takeaways
- WayzOne unveiled three LDM solutions — LDM2X, LDM TWIN, and AVION — at the 2026 Autonomous Driving Mobility Industry Exhibition (AME 2026) in Seoul
- LDM2X combines real-time road information (signals, traffic, incidents) with high-precision maps and converts it to standard V2X messages for vehicles
- LDM TWIN creates a real-time digital twin of traffic signals and vehicle flow for traffic operators to detect and predict hazardous situations
- AVION is a smart HMI that visualizes 3D surrounding objects and autonomous vehicle movement paths for drivers and control personnel
- The company was selected as an "Innovation Premier 1000" company by Korea's Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport, extending support through end of 2027

## Synthesis
WayzOne, a Korean spatial information-based autonomous driving and smart transportation infrastructure company, has unveiled its Local Dynamic Map (LDM) operating system technology at the 2026 Autonomous Driving Mobility Industry Exhibition (AME 2026) at COEX in Seoul. The company's core innovation addresses a fundamental limitation of autonomous vehicles: while onboard cameras and LiDAR can detect immediate surroundings, they cannot perceive road conditions beyond sensor range. LDM solves this by continuously feeding real-time road data — traffic signals, traffic volume, accidents, construction — into high-precision maps and transmitting them to vehicles via V2X communication.

The three-product portfolio serves different stakeholders in the autonomous driving ecosystem. LDM2X is the vehicle-facing solution that converts real-time road information into standard V2X messages, enabling safe driving decisions by providing advance information about road conditions that vehicle sensors cannot directly detect. LDM TWIN targets traffic operators, implementing a digital twin of traffic signals and vehicle flow in real time to analyze collected data, detect hazardous situations, and enable rapid response. AVION is the human-machine interface that visualizes surrounding 3D objects and the autonomous vehicle's movement path, designed for intuitive monitoring by both drivers and control personnel.

The technology's commercialization is supported by the Korean government through the "Innovation Premier 1000" program, which recognizes companies with strong technological competitiveness. WayzOne was simultaneously selected by the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport and the Ministry of SMEs and Startups, extending its policy financing support period from the original end of 2026 to the end of 2027. This extension provides the foundation for simultaneous technological advancement and business expansion. The company's participation in AME 2026 includes a demonstration program showcasing the actual operation of its autonomous driving operating system, positioning LDM as a key element in the competitive landscape of autonomous driving infrastructure.
