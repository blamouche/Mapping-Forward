# Zürich Moves Property Ownership Inquiry to New GIS-Browser
**Source**: https://www.zh.ch/de/news-uebersicht/mitteilungen/2026/planen-bauen/geoinformation/eigentumsauskunft-neu-im-geoportal.html
**Date**: 2026-08-20
**Author**: Kanton Zürich, Amt für Raumentwicklung – Abteilung Geoinformation
**Keywords**: Zürich, GIS-Browser, property ownership, geoportal, eigentumsauskunft, cadastre, Switzerland, geospatial portal

## Elevator pitch
The Canton of Zürich has migrated property ownership inquiries (Eigentumsauskunft) from the old geoportal application to the new GIS-Browser, providing a more accessible map-based interface for property information lookup.

## Takeaways
- The Eigentumsauskunft (property ownership inquiry) function has been moved to Zürich's new GIS-Browser as of August 20, 2026
- The function was deactivated in the previous application
- Users access property information through a dedicated "Eigentumsauskunft" map containing relevant data layers
- Clicking on a property in the map displays an information panel with a link to ownership details
- The migration is part of Zürich's broader modernization of its geospatial portal infrastructure

## Synthesis
The Canton of Zürich's Office of Spatial Development, Department of Geoinformation, has announced that the Eigentumsauskunft (property ownership inquiry) function is now available in the new GIS-Browser. As of August 20, 2026, the function has been deactivated in the previous application, completing the migration to the modernized platform.

The new approach provides a more intuitive map-based workflow. Users open the GIS-Browser and navigate to the "Eigentumsauskunft" map, which contains the data layers relevant to property ownership queries. Clicking on the desired parcel displays an information panel on the right side with a link to the Eigentumsauskunft. Following the link and completing the subsequent steps provides the ownership information.

This migration represents part of Zürich's ongoing modernization of its geospatial infrastructure. The GIS-Browser serves as the central hub for the canton's geographic information services, and consolidating property ownership inquiries into this platform streamlines access for citizens, notaries, and real estate professionals who need official property records. The Department of Geoinformation has established a help center through the geoportal's service desk for users with questions about the GIS-Browser.
