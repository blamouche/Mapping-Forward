# Google Maps Bug Won't Show Traffic Data on Android Auto and More
**Source**: https://9to5google.com/2026/08/24/google-maps-traffic-data-broken-android-auto/
**Date**: 2026-08-24
**Author**: Ben Schoon, 9to5Google
**Keywords**: Google Maps, Android Auto, traffic data, bug, traffic colors, navigation

## Elevator pitch
A recurring Google Maps bug that strips traffic data from the map has resurfaced, this time affecting Android Auto users who can no longer see the green, yellow, and red traffic lines while navigating.

## Takeaways
- Traffic data (green/yellow/red road lines) is broken for many Google Maps users on Android Auto
- The same bug was fixed in July 2026 but has returned, particularly affecting Android Auto
- Some map settings still show traffic data, but default navigation view is broken
- Google has not publicly acknowledged this latest wave of the bug
- Uninstalling Google Maps updates may temporarily fix the issue
- The bug may be linked to the Immersive Navigation rollout

## Synthesis
Google Maps' traffic data feature, which uses green, yellow, and red lines to indicate traffic flow speed, has become unreliable for many users. The issue first appeared in July 2026, when widespread reports of broken traffic data led Google to fix the problem. However, the bug has resurfaced, this time primarily affecting Android Auto users.

Reports from the past several days indicate that traffic data remains at least partially broken on Android Auto. Some users report that certain map settings still display traffic data, but general navigation in the default view shows no traffic lines. The issue also persists for some users on phones directly. The problem appears to be connected to the rollout of Google's Immersive Navigation feature, which was deployed to Android Auto in recent weeks.

Google has not publicly acknowledged this latest wave of the bug. Some users have found that uninstalling Google Maps updates temporarily restores traffic data, though this is not a permanent solution. The recurring nature of the bug and its timing alongside the Immersive Navigation rollout suggest a server-side issue rather than a simple app version problem, making it difficult for users to resolve on their own.
