## Takeaways
- The Colwash Fire spans Yakima and Benton counties in south-central Washington state
- As of August 24, 2026, the fire has burned 38,708.9 acres and is 8% contained
- Level 3 "Go" evacuation orders are in effect for areas in both Benton and Yakima counties

- An American Red Cross evacuation shelter has been established at Prosser High School

- Real-time air quality monitoring and wildfire maps are critical tools for public safety

## Synthesis
IQAir's wildfire map spotlight details the Colwash Fire, an active wildfire burning across Yakima and Benton counties in south-central Washington state as of August 24, 2026. The fire has consumed 38,708.9 acres and is only 8% contained, making it a significant incident requiring extensive emergency response and public safety measures.

The fire affects areas near Mabton and along State Routes 22 and 221, including land near Glade Road, Byron Hill Road, and the Yakima-Benton county line. Evacuation orders are tiered: Level 3 "Go" orders mandate immediate evacuation in the most threatened areas of both counties, Level 2 "Set" warnings prepare residents for potential evacuation, and Level 1 "Ready" notices advise awareness. The American Red Cross has established an evacuation shelter at Prosser High School.

The article emphasizes the importance of wildfire mapping and real-time air quality monitoring during such incidents. IQAir positions its air quality apps and monitors as essential tools for protecting against wildfire smoke, recommending that residents shut doors and windows, use HVAC recirculation, run air purifiers, and wear KN95/FFP2 masks when going outdoors. This spotlight illustrates how mapping technologies and air quality data integration are critical for public safety during wildfire events.
