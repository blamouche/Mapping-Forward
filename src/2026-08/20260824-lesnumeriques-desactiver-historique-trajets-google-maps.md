# En désactivant l'historique de trajets de Google Maps, j'ai récupéré du stockage et ma vie privée

**Keywords**: Google Maps, location history, privacy, storage, Vos trajets, timeline, data management

## Elevator pitch
Les Numériques explains how disabling Google Maps' trip history feature not only protects privacy but also reclaims valuable smartphone storage space, with the function now storing data locally by default since 2024.

## Takeaways
- Google Maps' "Vos trajets" (Your Trips) feature stores location history and movement data on-device since 2024
- The function is disabled by default but can consume significant storage when activated
- Disabling it frees up storage space and eliminates personal movement tracking
- Users can set automatic deletion of trip history after 3, 18, or 36 months
- The feature does not share data with third parties by default when enabled
- Optional settings allow sharing trip data for ad measurement or experience improvement

## Synthesis
Les Numériques provides a practical guide to disabling Google Maps' trip history feature, noting that since 2024 the function has undergone significant changes. Previously storing data in the cloud, it now keeps location history locally on the device by default. While this shift improves privacy by avoiding remote servers, it means the feature can consume substantial storage space on smartphones by accumulating personal movement data.

The article explains that the feature, while useful for "traveling back in time" to find previously visited places or routes, is far from essential. It is disabled by default, but for users who have it activated, the article provides step-by-step instructions to disable it and reclaim storage space. The guide also notes that users can opt for a middle ground — keeping the feature active while setting automatic deletion of history after 3, 18, or 36 months. Optional settings allow sharing trip data for ad effectiveness measurement or experience improvement, but these are off by default. The source emphasizes that disabling the feature has no impact on navigation functionality itself.
