# Connaissez-vous My Maps, la meilleure alternative à Google Maps en voyage ?
**Source**: https://www.lesnumeriques.com/societe-numerique/connaissez-vous-my-maps-la-meilleure-alternative-a-google-maps-en-voyage-n260737.html
**Date**: 2026-08-24
**Author**: Les Numériques
**Keywords**: Google My Maps, Google Maps, travel planning, road trip, custom maps, layers, offline navigation, navigation

## Elevator pitch
Les Numériques explains how Google's little-known My Maps tool serves as a powerful travel planning companion to Google Maps, letting users organize multi-week trips with custom layers, waypoints, and shared editing — though it cannot replace Maps for turn-by-turn navigation.

## Takeaways
- My Maps is a free Google tool for creating personalized maps with custom waypoints, lines, shapes, and thematic layers
- It excels at trip preparation on desktop: organizing stops, visits, reservations, and services across multiple layers
- Google Maps limits itineraries to 10 destinations, while My Maps supports up to 2,000 elements per layer and 10,000 per map
- My Maps custom maps are viewable in the Google Maps mobile app but cannot be used as continuous guided navigation
- Each waypoint accepts a title, description, hours, reservation number, price, photo, and link
- Maps can be shared by link and edited collaboratively, making it a geographic dashboard for group travel
- My Maps is not a reliable offline navigation tool; Google Maps' offline downloads remain necessary for driving

## Synthesis
Les Numériques offers a detailed guide to Google My Maps, positioning it as the ideal planning complement to Google Maps for long-distance travel. The key distinction is that Google Maps calculates routes and provides turn-by-turn guidance, while My Maps creates customized geographic dashboards where travelers can organize every aspect of their trip across thematic layers — accommodations, driving stages, visits, supply points, services, hikes, and backup plans.

The article highlights practical limits: Google Maps caps itineraries at 10 destinations, which is insufficient for multi-week trips. My Maps circumvents this by allowing users to build a comprehensive map with up to 10 layers, 2,000 elements per layer, and 10,000 elements per map. A road trip can be segmented into calque-based stages (e.g., Paris–Avignon, Avignon–Nice), with all locations visible on a single shared map.

However, My Maps has clear limitations. Custom maps are viewable in the Google Maps app but cannot be used as continuous guided navigation. Drawn lines remain visual references only. For driving, users must launch navigation to individual stops or reconstruct a multi-stop itinerary within Google Maps' own engine. The article recommends a combined approach: a My Maps overview for planning, pre-downloaded Google Maps areas for offline navigation, and locally saved coordinates for each stop.
