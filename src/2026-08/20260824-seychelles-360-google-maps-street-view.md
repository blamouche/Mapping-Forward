# Les Seychelles bientôt visibles en 360° sur Google Maps
**Source**: https://www.seychellesnewsagency.com/public/articles/22321/les-seychelles-bientot-visibles-en-360-sur-google-maps
**Date**: 2026-08-24
**Author**: Seychelles News Agency
**Keywords**: Google Maps, Street View, 360° photography, Seychelles, tourism mapping, WTI 360, virtual tours

## Elevator pitch
The Seychelles will soon be visible in 360° on Google Maps through a project led by World Travel in 360 (WTI 360) with the support of the Seychelles Tourism Board, bringing the archipelago's main attractions to virtual visitors worldwide.

## Takeaways
- The Seychelles will become visible in 360° on Google Maps through a filming project
- The project is led by World Travel in 360 (WTI 360) with support from the Seychelles Tourism Board (STB)
- WTI 360 will film the principal tourist attractions and scenic locations across the archipelago
- The 360° imagery will be integrated into Google Maps, making the Seychelles accessible to virtual visitors
- This initiative supports tourism promotion by allowing potential visitors to preview destinations
- The project continues Google Maps' global expansion of Street View and 360° coverage to remote destinations

## Synthesis
The Seychelles News Agency reports that the Seychelles will soon be visible in 360° on Google Maps through a dedicated filming project. The initiative is being carried out by World Travel in 360 (WTI 360), a company specializing in immersive destination photography, with the support of the Seychelles Tourism Board (STB). The project consists of filming the archipelago's principal tourist attractions and scenic locations to create immersive 360° imagery for Google Maps.

This development represents another step in Google Maps' ongoing expansion of Street View and 360° coverage to remote and tourism-relevant destinations. For the Seychelles, a nation whose economy is heavily dependent on tourism, the addition of 360° imagery to Google Maps provides a powerful promotional tool — allowing potential visitors from around the world to virtually explore the islands' beaches, nature reserves, and cultural sites before booking a trip. The collaboration between WTI 360 and the STB illustrates how national tourism boards are increasingly partnering with mapping platforms to enhance their digital visibility and attract international visitors through immersive online experiences.
