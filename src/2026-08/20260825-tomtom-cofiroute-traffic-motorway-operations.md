# Cofiroute Selects TomTom Traffic to Enhance Real-Time Motorway Operations and Driver Information
**Source**: https://www.tomtom.com/newsroom/press-releases/general/212447736991280/cofiroute-selects-tomtom-traffic-to-enhance-real-time-motorway-operations-and-driver-information/
**Date**: 2026-08-25
**Author**: TomTom Newsroom
**Keywords**: TomTom, Cofiroute, VINCI Autoroutes, TomTom Route Monitoring, traffic management, motorway operations, variable message signs, VMS, real-time traffic, France

## Elevator pitch
TomTom announced that Cofiroute, a division of VINCI Autoroutes and a leading private French highway operator, has selected TomTom Traffic and Route Monitoring to provide real-time traffic visibility, incident management, and driver information across its motorway network.

## Takeaways
- Cofiroute, a division of VINCI Autoroutes, has selected TomTom Traffic for its motorway network
- The solution leverages TomTom Route Monitoring to monitor traffic conditions in real time on designated areas or routes
- Cofiroute uses the technology for incident management and to inform drivers via variable message sign (VMS) boards
- Traffic information can be published through road signs, websites, mobile applications, and other public channels
- Jérôme Pasteau, Head of Traffic Safety Solutions at Cofiroute, emphasized timely and reliable driver information
- Nicolas Durvaux, Operations Manager at Cofiroute, highlighted a consistent real-time approach across the network
- Benoit Joly, Head of Sales at TomTom, emphasized faster, more informed operational decisions for Cofiroute teams

## Synthesis
TomTom has announced a significant partnership with Cofiroute, a division of VINCI Autoroutes and one of France's leading private highway operators. The agreement sees Cofiroute deploy TomTom Traffic, powered by TomTom Route Monitoring, across its motorway network to enhance real-time traffic operations and driver communication. The announcement was made on August 25, 2026 from TomTom's Amsterdam headquarters.

The solution enables Cofiroute to monitor traffic conditions in real time on designated areas or specific routes, supporting incident management and driver information through variable message sign (VMS) boards along its routes. Beyond VMS boards, the platform allows Cofiroute to publish traffic information to road users through multiple channels including websites, mobile applications, and other public communication systems, creating a multi-modal approach to traffic communication.

Jérôme Pasteau, Head of Traffic Safety Solutions at Cofiroute, emphasized that providing timely, reliable information to drivers is an important part of motorway network management, noting that TomTom's traffic technology gives teams a clear real-time view of traffic conditions. Nicolas Durvaux, Operations Manager, added that the technology helps maintain a consistent, real-time approach to operations across the entire network. Benoit Joly, Head of Sales at TomTom, highlighted that the collaboration supports Cofiroute's efforts to keep traffic moving and drivers informed through faster, more informed operational decisions. The partnership reinforces TomTom's position as a key provider of traffic intelligence to major highway operators across Europe.
