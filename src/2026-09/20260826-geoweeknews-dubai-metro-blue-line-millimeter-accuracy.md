# Capturing the Dubai Metro Blue Line with Millimeter Accuracy

**Source**: https://www.geoweeknews.com/articles/capturing-the-dubai-metro-blue-line-with-millimeter-accuracy/
**Date**: 2026-08-26
**Author**: Geo Week News
**Keywords**: Dubai Metro, 3D laser scanning, point clouds, Topcon, surveying, BIM, infrastructure, millimeter accuracy

## Elevator pitch
Geo Week News details how the Dubai Metro Blue Line surveying team used 3D laser scanning technology to capture existing metro stations and infrastructure with sub-5mm accuracy, supporting one of Dubai's most ambitious transportation projects.

## Takeaways
- The Dubai Metro Blue Line is a 30 km expansion with 14 stations, including underground sections, elevated viaducts, and Dubai Metro's first bridge over Dubai Creek
- Senior Survey Manager Murat Pehlivan's team used the Topcon CR-P1 laser scanner and GT-1200 total station to achieve positional accuracy under 5 mm
- 3D laser scanning replaced traditional surveying methods to capture large, complex structures at very high detail within a three-month timeframe

## Synthesis
The Dubai Metro Blue Line is a major expansion of the Dubai Metro network, currently under construction and scheduled to open in September 2029. Spanning approximately 30 km and featuring fourteen stations, the line will include underground sections, elevated viaducts, and Dubai Metro's first bridge crossing over Dubai Creek. The contract was awarded to the Mapa Limak CRRC Consortium, a partnership between Turkish companies MAPA and LIMAK and China's CRRC.

Within the consortium, Senior Survey Manager Murat Pehlivan and his surveying team are responsible for acquiring high-precision data of both new and existing project structures. Rather than relying on traditional surveying methods, the team chose 3D laser scanning as its primary data acquisition method, using the Topcon CR-P1 laser scanner and GT-1200 total station. This enabled the team to capture large and complex structures at a very high level of detail within a three-month timeframe, achieving positional accuracy under 5 mm.

The scanning campaign covered two existing metro stations—Creek and Centrepoint (formerly Rashidiya)—which are part of the existing Green and Red lines that will be connected by the new Blue Line. Each station covers approximately 13,000–14,000 square meters. The team also scanned the R11 multi-story parking structure, covering 150,000 square meters, including interior and exterior areas, access gates, footbridges, parking up to the rooftop level, and bus movement zones.

The deliverables are high-accuracy point cloud files that support engineering design and BIM model coordination. Upcoming scanning work will include underground tunnels, elevated viaducts, and new stations. The project demonstrates how 3D laser scanning and point cloud technology are transforming infrastructure surveying, enabling millimeter-accuracy documentation of complex existing conditions that would be impractical with traditional methods.
