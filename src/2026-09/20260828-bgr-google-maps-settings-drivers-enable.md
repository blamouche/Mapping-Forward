# 5 Google Maps Settings Drivers Should Enable

**Source**: https://www.bgr.com/2242473/google-maps-settings-drivers-should-enable/
**Date**: 2026-08-28
**Author**: Chris Smith, BGR
**Keywords**: Google Maps, navigation settings, car profile, offline maps, EV charging, Gemini AI, driving

## Elevator pitch
BGR highlights five key Google Maps settings drivers should enable to improve their navigation experience, including customising car profiles for EV owners, downloading offline maps, sharing ETAs, and enabling Gemini AI voice control.

## Takeaways
- Drivers should customise their car profile (gas, diesel, hybrid, electric) so Google Maps can suggest the most efficient route and show EV charging options
- Offline maps are essential for areas with spotty cellular connectivity, roaming charges abroad, or during natural disasters that disrupt service
- Sharing ETAs with friends and family provides safety benefits during road trips
- Gemini AI integration enables hands-free voice control for adding stops, finding restaurants, and asking questions about the route
- The settings are accessible on both Android and iPhone through the Google Maps navigation menu

## Synthesis
BGR's Chris Smith outlines five Google Maps settings that drivers should enable to go beyond the app's default navigation experience. While Google Maps automatically provides turn-by-turn voice directions, live traffic support, lane guidance, and ETA information, several additional features can significantly improve the driving experience.

The most impactful setting is customising the car profile. By default, Google Maps assumes a gasoline vehicle. Selecting diesel, hybrid, or electric changes how the app calculates efficient routes and enables EV-specific features such as charging station recommendations and estimated battery charge on arrival. For Android Auto users with supported EVs, the app can display battery predictions and an "Add Charging" button during route setup. The Plugs & Adapters menu also lets users specify compatible charging connectors, including Tesla plugs.

Offline maps remain a critical feature for drivers traveling through remote areas with poor cellular coverage or abroad where roaming charges apply. Google Maps allows users to download map regions for offline navigation, which can also be invaluable during natural disasters that disrupt cellular service. The app can still provide GPS-based turn-by-turn directions using downloaded map data.

Gemini AI integration represents the newest enhancement. On Android, Gemini can replace Google Assistant for hands-free navigation control, allowing drivers to add stops, search for restaurants, or ask questions about their route by voice. On iPhone, Gemini is accessible via a tap during navigation but has more limited functionality. The integration enables conversational queries about places of interest along the route, making it easier to find services without taking eyes off the road.
