# AMC Approves Citywide GIS Mapping and Digital Door IDs for Ahmedabad
**Source**: https://www.constructionworld.in/policy-updates-and-economic-news/amc-approves-citywide-gis-mapping-and-digital-door-ids/96787
**Date**: 2026-09-01
**Author**: Construction World

## Elevator pitch
The Amdavad Municipal Corporation approved a citywide GIS project covering 625 sq km with aerial LiDAR, 5,200 km of road surveys, 2.5 million geo-coded properties with Digital Door IDs, and a 3D digital twin — one of India's most ambitious urban mapping initiatives.

## Takeaways
- Aerial LiDAR and photography will cover the 625 sq km municipal area with 240 ground control points
- 5,200 km of roads surveyed with Mobile LiDAR and 360-degree imaging; underground utilities recorded for asset management
- 2.5 million properties geo-coded through door-to-door surveys with Digital Door IDs for property tax assessment
- 3D digital twin with Level of Detail 3 models for 500 key buildings
- 12 GIS-based applications for town planning, disaster management, health services, and a citizen geoportal

## Synthesis
The Amdavad Municipal Corporation (AMC) has approved one of India's most comprehensive citywide GIS mapping programs. The project updates the city's 2014 GIS to reflect urban expansion and infrastructure upgrades across the 625 sq km municipal area. Aerial LiDAR and photography will produce 30cm Digital Terrain Models, Digital Surface Models, and a 1:500 scale base map, supported by 240 DGPS ground control points.

The scope is remarkable: 5,200 km of roads will be surveyed with Mobile LiDAR and 360-degree imaging, while underground utilities including water pipelines and sewer lines will be recorded for asset management. Approximately 2.5 million properties will be geo-coded through door-to-door surveys and assigned Digital Door IDs to aid property tax assessment and verification. True orthophotos with 5cm accuracy will be produced.

The project includes a 3D digital twin with Level of Detail 3 models for 500 key buildings, and 12 GIS-based applications supporting town planning, disaster management, health services, and a citizen geoportal. These will be integrated with property tax systems, the Centralised Complaint Redressal System, and SCADA. During the five-year operation and maintenance period, the GIS database will be updated using drone and satellite imagery with machine learning-based change detection applied to annual satellite data.
