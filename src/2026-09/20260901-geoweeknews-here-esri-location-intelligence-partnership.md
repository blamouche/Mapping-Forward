# HERE and Esri Bring a New Chapter in Location Intelligence
**Source**: https://www.geoweeknews.com/articles/here-and-esri-bring-a-new-chapter-in-location-intelligence/
**Date**: 2026-09-01
**Author**: Abigail Hart (Geo Week News)
**Keywords**: HERE Technologies, Esri, ArcGIS, location intelligence, GIS, AI, location reasoning layer

## Elevator pitch
HERE Technologies and Esri are deepening their two-decade partnership to make location data a first-class enterprise IT asset, with a new location reasoning layer that lets AI interpret geospatial data without expensive model retraining.

## Takeaways
- HERE and Esri are expanding their partnership beyond API-delivered data to persistent location data accessible through ArcGIS visualization and analytics
- HERE argues location data deserves recognition as a distinct discipline within enterprise IT strategy, not just another dataset
- A new "location reasoning layer" acts as an intermediary allowing AI to interpret location data without requiring full model training
- The partnership aims to bring Esri's ArcGIS capabilities to non-GIS users working with traffic, EV charging, and autonomous vehicle data
- Early assumptions that knowledge graphs and vector queries would suffice for AI integration proved insufficient — AI hasn't been trained on geospatial services

## Synthesis
The collaboration between HERE Technologies and Esri represents what both companies describe as the most consequential phase of a partnership spanning over two decades. Chris Handley, HERE's senior vice president of project management, frames the initiative around a fundamental shift: location data should not be consumed in single API transactions but persisted behind visualization and analytics engines where it can serve financial controllers, operations teams, and executives seeking decision support.

Central to the expanded partnership is the challenge of making geospatial data accessible to artificial intelligence. Maps were designed as two-dimensional representations for human interpretation, but AI systems have not been trained on these services. HERE's solution is a location reasoning layer — an intermediary that allows AI to interpret location data without the expensive adjustments typically required for model training. The partnership with Esri applies this method to GIS data through the ArcGIS platform, which HERE considers best-in-class for visualization and analytics.

A key strategic goal is extending Esri's capabilities to non-GIS users. HERE customers working with traffic data, vehicle maneuvers, EV charging datasets, and autonomous vehicle information stand to benefit from tools previously reserved for GIS specialists. For Esri, this means extending their software's reach into a broader customer base. The drivers include the rise of agentic workflows that access data autonomously and the recognition of location as a primary key for structuring enterprise data.
