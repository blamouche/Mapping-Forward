# Why Ford Chose Apple Maps Over Google Maps for Its New EV Platform
**Source**: https://www.igen.fr/services/2026/09/pourquoi-ford-prefere-apple-maps-google-maps-pour-sa-nouvelle-plateforme-157769
**Date**: 2026-09-02
**Author**: iGeneration
**Keywords**: Ford, Apple Maps, Google Maps, EV navigation, autonomous driving, CarPlay, Fathom

## Elevator pitch
Ford chose Apple Maps over Google Maps for its upcoming universal EV platform (Fathom) because Apple offered better integration with vehicle systems for range estimation, charging, and battery preconditioning.

## Takeaways
- Ford selected Apple Maps for its universal EV platform, with the first model called Fathom
- Alan Clarke, Ford's VP of advanced vehicle projects, cited Apple Maps' constant improvements and engineering team flexibility as key factors
- EV navigation requires deep integration with powertrain systems: range estimation, real-time charging station availability, battery preconditioning
- Apple's ability to cross-reference navigation data with topography and weather for dynamic consumption predictions was decisive
- This is native integration, not CarPlay Ultra — Apple Maps runs on Ford's own operating system

## Synthesis
Ford's decision to integrate Apple Maps natively into its future electric vehicle platform represents a significant win for Apple's mapping service, which was widely ridiculed at its launch. Alan Clarke, Ford's vice president of advanced vehicle projects, explained in an interview with GreenCars that the choice came down to two factors: the steady improvements Apple has made to Maps over recent years, and the flexibility of Apple's engineering team.

The technical requirements for EV navigation go far beyond traditional turn-by-turn directions. In an electric vehicle, the navigation system becomes the orchestrator of the powertrain, needing to estimate remaining range precisely, locate available charging stations in real time, and precondition the battery before arrival at a charging station. Ford required a partner capable of crossing navigation data with topographic and weather information to dynamically adjust consumption forecasts. On these criteria, Apple reportedly delivered the best response.

Importantly, this integration is not CarPlay Ultra. Ford maintains control of its own in-vehicle operating system, with Apple Maps running natively on Ford's software infrastructure. Traditional smartphone projection via CarPlay and Android Auto remains available for drivers who prefer Waze or Google Maps. But by placing Apple Maps at the heart of the vehicle's navigation system, Ford gives Apple a strategic showcase and validates Apple Maps as an infrastructure-grade mapping solution.
