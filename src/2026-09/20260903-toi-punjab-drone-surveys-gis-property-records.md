# Punjab to Digitise Property Records Through Drone Surveys and GIS Mapping
**Source**: https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chandigarh/punjab-to-digitise-property-records-in-urban-villages-through-drone-surveys-gis-mapping/articleshow/133717341.cms
**Date**: 2026-09-03
**Author**: The Times of India
**Keywords**: Punjab, GIS, drone surveys, property records, digitisation, geo-referenced, India

## Elevator pitch
Punjab is launching a pilot project to digitise property records in 50 urban villages using drone surveys and GIS mapping technology to create accurate, transparent, and geo-referenced property documentation.

## Synthesis
The Indian state of Punjab is embarking on a significant property record digitisation initiative, targeting 50 urban villages in a pilot project that leverages drone surveys and GIS technology. The project aims to create accurate, transparent, and geo-referenced property records that improve property record management and reduce disputes.

The use of drone surveys combined with GIS mapping represents a modern approach to land administration, replacing traditional paper-based records with digital, spatially accurate documentation. This initiative aligns with broader Indian government efforts to modernise land records through technology, including similar programs in other states. The geo-referenced approach ensures that each property record is tied to precise geographic coordinates, enabling better urban planning, property tax assessment, and dispute resolution.

The project reflects a growing trend across Indian states of using geospatial technology for governance and land administration, building on frameworks like the Digital India Land Records Modernization Programme.
