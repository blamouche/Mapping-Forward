## Elevator pitch
Google launched WeatherNext 3, an AI-powered meteorological model from DeepMind that integrates real-time satellite observations to deliver precipitation forecasts up to 50% more accurate, with hourly updates and 5km spatial resolution.

## Takeaways
- WeatherNext 3 is Google DeepMind's latest AI weather prediction model
- Forecasts update every hour (vs. 6-hour lag in WeatherNext 2)
- Spatial resolution improved from 25km to 5km
- Precipitation forecasts are up to 50% more accurate
- The model integrates directly with Google Maps and Gemini

## Synthesis
Google unveiled WeatherNext 3, the third generation of its AI-powered meteorological model developed by Google DeepMind and Google Research. The new model represents a significant leap in both temporal frequency and spatial precision for weather forecasting, with direct integration into Google Maps and the Gemini AI assistant.

The most notable improvement is the shift from WeatherNext 2's six-hour forecast update cycle to hourly updates. WeatherNext 3 achieves this by processing raw meteorological observations in real-time, including satellite data, rather than relying on pre-processed inputs. This enables the model to capture rapidly evolving phenomena like localised rain and wind events that previous versions would miss.

Spatial resolution has also improved dramatically, from 25 kilometres to 5 kilometres at the surface level. This means forecasts can distinguish weather conditions between neighbourhoods rather than treating entire metropolitan areas as uniform zones. Google claims precipitation predictions are up to 50% more accurate than the previous model.

The rapid iteration — WeatherNext 2 was only launched in November 2025 — reflects Google DeepMind's aggressive push into AI meteorology, following GraphCast (2023), GenCast with probabilistic forecasts (2024), and now WeatherNext 3. The integration with Google Maps suggests weather-aware routing could become a standard feature, potentially warning users of impending precipitation on their planned routes or suggesting alternative timing.
