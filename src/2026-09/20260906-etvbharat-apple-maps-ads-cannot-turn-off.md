# Apple Maps Now Shows Ads You Cannot Turn Off

**Source**: https://www.etvbharat.com/en/technology/apple-maps-now-shows-ads-you-cannot-turn-off-enn26082900441
**Date**: 2026-08-29
**Author**: ETV Bharat Tech Team
**Keywords**: Apple Maps, ads, advertising, Apple One, iPhone, navigation, 9to5Mac, privacy, App Store, North America

## Elevator pitch
Apple has begun showing permanent, non-removable ads in Apple Maps for users in the US and Canada, with no opt-out setting — even for paying Apple One subscribers.

## Takeaways
- Apple Maps now displays permanent ads on the main search screen and in search results for US and Canadian users
- There is no setting to turn off the ads, and even Apple One subscribers will see them
- Apple claims the ads are privacy-preserving, using on-device data not shared with advertisers
- The ads follow Apple's existing ad model in App Store, Apple News, and Apple TV
- Apple plans to expand the ads to the rest of North America soon

## Synthesis
Apple has started showing permanent advertisements in the Maps app for iPhone users in the United States and Canada, marking a significant shift for a navigation service that had previously been ad-free. According to a report from 9to5Mac, Apple confirmed the rollout and plans to expand ads to the rest of North America in the near future.

The ads appear on the main search screen, just below the Suggested Places section, and also within search results, promoting businesses located near the user. Apple states that the ads are private by default, using information stored only on the device, and claims that interacting with ads will not provide advertisers with users' Apple account data. The company says it does not share personal information with third parties.

However, the most controversial aspect is that there is no setting to turn the ads off. They function the same way as ads already found in the App Store — every iPhone user will see them regardless of preference. Even subscribers to Apple One, Apple's premium subscription bundle, have no ad-free option. This is particularly notable given that Apple One subscribers already pay a monthly fee for premium services.

Until now, Apple Maps was distinct from competitors like Google Maps, which has shown business suggestions and ads for years. The move follows months after Apple announced the addition of ads as part of its new business platform rollout. Apple already runs ads in Apple News and Apple TV, and the expansion to Maps represents a broader monetization strategy across its services ecosystem. For now, reports suggest the ads are not too intrusive, but users have no control over their presence.
