# New Maps Show How Israel Has Thwarted Hopes for a Palestinian State

**Source**: https://www.newyorker.com/news/a-reporter-at-large/new-maps-show-how-israel-has-thwarted-hopes-for-a-palestinian-state
**Date**: 2026-08-29
**Author**: The New Yorker
**Keywords**: Israel, Palestine, West Bank, settlements, mapping, cartography, Frank Lowenstein, two-state solution, annexation, Oslo Accords

## Elevator pitch
Ten years after maps of Israeli settlements shocked President Obama, updated 2026 maps show that settlement expansion has confined Palestinians to just 8% of the land, rendering the two-state solution virtually impossible.

## Takeaways
- Over 500,000 Jewish settlers now live among 3 million Palestinians in the West Bank
- Since 2023, Israel approved 104 new settlements, up from just 3 during the entire Obama Administration
- Settler attacks documented by the UN surged from 107 in 2016 to over 5,300 since 2023 — more than 4 per day
- Palestinians now control approximately 8% of the land between the Jordan River and Mediterranean Sea; Israel controls 92%
- Netanyahu campaigns on never allowing a Palestinian state, while far-right Cabinet members suggest driving Palestinians out of Gaza

## Synthesis
Ten years after US special envoy Frank Lowenstein presented President Obama with maps of Israeli settlements in the occupied West Bank, his updated 2026 maps reveal that the situation has deteriorated far beyond the warnings of 2016. The maps, compiled with associate Liam Hamama using data from the Israeli government, Peace Now, the Palestinian authority, and UN agencies, show that settlement expansion has effectively eliminated the possibility of a contiguous Palestinian state.

The numbers are stark. During the eight years of the Obama Administration, Israel approved just three new settlements in the West Bank. Since the start of 2023, it has approved 104. The total number of settlements and outposts has more than doubled from about 300 to approximately 640, with authorized construction exceeding 58,000 housing units. Over 500,000 Jewish settlers now reside among more than 3 million Palestinians in territory designated for a future Palestinian state since the 1947 UN partition resolution.

Violence has escalated in tandem. In 2016, the UN counted 107 settler attacks that damaged Palestinian property or caused casualties. Since 2023, it has documented over 5,300 — a rate of more than four per day, with over 1,100 Palestinians killed. In the first seven months of 2026 alone, there were at least 1,380 settler attacks. Demolitions and intimidation displaced 117 Palestinian communities, uprooting over 5,900 people.

The maps show that Palestinians now control approximately 8% of the land between the Jordan River and the Mediterranean Sea, while Israel fully controls about 92%. Lowenstein asks: "Ninety-two per cent of the land for fifty per cent of the people — how is that sustainable?" The conversation, he argues, has shifted from "What can we do to get to a two-state solution?" to "What can be done to stop Israel from literally taking all of the land?" The Israeli Knesset passed a nonbinding resolution in 2025 declaring Israel's "natural, historical and legal right" to the West Bank and Gaza, and Netanyahu now campaigns on never allowing a Palestinian state.
