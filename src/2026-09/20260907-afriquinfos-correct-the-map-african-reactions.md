## Synthesis
The President of the African Union Commission, Mahmoud Ali Youssouf, welcomed the adoption by the UN General Assembly of the "Correct the Map" resolution, carried by Togo on behalf of the African Group in favor of a more just and faithful cartographic representation of Africa and other world regions.

He particularly saluted the leadership of the Republic of Togo and the commitment of President Faure Essozimna Gnassingbé, under whose impulsion Togo carried this initiative on behalf of Africa and contributed to mobilizing broad support within the international community. He also congratulated the African Union member states and the African Group in New York for their unity and mobilization around the initiative.

The AU Commission's statement noted that the Mercator projection, conceived in the 16th century for navigation needs, does not faithfully render the relative surface areas of different world regions and gives a "considerably reduced visual representation of the real size of the African continent." Beyond cartography, the initiative recalls the importance of representing Africa on the basis of objective and scientifically established realities.

The Commission encourages international institutions, education systems, publishers, and digital actors to progressively favor the use of cartographic representations more faithful to actual surface areas. "Africa must be represented as it is: in its true dimensions and in its rightful place in the world," the AU stated.

Togo's diplomacy had earlier hailed the adoption of the text as "a victory for accuracy" and announced its intention to concentrate efforts on the concrete implementation of the resolution. The AU Commission affirmed it will continue collaboration with Togo, member states, and concerned partners to accompany implementation efforts.
