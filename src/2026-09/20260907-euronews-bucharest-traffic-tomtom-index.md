## Synthesis
Bucharest's traffic congestion remains a persistent problem, with the TomTom Traffic Index 2025 ranking Romania's capital 20th globally among the most congested cities. Drivers lose an average of over 171 hours per year in traffic — more than seven full days — with a 10-kilometer journey taking over 32 minutes on average and exceeding 45 minutes during evening rush hour.

The average speed during congested periods drops to approximately 14 km/h, making driving in the city comparable to cycling speed. The situation worsened compared to 2024: travel times for 10-kilometer trips increased by 1 minute and 11 seconds, while average rush hour speed decreased by approximately 1 km/h.

The data reveals significant daily variation. According to TomTom, October 7, 2025 — a Tuesday — was the most congested day in Bucharest, with congestion levels reaching 106%. At 5 PM on that day, congestion climbed to 176%, meaning drivers could cover only 2.6 kilometers in 15 minutes. The return of students to school adds further pressure to the capital's already strained streets.

TomTom's Traffic Index data is built from anonymized GPS information and actual speeds recorded on the road network, providing a comprehensive picture of urban congestion patterns. The index serves as a key reference for urban planners and mobility policymakers evaluating traffic management strategies.
