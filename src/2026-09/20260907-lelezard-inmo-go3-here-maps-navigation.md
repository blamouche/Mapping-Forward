# INMO GO3 AI Glasses Win IFA Innovation Award with HERE Maps Navigation

**Source**: https://www.lelezard.com/communique-22356185.html
**Date**: 2026-09-07
**Author**: Le Lézard
**Keywords**: INMO GO3, AI glasses, HERE Maps, navigation, IFA Innovation Award, wearable technology, hands-free navigation, pedestrian navigation, cycling navigation

## Elevator pitch
The INMO GO3 lightweight AI glasses won the IFA Innovation Award, integrating HERE Maps for hands-free pedestrian and cycling navigation with step-by-step directions displayed directly in the glasses.

## Takeaways
- INMO GO3 AI glasses won the IFA Innovation Award for innovations in the wearable technology domain
- The GO3 integrate HERE Maps for pedestrian and cycling navigation in hands-free mode
- Navigation directions are displayed step-by-step directly in the glasses' display
- HERE Maps provides the mapping and routing backbone for the glasses' navigation features
- The glasses are positioned as lightweight AI eyewear designed for everyday use
- The award was announced at IFA, one of the world's leading consumer electronics trade shows

## Synthesis
The INMO GO3 AI glasses have received the IFA Innovation Award for their innovations in wearable technology. The GO3 model integrates HERE Maps for hands-free pedestrian and cycling navigation, displaying step-by-step directions directly in the glasses' built-in display.

The integration of HERE Maps as the navigation provider marks a notable deployment of the mapping platform in the wearable technology space. While Google Maps and Apple Maps dominate smartphone navigation, HERE Maps has found a niche in embedded and wearable applications where hands-free operation is essential. The GO3 glasses leverage this positioning by offering navigation without the need to look at a phone screen, making it particularly useful for walking and cycling scenarios where attention to surroundings is critical.

The IFA Innovation Award recognition highlights the growing intersection of AI-powered wearable devices and location-based services. The glasses represent a category of lightweight AI eyewear designed for everyday use, where mapping and navigation services serve as core differentiating features. HERE Maps' selection as the navigation backbone reflects the platform's strength in providing routing and mapping APIs for embedded applications beyond traditional smartphone navigation.
