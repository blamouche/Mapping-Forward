# Movie Scene Map: A Free Interactive Map of 16,000 Film and Series Locations Worldwide

**Source**: https://parissecret.com/carte-interactive-tournages/
**Date**: 2026-09-07
**Author**: Paris Secret
**Keywords**: interactive map, film locations, Movie Scene Map, cinema, tourism, Paris, cultural mapping, data visualization, location-based

## Elevator pitch
Movie Scene Map is a free interactive map listing nearly 16,000 real filming locations across 166 countries, letting users discover which films and series were shot near their home, with a particularly rich collection of Parisian locations.

## Takeaways
- Movie Scene Map catalogs nearly 16,000 real filming locations across 166 countries
- The platform covers over 10,000 films and series, plus video games, anime, and manga locations
- Paris is among the most represented cities, with locations from Mission: Impossible – Fallout, Emily in Paris, and other productions
- France has nearly 1,000 listed filming locations including châteaux, beaches, villages, stations, and streets
- Users can search by location by zooming on the map, or by film title to find all shooting locations
- The map includes fictional locations from anime and manga rather than physical filming sites

## Synthesis
Movie Scene Map is a free interactive mapping platform that catalogs nearly 16,000 real-world filming locations across 166 countries. The tool allows users to explore their neighborhood or city and discover which films and series were shot on their streets, from simple roads to châteaux, studios, monuments, and natural landscapes.

Paris figures prominently among the most represented cities on the platform. Fans of Mission: Impossible – Fallout can locate the Grand Palais, Palais-Royal, Canal Saint-Martin, Pont-Neuf, and several streets used during the film's Parisian shoot with Tom Cruise. Emily in Paris locations appear around the Place du Panthéon and La Samaritaine. France as a whole has nearly 1,000 listed locations, transforming the map into a tool for planning walks in the footsteps of cinema.

The platform extends beyond live-action filming. Its database of over 10,000 titles includes video games, anime, and manga. For these animated works, the map indicates the real-world locations where the stories are set rather than physical filming locations, since they were not filmed on location.

Users can navigate by zooming into a specific area and selecting points to see which productions were filmed there, or search by film title to find all locations used during a production's shoot.
