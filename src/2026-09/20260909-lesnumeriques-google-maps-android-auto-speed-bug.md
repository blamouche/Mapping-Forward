# Google Maps Loses Speedometer and Speed Limits on Android Auto Just Weeks After Launch
**Source**: https://www.lesnumeriques.com/gps/a-peine-lancee-cet-ete-google-maps-perd-l-une-de-ses-meilleures-fonctionnalites-sur-android-auto-n261582.html
**Date**: 2026-09-09
**Author**: Les Numériques
**Keywords**: Google Maps, Android Auto, speedometer, speed limits, bug, CarPlay, Waze

## Elevator pitch
A bug on Android Auto causes Google Maps' newly launched speedometer and speed limit display to disappear, along with traffic lights and stop signs, while everything works fine on Apple CarPlay.

## Takeaways
- Google Maps' real-time speedometer, deployed on Android Auto in July 2026, has disappeared for many users due to a display bug
- The bug also removes speed limit indicators, and in some cases traffic lights, stop signs, and traffic lines
- Apple CarPlay remains unaffected — the speedometer and all indicators continue working normally
- Some users recovered the feature by leaving the Google Maps and Android Auto beta channels and reinstalling stable versions, but this workaround is inconsistent
- Google has not issued an official fix or statement; Waze is also experiencing display glitches

## Synthesis
Just weeks after Google Maps finally brought its real-time speedometer to Android Auto in July 2026 — filling a long-standing gap compared to Apple CarPlay, which had the feature since summer 2024 — the feature has vanished for numerous users. The speedometer, along with speed limit indicators, traffic lights, stop signs, and traffic lines, has disappeared from the Android Auto interface, according to widespread reports on Reddit.

The timing is particularly frustrating for Android Auto users who had waited years for feature parity with CarPlay. The speedometer and speed limits were deployed just weeks ago, offering drivers the essential information needed to monitor their speed without taking their eyes off the road. Now, the display has regressed to a pre-July state. Meanwhile, Apple CarPlay users report no issues — all indicators remain functional on the iOS side.

The bug appears to be a temporary display issue rather than a deliberate removal, but Google has not yet responded officially. Some users on Reddit have found that leaving the beta channels for Google Maps and Android Auto, then reinstalling stable versions, restored the functionality — though this workaround doesn't work for everyone. Waze, Google's other navigation app, is also experiencing display glitches, suggesting a broader issue with Google's mapping display pipeline on Android Auto.
