# Xpeng Integrates Google Maps Into Its Embedded Navigation
**Source**: https://revueautomobile.ch/technique/xpeng-integre-google-maps-dans-sa-navigation-embarquee
**Date**: 2026-09-10
**Author**: automobil revue
**Keywords**: Xpeng, Google Maps, embedded navigation, cartographic data, automotive, EV, China, navigation

## Elevator pitch
Chinese automaker Xpeng has integrated Google Maps cartographic data and navigation capabilities directly into its embedded vehicle navigation system — not as an app, but as a native integration of Google's mapping data.

## Elevator pitch
Chinese EV manufacturer Xpeng has integrated Google Maps directly into its embedded navigation system, becoming the first Chinese automaker to do so — but not as a standalone app, rather by incorporating Google's cartographic data and navigational APIs natively.

## Takeaways
- Google Maps data arrives natively in Xpeng vehicles, not as a standalone application
- Xpeng integrates Google's cartographic data and navigational APIs for the first time
- This makes Xpeng the first Chinese automaker to natively integrate Google Maps data
- The integration provides real-time cartographic data and navigation features
- Previous coverage noted Xpeng's integration of Google Maps Auto SDK in August 2026

## Synthesis
Xpeng's integration of Google Maps into its embedded navigation represents a significant move for both the automotive and mapping industries. Rather than offering Google Maps as a projection or app within the car's infotainment system, Xpeng has incorporated Google's cartographic data and navigation APIs directly into its native navigation platform. This approach gives drivers Google's mapping quality without requiring a smartphone connection.

For Google, this partnership expands its Maps platform into the Chinese automotive market through a backdoor approach — by providing data and APIs rather than a consumer-facing app. For Xpeng, it offers a competitive differentiator in the crowded Chinese EV market, where navigation quality increasingly matters to consumers. This follows the earlier August 2026 report of Xpeng integrating the Google Maps Auto SDK, suggesting a deepening technology relationship.
