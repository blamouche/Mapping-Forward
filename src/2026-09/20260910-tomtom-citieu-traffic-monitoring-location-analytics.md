# TomTom and citiEU Strengthen Ties to Deliver Advanced Traffic Monitoring and Location Analytics
**Source**: https://www.tomtom.com/newsroom/press-releases/general/218203076020215/tomtom-and-citieu-strengthen-ties-to-deliver-advanced-traffic-monitoring-and-location-analytics-solutions/
**Date**: 2026-09-10
**Author**: TomTom
**Keywords**: TomTom, citiEU, traffic monitoring, location analytics, location intelligence, partnership, Europe, mobility

## Elevator pitch
TomTom announced an expanded partnership with citiEU to deliver advanced location analytics, traffic intelligence, and mobility solutions across Europe and the Middle East, leveraging TomTom's full product portfolio including Traffic Analytics.

## Takeaways
- citiEU will leverage TomTom's location intelligence to support advanced location analytics, traffic intelligence, and mobility solutions
- The expanded agreement covers clients in Europe and the Middle East
- citiEU will use a broad portfolio of TomTom products including Traffic Analytics, traffic volumes, and routing data
- The partnership was announced September 10, 2026 from Amsterdam
- TomTom positions itself as "the specialist in mapping and location technology"
- Multiple financial and trade outlets covered the announcement (Yahoo Finance, Zonebourse, TradingView, Barchart)

## Synthesis
TomTom's expanded partnership with citiEU reinforces the company's strategic pivot toward providing location intelligence as a B2B service rather than competing directly in the consumer navigation space. Under the renewed agreement, citiEU will deploy TomTom's Traffic Analytics and related products to serve clients across Europe and the Middle East, offering traffic, location, and mobility insights.

The announcement was widely picked up by financial media, suggesting market significance. The partnership covers both public sector (traffic monitoring) and private sector (mobility solutions) use cases. This positions TomTom's data assets — particularly real-time traffic information and historical traffic patterns — as infrastructure for other mobility platforms rather than just features of its own navigation apps. The expansion follows TomTom's broader strategy of opening up its Orbis platform and partnering across the mobility ecosystem.
