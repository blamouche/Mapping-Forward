# TomTom AmiGo Becomes New Map Option for Android Auto
**Source**: https://www.estadao.com.br/tecmundo/produto-e-review/215394-tomtom-amiggo-nova-opcao-mapa-android-auto/
**Date**: 2026-09-11
**Author**: Luiz Paulo Charleaux (Estadão)
**Keywords**: TomTom AmiGo, Android Auto, Google Maps, Waze, navigation apps, connected cars, GPS, speed cameras, real-time alerts

## Elevator pitch
TomTom AmiGo has launched as a new navigation option for Android Auto, offering a free, ad-free alternative to Google Maps and Waze with community-powered speed camera alerts, real-time traffic, and a cleaner interface.

## Takeaways
- TomTom AmiGo is now available as a navigation app for Android Auto, offering an alternative to Google Maps and Waze
- The app provides community-powered real-time alerts for fixed and mobile speed cameras, accidents, roadblocks, and speed limits
- TomTom AmiGo is completely free and ad-free, unlike TomTom's premium subscription services
- The app features a cleaner, more minimalist interface than its competitors, showing only essential driving information
- TomTom AmiGo has been available in Brazil since 2017 as a standalone mobile app
- The expansion to Android Auto follows Sygic as the first third-party navigation app on Google's car platform

## Synthesis
TomTom has brought its AmiGo navigation app to Android Auto, expanding the ecosystem of in-car navigation options beyond Google Maps and Waze. The app combines the community-driven alert model popularized by Waze — real-time speed camera warnings, accident reports, and road hazard alerts — with a cleaner, more minimalist interface that displays only essential driving information.

TomTom AmiGo is notable for being completely free and ad-free, a contrast to TomTom's premium GO Navigation subscription service. The app has been available as a standalone mobile app in Brazil since 2017, and its expansion to Android Auto follows Google's decision to open the platform to third-party navigation developers. Sygic was the first third-party navigation app to arrive on Android Auto, and TomTom AmiGo's arrival signals growing diversification in the connected-car navigation market. The development is significant for TomTom's consumer strategy, as the company increasingly positions itself as a mapping data and platform provider (through Orbis Maps) while maintaining a consumer-facing presence through AmiGo.
