# Google Explains the Disappearing Speedometer in Maps on Android Auto
**Source**: https://www.androidauthority.com/android-auto-maps-speedometer-3710468/
**Date**: 2026-09-12
**Author**: Stephen Schenck (Android Authority)
**Keywords**: Google Maps, Android Auto, speedometer, speed limit, traffic signals, Google, navigation, rollout, Mishaal Rahman

## Elevator pitch
Google has explained that the disappearing speedometer in Google Maps on Android Auto is not a bug but a rollout issue: the ability to view the speedometer, speed limit, and traffic signs without a destination set is still rolling out gradually, and users need to set a destination for the features to appear in the meantime.

## Takeaways
- Google Maps on Android Auto gained a live speedometer feature earlier in 2026, but users recently reported it had vanished
- Google confirmed the speedometer rollout is proceeding normally — the issue is that viewing it without a destination set is a separate feature still rolling out
- Users need to set a destination for the speedometer, speed limit, and traffic signals/stop signs to appear
- Android Authority alum and current Googler Mishaal Rahman confirmed the explanation after speaking with the Android Auto product team
- The "disappearances" were not a bug but rather the absence of a not-yet-fully-deployed feature for destination-free display

## Synthesis
After reports surfaced that the Google Maps speedometer on Android Auto had disappeared for some users, Google has provided an explanation that clears up the confusion. The speedometer feature, which began rolling out in summer 2026, is functioning normally — but the ability to view it (along with speed limits and traffic sign displays) without a destination programmed is a separate capability that is still being gradually deployed.

Mishaal Rahman, a former Android Authority journalist now at Google, confirmed after speaking with the Android Auto product team that the destination-free display of these features has not yet reached all users. This explains why some users saw the speedometer vanish: they were among those who had not yet received the persistent display feature. The solution in the interim is simple — set a destination before driving, and the speedometer and related features will appear. Google expects the destination-free mode to roll out to all users in the near future.
