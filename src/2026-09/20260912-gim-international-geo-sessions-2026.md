## Elevator pitch
NV5's Geo Sessions 2026 virtual event highlights a fundamental shift in the geospatial industry: the challenge is no longer collecting data but turning it into operational intelligence, with AI connecting multiple remote sensing technologies into seamless decision-support workflows.

- NV5's Geo Sessions 2026 is a global virtual event focused on the practical application of geospatial technologies, attracting several thousand registrants from 100+ countries
- The industry has "largely solved the collection problem" — commercial satellites, drones, and airborne sensors generate unprecedented amounts of data

## Synthesis
NV5's Geo Sessions 2026 reflects a turning point in the geospatial industry. As Dan Gruidel, VP of Strategy & Business Development at NV5, explains, the challenge has shifted from data collection to operational intelligence. Commercial satellites capture the Earth daily, airborne lidar maps entire regions in remarkable detail, and SAR delivers reliable observations regardless of weather — but the volume of data now exceeds the ability of decision-makers to act on it.

The event emphasizes connecting individual technologies — lidar, SAR, spectral imagery, GIS, AI — into integrated workflows that move from collection to analysis to decision support and ultimately to action. AI plays a central role in this transformation, not by replacing remote sensing but by automating workflows and surfacing insights that previously required significant manual effort. With dedicated sessions on SAR, spectral imaging, lidar, and a new GeoAI track for 2026, Geo Sessions brings together experts from industry, government, and academia to discuss how geospatial technologies are solving real-world problems across transportation, utilities, defense, environmental management, agriculture, and emergency response.
