# Google Maps Rolling Out Always-On Speed Limits and Speedometer on Android Auto
**Source**: https://9to5google.com/2026/09/14/google-maps-android-auto-speed-limits-speedometer-update/
**Date**: 2026-09-14
**Author**: 9to5Google
**Keywords**: Google Maps, Android Auto, speed limits, speedometer, navigation, always-on, update, traffic signals

## Elevator pitch
Google Maps is rolling out speed limits, a live speedometer, and traffic signals/stop signs on Android Auto that display even when no destination is set, while also investigating a recent bug that caused the speedometer to disappear for some users.

## Takeaways
- Google Maps is rolling out always-on speed limits and speedometer on Android Auto, even without a destination set
- The feature includes traffic signals and stop signs display without active navigation
- The rollout is gradual and not yet available to all users
- A recent bug caused the speedometer to vanish for some Android Auto users, which is being investigated
- Speed limits in Google Maps originally launched in 2019, with the speedometer appearing later
- The feature was previously only visible during active navigation
- The update makes Google Maps more useful for shorter or familiar trips where setting a destination isn't necessary

## Synthesis
Google Maps is rolling out a significant update for Android Auto users: speed limits, a live speedometer, and traffic signals/stop signs will now display even when no destination is set. This "always-on" mode makes the features available during short or familiar trips where drivers don't need turn-by-turn navigation but still want speed awareness.

The rollout is gradual, and not all users will have access immediately. Speed limits originally launched in Google Maps back in 2019, with the live speedometer appearing later and only starting to show up on Android Auto a few months ago. Previously, these features were only visible during active navigation with a set destination.

The update comes alongside an ongoing investigation into a bug that caused the speedometer to disappear for some Android Auto users earlier in September 2026. The Android Auto team has confirmed they are looking into the issue. The new always-on functionality addresses a common use case where drivers use Google Maps on their car display for speed awareness even on familiar routes.
