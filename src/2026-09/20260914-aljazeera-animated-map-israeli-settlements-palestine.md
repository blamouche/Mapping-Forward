## Elevator pitch
Al Jazeera has published an animated map visualizing the spread of illegal Israeli settlements and outposts across Palestine, showing 163 settlements housing 744,700 settlers alongside more than 400 unauthorized outposts by 2024.

## Synthesis
Al Jazeera has published an animated map that visualizes the progressive spread of illegal Israeli settlements and outposts across Palestine. The visual explainer tracks how settlements have expanded geographically over time, providing a cartographic record of territorial changes on the ground.

By 2024, the map shows that 163 illegal settlements were home to 744,700 Israeli settlers — 511,100 in the West Bank and 233,600 in East Jerusalem. Alongside these officially recognized settlements, a record number of more than 400 illegal outposts have been established. These outposts are not officially counted by Israeli authorities but are often absorbed into recognized settlements over time.

The animated map also documents the settler pogroms and violence accompanying the settlement expansion, providing a comprehensive visual account of how the settlement enterprise has reshaped the geographic and demographic reality of Palestine.
