# Wildfire Map Spotlight: Brac Island Fire, Croatia
**Source**: https://www.iqair.com/ca-fr/newsroom/wildfire-map-spotlight-brac-island-wildfire-croatia
**Date**: 2026-09-14
**Author**: IQAir Staff Writers
**Keywords**: wildfire map, Brac Island, Croatia, satellite imagery, Copernicus, evacuation, bora winds, fire mapping

## Elevator pitch
A major wildfire on Croatia's largest Adriatic island, Brac, has burned over 5,600 hectares — nearly 14% of the island's total area — forcing the evacuation of nearly 700 people, with satellite imagery from Copernicus tracking the fire's spread.

## Takeaways
- A wildfire broke out on September 10, 2026, near the village of Ložišće on Brac Island, Croatia
- The fire has burned more than 5,600 hectares, affecting nearly 14% of the island's total area
- Nearly 700 people have been evacuated, including 230 by sea near Milna
- 220 firefighters, 69 fire vehicles, and four Canadair water-bombing aircraft have been deployed
- Strong bora winds have driven the fire's rapid spread toward the southern coast
- Satellite imagery from the EU's Copernicus program has been used to monitor the fire's progression
- The fire spread toward the Blaca desert area and the island's southern coast

## Synthesis
A major wildfire is burning on Brac, Croatia's largest Adriatic island, since September 10, 2026. The fire broke out near the village of Ložišće and spread rapidly under strong bora winds, expanding toward the island's southern coast and the Blaca desert area. As of September 14, the fire has burned more than 5,600 hectares — nearly 14% of Brac's total area.

The response involves 220 firefighters, 69 fire vehicles, and four Canadair water-bombing aircraft. Nearly 700 people have been evacuated from threatened areas, including approximately 230 people evacuated by sea near Milna using vessels from the harbour master's office, maritime police, and private boats. The national firefighting command reported significant improvement by Monday morning following an extremely difficult night, with firefighters successfully protecting the town of Milna.

Satellite imagery from the EU's Copernicus Emergency Management Service has been critical in tracking the fire's spread, showing continued expansion toward the southern coast despite firefighting efforts. The fire represents one of the most significant wildfires in Croatia's Adriatic islands in recent years.
