## Synthesis
Matrix Geo Solutions Limited, an NSE-listed geospatial technology and engineering consultancy, has secured a three-year Annual Rate Contract (ARC) from Adani Infra for geospatial, LiDAR, surveying, mapping, and allied technology-enabled services. The contract covers a comprehensive range of geospatial capabilities including GIS, digital terrain and surface modelling, infrastructure mapping, and asset intelligence across pan-India project locations.

The engagement brings together Matrix Geo's expertise in drone-based precision surveying with Adani Infra's large-scale infrastructure projects. The contract underscores the growing demand for geospatial and drone surveying services in India's infrastructure sector, where accurate mapping and terrain modelling are critical for project planning and execution.

Matrix Geo Solutions has been expanding its geospatial capabilities, having recently completed a 314 km geospatial survey across five highway sections for RITES-NHAI and reported strong project execution in Q1 FY27 with new orders worth ₹6.41 crore. The three-year ARC with Adani Infra represents a significant milestone in the company's growth trajectory in the geospatial services market.
