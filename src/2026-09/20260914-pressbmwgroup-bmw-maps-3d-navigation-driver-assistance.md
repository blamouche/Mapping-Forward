# BMW Maps Displays Navigation and Driver Assistance Information in a Single 3D View
**Source**: https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/global/article/detail/T0460275EN/bmw-maps-displays-navigation-and-driver-assistance-information-in-a-single-3d-view
**Date**: 2026-09-14
**Author**: BMW Group PressClub
**Keywords**: BMW Maps, 3D navigation, driver assistance, Mapbox, Operating System X, semi-automated driving, Highway Assistant, connected vehicles

## Elevator pitch
Starting October 2026, BMW Maps will unify route guidance, surrounding perception, and driver assistance information into a single 3D view on the central display, helping drivers understand both navigation and assisted driving functions in complex traffic situations.

## Takeaways
- BMW Maps with driver assistance launches in October 2026 for vehicles equipped with BMW Operating System X
- The 3D view combines navigation, vehicle surroundings, and driver assistance information in one display
- The system shows the vehicle precisely within its lane and at the correct road level
- Highway Assistant 2 enables hands-free driving at up to 130 km/h with automated lane changes via glance confirmation
- The technology was developed by BMW Group, combining precise map data, onboard sensors, and AI, with Mapbox mapping technology support
- Starting in 2027, navigation-guided semi-automated driving will roll out in urban areas, initially in Germany
- The feature covers the entire highway network and the city of Paris at launch, with gradual expansion to other cities

## Synthesis
BMW Group is introducing a significant evolution in automotive navigation with BMW Maps with driver assistance, launching in October 2026. The system merges route guidance, real-time vehicle surroundings, and driver assistance information into a single 3D view on the central display. This allows drivers to see not only the next navigation step but also how the vehicle perceives its environment and which assistance functions are supporting the driving task.

The display positions the driver's vehicle precisely within its lane and at the correct road level, aiding navigation on highways, at intersections, and in city traffic. This integration is particularly relevant for driver-assistance systems that support driving over long distances or in complex traffic. The Highway Assistant 2 enables hands-free highway driving at speeds up to 130 km/h, controlling both longitudinal and lateral movement, with automated lane changes triggered by a driver's glance confirmation.

The technology was developed in-house by BMW Group, combining precise cartographic data, onboard sensors, and artificial intelligence, with mapping technology support from Mapbox. At launch, the feature covers the entire highway network plus the city of Paris, with gradual expansion planned. Starting in 2027, BMW models with Operating System X will begin rolling out navigation-guided semi-automated driving in urban areas, initially in Germany.
