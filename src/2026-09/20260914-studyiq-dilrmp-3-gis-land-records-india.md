## Synthesis
The Digital India Land Records Modernisation Programme (DILRMP) 3.0, covering 2026–2031, marks the third phase of India's ambitious land-records modernisation effort. Launched through the Department of Land Resources (DoLR), the programme aims to create an integrated, GIS-enabled, and interoperable land administration system.

A cornerstone of DILRMP 3.0 is Bhu-Aadhaar (ULPIN), which provides every geo-referenced land parcel with a 14-digit Unique Land Parcel Identification Number. This system creates a standardized, geospatially referenced identity for each land parcel, enabling transparent and efficient land administration. GIS and cadastral maps form the backbone of the programme, ensuring that land records are spatially accurate and digitally accessible.

The programme also includes the NAKSHA scheme for land survey modernisation, paperless registration capabilities, and integration with revenue courts. DILRMP 3.0 represents a significant shift from merely digitising land records to creating a comprehensive digital land-governance ecosystem. By combining GIS, unique parcel identification, online registration, and judicial integration, the programme aims to improve transparency, reduce land disputes, and enhance both the Ease of Living and Ease of Doing Business in India.
