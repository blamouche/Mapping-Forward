# Cameroon Wraps Up Population Census After Two Deadline Extensions, Using Satellite Mapping

**Author**: Cameroon Tribune
**Keywords**: Cameroon, population census, satellite mapping, GIS, BUCREP, RGPH-4, GPS tablets, rural settlements, data collection

## Elevator pitch
Cameroon completed its 4th General Population and Housing Census after two deadline extensions, deploying 33,000 enumerators with GPS-enabled tablets and high-resolution satellite mapping to locate unrecorded rural settlements — preliminary results expected in five months.

## Takeaways
- Cameroon's RGPH-4 census ran from April 24 to September 15, 2026, extended twice by the Prime Minister due to operational hurdles
- Over 33,000 enumerators deployed with encrypted, GPS-enabled mobile tablets for real-time data transmission

- The census was conducted jointly with the General Census of Agriculture and Livestock (RGAE) — a first for Cameroon

## Synthesis
Cameroon has completed the physical data collection phase of its 4th General Population and Housing Census (RGPH-4), which ran from April 24 to September 15, 2026. The operation, conducted by the Central Bureau for Censuses and Population Studies (BUCREP) under MINEPAT, was for the first time coupled with the General Census of Agriculture and Livestock (RGAE) — marking a significant expansion in scope compared to the standalone 2005 census.

The census faced operational challenges including delayed per-diem disbursements, logistical friction, reluctant urban household participation, and access constraints in remote and security-sensitive areas. Prime Minister Joseph Dion Ngute signed two extensions: the first through July 31 (during which high-resolution satellite mapping was incorporated to locate unrecorded rural settlements), and a second 46-day extension through September 15 focusing on uncounted urban households and complex agricultural data collection.

Technologically, RGPH-4 represents a major evolution. The 2005 census relied on paper questionnaires requiring manual processing, resulting in a publication delay of over four years. The 2026 census adopted a fully digital Computer-Assisted Personal Interviewing (CAPI) model, deploying over 33,000 enumerators equipped with encrypted, GPS-enabled mobile tablets for direct real-time data transmission. BUCREP also operated a toll-free helpline (8585) for household inquiries and agent verification.

The last official census recorded Cameroon's population at 17,463,836 in 2005. Pre-census projections estimated over 28.8 million. BUCREP now moves into the Post-Enumeration Survey and digital data aggregation phase, with preliminary results targeted within five months.
