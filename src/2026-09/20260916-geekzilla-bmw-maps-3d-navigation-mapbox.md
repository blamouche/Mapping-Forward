## Synthesis
BMW has presented a major update for BMW Maps that will arrive starting October 2026 in vehicles equipped with BMW Operating System X. The new version combines three-dimensional navigation with driver assistance systems to display the route, surrounding environment, and exact vehicle position on the road in a single screen view.

Developed in collaboration with Mapbox, the update shows in real time the lane the vehicle is traveling in, the road level, and the nearby environment through 3D graphics. Navigation information and driving assistants work together to provide a clearer representation on highways and complex intersections. The integration allows the vehicle to more precisely represent the correct lane, road elevation changes, and other relevant driving elements.

BMW also confirmed the evolution of Highway Assistant, a system that can assist driving on compatible highways at speeds up to 130 km/h. Depending on market and vehicle, it also supports automatic lane changes after visual confirmation from the driver. As part of this evolution, BMW indicated that some models will incorporate new semi-automated urban driving assistance features starting in 2027, beginning in specific markets where regulation permits.

The combination of 3D maps and driver assistance systems aims to make the road easier to read and reduce the information load on the driver. However, BMW has not yet confirmed when these features will reach Mexico or other Latin American markets, with availability dependent on both vehicle hardware and local regulatory frameworks.
