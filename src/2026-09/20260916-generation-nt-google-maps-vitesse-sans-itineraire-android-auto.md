# Google Maps affiche enfin la vitesse sans itinéraire actif sur l'écran
**Source**: https://www.generation-nt.com/actualites/google-maps-vitesse-sans-itineraire-2081213
**Date**: 2026-09-16
**Author**: Mathieu M. — Generation-NT
**Keywords**: Google Maps, Android Auto, speedometer, speed limits, navigation, car display

## Elevator pitch
Google deploys a server-side update enabling permanent display of speedometer, speed limits, and stop signs on Android Auto — even without an active navigation route — closing a long-standing gap with Apple CarPlay.

## Takeaways
- Google Maps now displays real-time speed, speed limits, and stop signs permanently on Android Auto screens without requiring an active route
- The update is rolling out progressively via server-side flags; users need the latest app version but no manual action
- A recent display bug temporarily removed the speedometer for some users after cache clearing; Google confirmed it was isolated and a fix is being distributed
- Apple CarPlay already had this persistent speed display, highlighting a feature gap Google has now closed

## Synthesis
Google has begun globally deploying a persistent display of speed indicators on Android Auto, allowing drivers to see their current speed, applicable speed limits, and stop signs without needing to launch a navigation route. This addresses a long-standing frustration where the speedometer was only visible during active turn-by-turn navigation — an absurd constraint for routine, familiar trips where drivers don't need directions but still want speed information on their dashboard screen.

The activation is transparent: the rollout occurs progressively through Google's servers. Users with the latest Android Auto version will see the change automatically. However, the asynchronous deployment means some users may not yet have access. The update also corrected a recent bug where the speedometer disappeared entirely for some users after clearing the app cache — Google confirmed this was an isolated display glitch, not an intentional removal, and a fix is being distributed.

The update underscores a surprising lag in Google's execution: Apple CarPlay had already integrated persistent speed display in Google Maps, leaving Android Auto users waiting. The feature transforms the daily driving experience by showing speed information the moment the app opens on the car's central screen, without any dangerous manual interaction required.
