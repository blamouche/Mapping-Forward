## Synthesis
Aerial Surveys International (ASIntl), a Watkins, Colorado-based provider of high-resolution digital aerial imagery, LiDAR data, and geospatial analytics, has been awarded a Multiple Award Schedule (MAS) contract by the U.S. General Services Administration (GSA). The award (contract number 47QTCA26D008Q) enables federal, state, local, and tribal government agencies to procure GIS and Earth observation services directly from ASIntl as a prime contractor — a significant expansion from the company's nearly three-decade role as a trusted subcontractor.

The GSA Schedule covers two Special Item Numbers: SIN 541370GIS for GIS services (geospatial analysis, spatial modeling, mapping, cartography, environmental GIS, mitigation planning, emergency response GIS, data visualization) and SIN 541370GEO for Earth observation solutions (aerial imagery including electro-optical, SAR, hyperspectral, LiDAR, thermal, UAS; geospatial analytics; change detection; persistent monitoring; environmental monitoring; disaster response/recovery). This places ASIntl among a select group of contractors authorized to deliver both categories under a single GSA vehicle.

The award extends beyond ASIntl itself: the company maintains formal teaming agreements with 27 partner organizations (7 large businesses, 20 small businesses including SDB, WOSB, HUBZone, and DVOB designations), representing nearly 4,000 professionals. The GSA prime position gives this entire partner network a direct path into federal task orders. The award also strengthens the TrinityGEO geospatial intelligence framework, which combines ASIntl's aerial imagery and LiDAR collection with Polygon Solutions' 3D modeling and Urban Hawk's AI-powered change-detection capabilities.
