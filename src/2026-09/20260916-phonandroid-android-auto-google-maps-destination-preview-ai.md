# Android Auto: Better Destination Preview Coming to Google Maps with Photos and AI Summaries
**Source**: https://www.phonandroid.com/android-auto-un-meilleur-apercu-de-la-destination-sera-affiche-sur-google-maps.html
**Date**: 2026-09-16
**Author**: Alexandre Schmid — PhonAndroid
**Keywords**: Android Auto, Google Maps, destination preview, Gemini AI, photos, UI redesign, navigation

## Elevator pitch
Android Auto version 17.8 brings a redesigned Google Maps destination preview with photos, Gemini AI-generated summaries, and a reworked interface that moves trip time and distance to a bottom bar.

## Takeaways
- Android Auto 17.8 update adds photos and AI-generated summaries to Google Maps destination previews, a feature previously only available on smartphones
- Google redesigned the entire UI: trip time and distance now display in a bottom horizontal bar that can be expanded for alternate routes and stops
- Destination photos can be viewed in a dedicated gallery, eliminating the need to check the phone
- A Gemini-generated summary helps drivers confirm the correct destination and anticipate what to expect before arriving
- Features are ready but not yet officially deployed by Google; changes may occur before general availability

## Synthesis
Google Maps via Android Auto is getting a significant upgrade with version 17.8, bringing destination preview capabilities that were previously limited to smartphones. When a user enters a destination, the updated interface now shows photos of the location alongside a Gemini AI-generated summary, helping drivers confirm they've selected the right place and anticipate what to expect upon arrival.

To accommodate these new elements, Google reworked the entire Google Maps interface on Android Auto. Trip time and estimated distance now appear in a bottom horizontal bar, which can be swiped upward to expand into a larger panel showing alternate routes and stops. Destination photos can be displayed in a full-size gallery, and the AI summary — a shortened version of a Gemini-generated description — can be tapped to reveal the full text.

These features have been discovered in the APK teardown by Android Authority and appear ready for release, but Google has not yet officially deployed them. The company may make changes before general availability. The update represents a broader effort to bring smartphone-grade richness to the car display experience while keeping driver distraction to a minimum.
