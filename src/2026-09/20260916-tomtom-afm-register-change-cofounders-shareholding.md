# TomTom Clarifies AFM Register Change Relating to Co-Founders' Shareholding
**Source**: https://www.tomtom.com/newsroom/press-releases/general/29626/tomtom-clarifies-afm-register-change-relating-to-co-founders-shareholding/
**Date**: 2026-09-16
**Author**: TomTom
**Keywords**: TomTom, AFM, shareholding, co-founders, Harold Goddijn, Corinne Vigreux, restructuring, corporate

## Elevator pitch
TomTom confirmed that a change in the Dutch AFM substantial holdings register regarding co-founders Harold Goddijn and Corinne Vigreux is merely an internal restructuring of how their shares are held — their combined 25.6% position remains unchanged.

## Takeaways
- TomTom co-founders Harold Goddijn and Corinne Vigreux restructured how their TomTom shares are held, triggering an AFM register update
- Their total combined position remains 31,971,749 shares, representing 25.6% of total shares — no change in actual shareholding
- The restructuring was reported to the Dutch Authority for the Financial Markets (AFM) in accordance with regulations
- Both co-founders reaffirmed their commitment to TomTom's strategy, technology, and people

## Synthesis
TomTom issued a clarification on September 16, 2026, confirming that a change reflected in the AFM (Netherlands Authority for the Financial Markets) substantial holdings register relating to co-founders Harold Goddijn and Corinne Vigreux is the result of an internal restructuring of the way their TomTom shares are held. The restructuring does not represent any change in their combined shareholding position.

The co-founders' total combined position remains at 31,971,749 shares, representing 25.6% of TomTom's total shares. The related notifications were made in accordance with applicable regulations and reported to the AFM where required. Both Goddijn and Vigreux issued a joint statement emphasizing that the restructuring relates only to the structure of their shareholding and reaffirming their full commitment to TomTom's strategy, technology, and people.

TomTom, headquartered in Amsterdam with 3,300 employees worldwide, has been shaping the future of mobility for over 30 years as a location technology specialist providing maps, routing, real-time traffic, APIs, and SDKs to drivers, carmakers, businesses, and developers.
