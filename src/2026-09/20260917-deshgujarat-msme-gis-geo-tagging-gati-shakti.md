# Gujarat to Geo-Tag and Map Manufacturing MSMEs on PM Gati Shakti Portal

**Source**: https://deshgujarat.com/2026/09/17/gujarat-to-geo-tag-map-manufacturing-msmes-on-pm-gati-shakti-portal/
**Date**: 2026-09-17
**Author**: DeshGujarat
**Keywords**: Gujarat, MSME, GIS, geo-tagging, PM Gati Shakti, RAMP scheme, Udyog Portal, industrial mapping, infrastructure planning

## Elevator pitch
The Gujarat government is undertaking a statewide GIS-based geo-tagging and mapping exercise of manufacturing MSMEs, integrating the data with the PM Gati Shakti national infrastructure portal to enable data-driven planning and infrastructure development.

## Takeaways
- The Gujarat MSME Commissionerate is leading the GIS-based geo-tagging exercise under the central government's RAMP (Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance) scheme
- Gujarat Info Petro Limited (GIPL) has been entrusted with carrying out the geo-tagging and mapping of manufacturing MSMEs registered on the Udyog Portal
- GIPL representatives will physically visit MSME units across the state to geo-tag locations and collect/verify information required for mapping
- The database will be integrated with the PM Gati Shakti Portal, India's national infrastructure planning platform
- The GIS-based mapping aims to create an integrated, structured database supporting infrastructure planning, policy formulation, and government scheme implementation
- Industry associations have been asked to facilitate the exercise among their members

## Synthesis
The Gujarat government has launched a comprehensive GIS-based geo-tagging and mapping exercise for manufacturing Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) across the state. The initiative, implemented by the MSME Commissionerate under the central government's RAMP scheme, represents a significant application of geospatial technology to industrial policy and infrastructure planning.

Gujarat Info Petro Limited (GIPL) has been tasked with the field execution. Authorized GIPL representatives will visit MSME units registered on the Udyog Portal to geo-tag their physical locations and collect verification information. This ground-truth approach ensures that the database reflects actual locations rather than registered addresses, which may differ.

The resulting GIS database will be integrated with the PM Gati Shakti Portal — India's national infrastructure planning platform that coordinates cross-sectoral infrastructure projects. This integration will provide policymakers with a geographical view of Gujarat's manufacturing base, helping identify infrastructure requirements, connectivity gaps, and sector-specific needs.

Gujarat's approved RAMP plan specifically includes mapping MSME clusters with PM Gati Shakti to align industrial clusters with infrastructure requirements. The MSME Commissionerate stated that the project would contribute to more effective, data-driven planning and improve the delivery of need-based support and amenities to businesses across the state.

The exercise illustrates how India's PM Gati Shakti initiative is driving the adoption of GIS-based mapping at the state level, creating spatial databases that link industrial activity to infrastructure planning in ways that were previously impossible with traditional tabular data.
