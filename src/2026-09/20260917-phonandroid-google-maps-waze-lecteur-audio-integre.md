# Google Maps et Waze intègrent un lecteur audio pour les conducteurs sans Android Auto

**Source**: https://www.phonandroid.com/pas-dandroid-auto-ne-jonglez-plus-entre-votre-musique-et-votre-gps-grace-a-cette-astuce-gratuite-sur-google-maps.html
**Date**: 2026-09-17
**Author**: Céline Demoineret / PhonAndroid
**Keywords**: Google Maps, Waze, audio player, streaming, navigation, Android Auto, CarPlay, Spotify, driving

## Elevator pitch
PhonAndroid reveals a little-known native feature in Google Maps and Waze that lets drivers control music streaming apps directly from the navigation interface — no Android Auto or CarPlay required.

## Takeaways
- Google Maps includes a hidden "audio playback controls" feature accessible via Settings > Navigation on both iOS and Android
- The feature supports popular streaming apps like Spotify, allowing play/pause, skip, and playlist access without leaving the navigation screen
- Waze similarly integrates an audio player accessible via a music icon in the top right corner, with support for multiple streaming services
- Users can also connect Google Maps or Waze to Spotify directly from the Spotify app via Settings > Apps and devices
- The feature works while walking as well as driving, and is particularly useful for older vehicles without infotainment systems
- Both apps require a one-time authorization to connect the streaming service to the navigation app

## Synthesis
For drivers without modern infotainment systems, the constant switching between a GPS navigation app and a music streaming app on a smartphone is both frustrating and dangerous. PhonAndroid's Céline Demoineret details a solution that has been hiding in plain sight: both Google Maps and Waze offer native audio playback controls that eliminate the need to toggle between apps.

On Google Maps, the feature is buried in Settings > Navigation > Audio playback controls on iOS, or Settings > Navigation > Show playback controls on Android. Once enabled and connected to a streaming service like Spotify, users get transport controls (play, pause, skip, previous) and playlist access directly overlaid on the navigation interface.

Waze offers a similar feature through a music icon in the top right corner of the screen. After selecting a streaming app and granting authorization, the icon transforms into the streaming app's logo, providing one-tap access to audio controls during navigation.

The article also notes that Spotify itself can initiate the connection from its side, via Settings > Preferences and privacy > Apps and devices, allowing users to associate either or both navigation apps with their Spotify account in a single workflow.

While these features are not new, their relative obscurity — the author herself discovered them while researching an unrelated topic — highlights how navigation apps are quietly becoming multi-function driving companions, reducing the need for dedicated infotainment hardware.
