# I tested 4 Android Auto navigation apps; one underrated option handles heavy traffic better than Google Maps

**Source**: https://www.androidpolice.com/tested-android-auto-navigation-apps-one-underrated-option-handles-heavy-traffic-better-than-google-maps/
**Date**: 2026-09-18
**Author: Rahul Naskar / Android Police
**Keywords**: Android Auto, Google Maps, navigation apps, TomTom GO, HERE WeGo, OsmAnd, traffic, alternatives

## Elevator pitch
An Android Police writer tested four navigation apps on Android Auto and found TomTom GO delivers more accurate real-time traffic data and better rerouting than Google Maps in heavy congestion, while Google Maps struggles with wrong-direction routing and closed-road suggestions.

## Takeaways
- Google Maps frequently routes users miles in the wrong direction before circling back, especially in small towns
- Google Maps suggested routes through roads closed for multi-day construction work, despite its real-time data claims
- TomTom GO provided more accurate real-time traffic data and better alternate routing than Google Maps, HERE WeGo, and OsmAnd in the author's testing
- HERE WeGo and OsmAnd had their own issues during testing, making TomTom GO the standout for heavy traffic scenarios
- The author advocates limiting Android Auto apps to essential ones for safety, and using a dedicated traffic-focused app rather than relying solely on Google Maps
- The article notes Google's focus on AI features in Maps while basic navigation reliability appears to be declining

## Synthesis
This hands-on comparison highlights Google Maps' declining reliability in heavy traffic scenarios, with TomTom GO emerging as the underrated winner for real-time congestion handling. The article reflects a growing sentiment that Google's investment in AI-powered Maps features may be coming at the expense of core navigation quality, pushing users toward alternatives like TomTom GO for specific use cases.
