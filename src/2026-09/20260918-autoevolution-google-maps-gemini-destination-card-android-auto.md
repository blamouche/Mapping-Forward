# New Google Maps Feature on Android Auto: Gemini-Powered Destination Information

**Source**: https://www.autoevolution.com/news/new-google-maps-feature-on-android-auto-gemini-powered-destination-information-275763.html
**Date**: 2026-09-18
**Author**: Autoevolution
**Keywords**: Google Maps, Android Auto, Gemini, AI, destination card, navigation, photos, AI summaries, Android Auto 17.8

## Elevator pitch
An APK teardown of Android Auto 17.8 reveals a hidden Gemini-powered destination card in Google Maps that provides AI summaries, photos from reviews, and practical details (parking, accessibility) about destinations — all without touching your phone.

## Takeaways
- The new destination card includes a Gemini-powered AI summary based on Google reviews, similar to smartphone summaries
- The card shows address, phone number, and "Good to know" info including parking and accessibility options
- Photos pulled from Google reviews help users explore the destination before driving
- The image gallery UI doesn't appear fully optimized for Android Auto yet, suggesting it's still a work in progress
- Feature was discovered hidden in Android Auto 17.8 and may not reach production builds
- Google's broader Gemini integration on Android Auto has been criticized for instability, inability to make calls, excessive chattiness, and causing app crashes
- Google Assistant is being phased out this month, making Gemini's role in Android Auto increasingly central regardless of current user complaints

## Synthesis
Google continues to embed Gemini deeper into the Android Auto experience, with the latest finding being a destination information card that brings AI summaries and review photos to the car screen. While the feature addresses a genuine need — getting destination context without picking up your phone — it arrives amid widespread user frustration with Gemini's reliability on Android Auto. The feature remains experimental and could be dropped before public release.
