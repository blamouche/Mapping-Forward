# Quand Google Maps répond en phrases : les cinq champs de votre fiche que l'IA lit en premier

**Source**: https://www.info-lux.com/ask-maps-google-maps-repond-fiche-entreprise/intelligence-artificielle/
**Date**: 2026-09-18
**Author**: Info-lux.com
**Keywords**: Google Maps, Ask Maps, Gemini, AI, conversational search, Google Business Profile, local SEO, business listing

## Elevator pitch
Ask Maps, the Gemini-powered conversational feature in Google Maps, now covers 150+ countries since August 2026. It reads five specific fields on a business listing — category/services, hours, reviews/replies, photos, and Q&A — to compose natural-language answers, making listing completeness critical for visibility.

## Takeaways
- Ask Maps was announced March 12, 2026 in the US and India, extended to 150+ countries on August 7, 2026; European availability (Belgium, France, Luxembourg) is not yet confirmed as of September 2026
- Ask Maps draws from 300+ million places, 500 million contributors' reviews, Street View, and photos to answer complex natural-language queries
- Only two or three businesses are returned per query — an incomplete listing isn't "ranked lower," it's simply not proposed at all
- The five fields Ask Maps reads: (1) category and services/attributes, (2) real hours including exceptions, (3) reviews and owner replies, (4) recent captioned photos, (5) Q&A section
- Owner replies to reviews count as readable text; review content like "pleasant terrace" can substitute for a service attribute
- Gemini also complements listing data with external web content (site, articles, directories), making a detailed web presence beyond the listing itself important

## Synthesis
Ask Maps represents Google's shift from keyword-based listing search to conversational AI queries in Maps. A business that doesn't mention "terrace" or "groups" in its attributes won't appear when a user asks for "a restaurant with a covered terrace open Monday that accepts groups." The article provides a practical five-step guide for Belgian and Luxembourg businesses to prepare their Google Business Profile before Ask Maps arrives in Europe, emphasizing that completeness — not ranking optimization — is the new visibility criterion.
