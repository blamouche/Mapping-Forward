# 68% de los mexicanos busca esquemas laborales híbridos para reducir el tiempo de traslado

**Source**: https://www.siempre.mx/2026/09/68-de-los-mexicanos-busca-esquemas-laborales-hibridos-para-reducir-el-tiempo-de-traslado/
**Date**: 2026-09-18
**Author**: Siempre!
**Keywords**: TomTom Traffic Index, Mexico, hybrid work, mobility, commute, World Car-Free Day, traffic congestion

## Elevator pitch
A study citing the TomTom Traffic Index reveals that 68% of Mexicans seek hybrid work arrangements to reduce commute times, with 67.7% citing reduced travel time as their primary motivator, ahead of World Car-Free Day on September 22.

## Takeaways
- 56.93% of Mexican professionals prefer a hybrid work model, compared to 33% currently working under one (WeWork/Michael Page study)
- 67.7% of those preferring hybrid work cite reducing commute times as their primary motivator
- The article references the TomTom Traffic Index 2025 as a key data source on urban mobility and congestion
- Published in the context of World Car-Free Day (September 22), linking traffic reduction to alternative mobility policies
- Hybrid work is framed as a practical solution to recover time lost to daily commutes in congested Mexican cities

## Synthesis
This article connects TomTom Traffic Index data to the growing demand for hybrid work in Mexico, showing that commute reduction is a stronger driver than workplace flexibility itself. The piece ties into the broader mobility conversation around World Car-Free Day, positioning traffic data as evidence for policy changes in urban work arrangements.
