# New Google Maps Speedometer Headache on Android Auto, Users Calling for Another Fix

**Author**: Bogdan Popa (autoevolution)

## Elevator pitch
Google Maps' speedometer on Android Auto is randomly switching between kilometers per hour and miles per hour mid-drive, ignoring user settings and requiring app restarts to fix, with no official response from Google yet.

- The Google Maps speedometer on Android Auto randomly switches between kph and mph during drives, despite being configured for one unit
- The issue appears to mostly affect users who configured kph rather than mph
- The settings toggle still shows the correct unit after the switch, meaning the app ignores its own configuration
- The only workaround is restarting Google Maps, which is inconvenient on Android Auto as it requires force-closing the app on the phone

- Separately, Google continues rolling out the speedometer without a configured destination, though availability is not yet universal

## Synthesis
A new glitch in the Google Maps speedometer on Android Auto is causing frustration among users. The speedometer randomly switches between kilometers per hour (kph) and miles per hour (mph) during active navigation, despite being configured to use only one unit. The issue appears to predominantly affect users who have configured kph as their preferred setting.

The problem is particularly confusing because the settings toggle continues to show the correct unit (kph) even after the speedometer switches to mph, meaning the application is ignoring its own configuration. The only workaround is to restart Google Maps, which is a significant inconvenience on Android Auto as it typically requires picking up the phone and force-closing the app.

The number of affected users doesn't appear to be very large, and the glitch happens sporadically. In some cases, the speedometer switches to mph but returns to kph almost instantly. Google has not yet responded to reports of this new error.

Separately, Google continues the gradual rollout of the speedometer feature without a configured destination. The speedometer appeared in Google Maps on Android Auto earlier in 2026 after being available on mobile devices for years. Some users discovered the speedometer disappeared after an early September update, which Google later explained was part of the phased rollout rather than a bug. Full availability is expected by end of 2026.
