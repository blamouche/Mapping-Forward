## Elevator pitch

## Synthesis
The 2025 TomTom Traffic Index has ranked the Philippines as the most congested country in Asia, ahead of India and Singapore, putting quantitative weight behind the daily experience of Metro Manila commuters. The TomTom Traffic Index measures urban congestion by comparing travel times during peak hours with free-flow conditions, providing city-level and country-level congestion data across the globe.

The ranking highlights the severity of Metro Manila's traffic crisis, which has been exacerbated by rapid urbanization, inadequate public transport infrastructure, and a heavy reliance on private vehicles. While the Philippine government has invested in road infrastructure and rail transit expansion, the pace of development has not kept up with population growth and vehicle ownership trends.

The TomTom data adds to growing calls from urban planners and transport advocates for a shift from road-widening projects toward integrated public transport systems, including bus rapid transit, rail network expansion, and active transport infrastructure. The 2025 ranking also positions the Philippines alongside other highly congested nations globally, raising questions about the economic cost of lost productivity due to traffic delays.

The Inquirer opinion piece frames the crisis as requiring both immediate congestion management and long-term structural investment in sustainable urban mobility.
