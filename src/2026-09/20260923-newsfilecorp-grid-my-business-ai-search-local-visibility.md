# Grid My Business Launches AI Search to Map Local Visibility Across AI Platforms

**Source**: https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/315664/Grid-My-Business-Launches-AI-Search-to-Map-Local-Visibility-Across-AI-Platforms
**Date**: 2026-09-23
**Author**: Plentisoft (press release)
**Keywords**: AI search, local SEO, Google Maps, ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode, AI Overviews, local visibility, business mapping, Grid My Business

## Elevator pitch
Grid My Business has launched AI Search, a feature that measures local business visibility across four AI platforms — ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Google AI Mode, and Google AI Overviews — by scanning locations, mapping mentions, and computing an AI visibility score.

## Takeaways
- AI Search scans ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Google AI Mode, and Google AI Overviews to measure where a business is mentioned across its service area
- Users enter a customer question, select AI platforms, and define a geographic area; the tool maps where each platform mentions the business, names a competitor, or returns no mention
- An AI Share of Voice metric shows the percentage of answered locations where the business was mentioned
- The feature tracks cited sources used by AI platforms, competitor comparisons, and actual AI answer history per scanned location
- Recurring monitoring allows rerunning questions daily, weekly, or monthly to track changes over time
- An AI visibility score is computed based on mention frequency, answer position, platform coverage, owned-site citations, and sentiment

## Synthesis
Grid My Business, an all-in-one local SEO platform, has launched AI Search, a feature that measures local visibility across four AI platforms: ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Google AI Mode, and Google AI Overviews. The tool addresses a growing gap as consumers increasingly ask AI assistants for local business recommendations rather than consulting Google Maps or traditional search alone.

Users select a business, enter a question a customer might ask, choose the AI platforms to check, and define a geographic area. AI Search then scans the selected locations and maps where each platform mentions the business, names a competitor, or returns no business mention. Key features include location-based results showing how AI answers vary across different parts of a service area, an AI Share of Voice metric, competitor comparisons across questions and platforms, and cited source tracking showing which websites AI platforms used as references.

The tool calculates an AI visibility score based on mention frequency, answer position, platform coverage, owned-site citations, and sentiment. Users can review results by question or AI platform, export maps and reports for client reporting, and rerun questions on a recurring schedule to track changes over time. Mark Gan, Community Manager at Grid My Business, noted that "local discovery is no longer happening only on Google Maps or traditional search" and that businesses need to know whether they are showing up in AI answers.

The feature is available to every Grid My Business account with an active plan.
