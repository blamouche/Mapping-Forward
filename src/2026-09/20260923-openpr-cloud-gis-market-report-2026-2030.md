## Synthesis
The cloud geographic information systems (GIS) market is experiencing rapid growth, according to a new market report from The Business Research Company. The market size is projected to increase from $1.97 billion in 2025 to $2.36 billion in 2026, representing a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 19.5%. Looking ahead, the market is expected to accelerate further, reaching $4.76 billion by 2030 at a CAGR of 19.2%.

Historical growth has been propelled by urban planning projects, early adoption of GIS software, expanded availability of geospatial data, cost-efficient mapping solutions, and government infrastructure initiatives. Future expansion will be fueled by smart city projects, AI-powered geospatial analytics, growing cloud infrastructure, climate monitoring programs, and the increasing use of location-based services.

Prominent companies in the cloud GIS market include Google LLC, Microsoft Corporation, Amazon Web Services Inc., Hexagon AB, Autodesk Inc., Trimble Inc., Maxar Technologies Inc., Bentley Systems Incorporated, Here Technologies, Mapbox Inc., CartoDB Inc., and others. Notable acquisitions include NV5 Global's acquisition of GIS Solutions Inc. in April 2024 to strengthen its geospatial and cloud GIS expertise.

The market is segmented by component (hardware, software, services), function (mapping, surveying, location-based services, telematics and navigation), deployment mode (public cloud, private cloud, hybrid cloud), and applications including watershed management, risk management, urban planning, defense management, and building information modeling. End-user industries span agriculture, real estate, oil and gas, construction, mining, transportation, government, utilities, and retail.

Emerging trends shaping the market include cloud-based spatial data management, real-time geospatial analytics, location intelligence platforms, integration with IoT and smart city technologies, and scalable GIS data visualization tools.
