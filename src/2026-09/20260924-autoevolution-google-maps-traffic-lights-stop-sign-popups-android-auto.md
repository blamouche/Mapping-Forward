# New Google Maps Navigation Update: Traffic Lights and Stop Sign Pop-Ups on Android Auto

**Source**: https://www.autoevolution.com/news/new-google-maps-navigation-update-traffic-lights-and-stop-sign-pop-ups-on-android-auto-276038.html
**Date**: 2026-09-24
**Author**: Bogdan Popa (autoevolution)
**Keywords**: Google Maps, Android Auto, traffic lights, stop signs, navigation update, pop-ups, intersection safety, driving assistance

## Elevator pitch
Google Maps on Android Auto has started showing pop-up indicators for traffic lights and stop signs at the next turn location during navigation, providing drivers with a clearer way to anticipate intersections without distracting alerts.

## Takeaways
- Google Maps on Android Auto now shows pop-ups at the next turn location with traffic light or stop sign indicators
- Unlike traditional alerts (e.g., speed traps), these pop-ups integrate into the navigation interface rather than sending notifications
- Google Maps has shown traffic lights on the map for years, but only as static icons without navigation-time alerts
- The new approach avoids the annoyance of frequent intersection alerts on city roads while still improving awareness
- The rollout appears to be gradual, with the feature being tested before wider deployment

## Synthesis
Google has started rolling out a new way to display traffic light and stop sign information during navigation on Android Auto. While Google Maps has been showing traffic lights on the map for quite some time, these icons only appeared on the screen as static elements with no visual indicator or alert during active navigation. Showing an alert every time a driver approaches an intersection would become distracting and annoying, especially on city roads where intersections are frequent.

Google's solution is a pop-up that appears on the map at the location of the next turn, accompanied by a traffic light or stop sign indicator. This new pop-up doesn't work like a traditional traffic alert—such as when Google Maps warns about a speed trap—but instead integrates the information directly into the navigation interface. This approach provides drivers with intersection awareness without the cognitive load of repeated notifications.

The rollout appears to be in its early stages, with the feature being observed on Android Auto devices. This represents a continuation of Google's efforts to improve intersection safety through its mapping platform, complementing existing features like speed camera alerts and lane guidance.
