# Toyoda, TomTom founders inducted into 2026 Automotive Hall of Fame

**Source**: https://www.autonews.com/executives/an-automotive-hall-of-fame-induction-photos-0924/
**Date**: 2026-09-24
**Author**: Automotive News
**Keywords**: TomTom, Automotive Hall of Fame, Harold Goddijn, Corinne Vigreux, navigation, GPS, automotive industry, Akio Toyoda, Dallara

## Elevator pitch
TomTom founders Harold Goddijn and Corinne Vigreux have been inducted into the 2026 Automotive Hall of Fame alongside Akio Toyoda and Giampaolo Dallara, recognizing TomTom's pioneering role in bringing GPS navigation to the automotive industry.

## Takeaways
- TomTom founders Harold Goddijn and Corinne Vigreux inducted into the 2026 Automotive Hall of Fame
- Other inductees include Toyota Motor Corp. Chairman Akio Toyoda and Dallara Motorsports founder Giampaolo Dallara
- The recognition highlights TomTom's foundational role in consumer and automotive GPS navigation
- TomTom has evolved from consumer navigation devices to providing maps and traffic data for automotive and enterprise applications
- The Automotive Hall of Fame honors individuals who have made significant contributions to the automotive industry

## Synthesis
The 2026 Automotive Hall of Fame has inducted TomTom founders Harold Goddijn and Corinne Vigreux, alongside other automotive industry luminaries including Toyota Motor Corp. Chairman Akio Toyoda and Dallara Motorsports founder Giampaolo Dallara. The induction recognizes TomTom's pioneering contribution to making GPS navigation accessible to consumers and integrating navigation technology into the automotive experience.

TomTom, founded in 1991, revolutionized personal navigation with its portable GPS devices before transitioning to become a leading provider of digital maps, traffic data, and navigation technology for automotive manufacturers, enterprises, and developers. The company's mapping and traffic intelligence now powers navigation systems for major automakers and serves as critical infrastructure for autonomous driving development.

The Automotive Hall of Fame honors individuals who have made significant and lasting contributions to the automotive industry. The induction of TomTom's founders highlights the increasingly central role that mapping, navigation, and location intelligence play in the automotive sector, from connected vehicles to autonomous driving systems.
