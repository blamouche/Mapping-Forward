# Apple Maps just added two new features in iOS 27

**Source**: https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/24/heres-everything-new-for-apple-maps-in-ios-27/
**Date**: 2026-09-25
**Author**: Ryan Christoffel (9to5Mac)
**Keywords**: Apple Maps, iOS 27, Flyover, Local Lists, navigation, AI, offline maps

## Elevator pitch
iOS 27 brings two flagship Apple Maps features — an AI-enhanced Flyover experience and privacy-first Local Lists — alongside incremental improvements to suggested places, natural language routing, and offline maps.

## Takeaways
- Apple Maps' Flyover feature gets a major upgrade combining aerial imagery with AI for stunning, lifelike city views in select destinations worldwide
- New Local Lists feature (US-only) surfaces trending restaurants, kid-friendly spots, and locally relevant place collections — all derived from aggregate insights with individual privacy protection
- Suggested Places now supports horizontal swiping through many more recommendations, expanding on the feature introduced in iOS 26.5
- Natural language search extends to routing, letting users specify routing preferences conversationally via AI
- A new Maps Parked Car widget in the Smart Stack helps locate your vehicle via Apple Watch, and offline maps receive unspecified improvements

## Synthesis
Apple's iOS 27 update for Apple Maps is notable not for a dramatic visual overhaul but for a series of targeted improvements that deepen the app's intelligence and local relevance. The two tentpole features — enhanced Flyover and Local Lists — reflect Apple's dual strategy of leveraging AI for immersive experiences while maintaining its privacy-first positioning.

The upgraded Flyover experience combines aerial imagery with AI to produce sharper, more lifelike city views. Apple positions this as useful for both pre-trip scouting and casual exploration, placing it in competition with Google Maps' Immersive View. The technology signals Apple's investment in AI-powered visual experiences as a differentiator in the mapping space.

Local Lists, the second flagship feature, is currently US-only and surfaces locally relevant place collections — trending restaurants, spots for kids, meeting-friendly venues — derived from aggregate, anonymised insights. This is Apple's answer to Google Maps' community-driven recommendations, but with a distinctly Apple approach: all insights are derived with privacy in mind and never tied to individual users. The feature taps into the growing trend of curated local discovery, where algorithmic recommendations replace manual search.

Beyond the headliners, iOS 27 adds a Trending Restaurants section to the search screen, extends natural language AI search to routing specifics, introduces a Parked Car widget in Smart Stack for Apple Watch users, and improves offline maps. Visited Places and Guides expand to more territories, and the Maps icon gets a refreshed design. These incremental changes collectively push Apple Maps closer to feature parity with Google Maps while preserving Apple's privacy-centric identity.
