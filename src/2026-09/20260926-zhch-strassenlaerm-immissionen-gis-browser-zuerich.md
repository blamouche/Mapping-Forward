# Road Noise Immissions Now Available in the GIS Browser of the Canton of Zurich

**Source**: https://www.zh.ch/de/news-uebersicht/mitteilungen/2026/planen-bauen/geoinformation/strassenlaerm-immissionen-neu-im-gis-browser.html
**Date**: 2026-09-26
**Author**: Amt für Raumentwicklung, Kanton Zürich (BD ARE GEO GIS)
**Keywords**: GIS, noise mapping, road noise, immissions, Canton Zurich, Switzerland, GIS-Browser, Lärmschutzverordnung, LSV, urban planning, environmental data, transparent geodata

## Elevator pitch
The Canton of Zurich has published two new road noise immission maps in its GIS-Browser, showing per-building noise exposure data for both the current state and planning horizon, providing transparent access to calculated road noise levels for property owners, planning offices, and authorities.

## Takeaways
- Two new maps published in the Canton of Zurich's GIS-Browser cover road noise immissions across the entire canton (excluding Zurich city)
- Maps show the current state (Istzustand) and planning horizon (Planungshorizont) noise exposure per building
- Each building displays the maximum calculated assessment level (Beurteilungspegel)
- Data includes immissions per measurement location at each building, information on relevant land use, and classification per LSV threshold system
- Information covers planning value (Planungswert), immission limit value (Immissionsgrenzwert), and alarm value (Alarmwert)
- Most existing noise barriers in the canton are also documented in the maps
- The data serves as a basis for planning remediation measures by road owners and responsible authorities

## Synthesis
The GIS-Browser of the Canton of Zurich now features two new maps covering road noise immissions across the entire cantonal territory (excluding the city of Zurich). Published on September 25, 2026, by the Amt für Raumentwicklung's geoinformation division, the maps provide detailed information on road noise exposure calculated for every building.

The two maps show the current state (Istzustand) and the planning horizon (Planungshorizont) respectively. For each building, the maximum calculated assessment level (Beurteilungspegel) is displayed. Users can also access immission data per measurement location at each building, information on the relevant land use category, and the classification according to the threshold system of the Noise Abatement Ordinance (Lärmschutzverordnung, LSV) — specifically the planning value (Planungswert), immission limit value (Immissionsgrenzwert), and alarm value (Alarmwert). Information about most existing noise barriers in the canton is also included.

The new maps provide property owners, planning offices, and other interested parties with transparent access to calculated road noise exposure at their properties at any time. The data also serves as a foundation for planning remediation measures by road owners and the authorities responsible for noise abatement. The interactive maps are accessible through the Canton of Zurich's geoportal at geo.zh.ch/maps.
