# Google Maps' Ask Maps feature completely changed how I plan my road trips

**Source**: https://www.androidpolice.com/goolge-ask-maps-road-trip/
**Date**: 2026-09-27
**Author**: Akshay Bhalla (Android Police)
**Keywords**: Google Maps, Ask Maps, Gemini, AI, road trip planning, navigation, conversational AI, Google Workspace

## Elevator pitch
Android Police's Akshay Bhalla reports that Google Maps' Gemini-powered Ask Maps feature transforms road trip planning from a multi-app, spreadsheet-heavy weekend task into a conversational experience that generates custom itineraries with scenic routes, food stops, and rest breaks based on personal preferences.

## Takeaways
- Ask Maps is a Gemini-powered conversational feature in Google Maps accessible below the search bar, using live data from 300+ million places, reviews, and photos
- The feature leverages users' saved places, food preferences, and past searches to generate personalized road trip itineraries
- Ask Maps can pull from Google Calendar and Gmail when Gemini is connected to Google Workspace via Personal Intelligence settings
- Responses include visual customized maps with photos, summaries of places with reviews, hours, and other details
- The chat can be shared with travel companions for real-time trip tracking, and adapts on the fly to weather changes or route preferences
- Conversation memory carries over like Gemini, so users don't need to restart chats to modify itineraries

## Synthesis
Google Maps' Ask Maps feature represents a significant shift in how users can plan road trips, moving from manual pin-dropping and multi-app coordination to a conversational AI-driven experience. The feature, powered by Gemini and accessible below the Google Maps search bar, draws on the platform's live data from over 300 million places, reviews, and photos to generate personalized recommendations.

What sets Ask Maps apart is its ability to incorporate personal context: saved places, food preferences, and past search history all inform its suggestions. A recent update also enables integration with Google Calendar and Gmail when users connect Gemini with Google Workspace through the Personal Intelligence settings, allowing the tool to factor in scheduled meetings or events when planning trips.

The author tested the feature on a tropical vacation, asking Ask Maps to plan a scenic drive with good food, coffee stops, clean restrooms, and no tourist traps. The tool returned a route with quaint coffee shops, sprawling vistas, and restaurants matching the user's known pizza preferences. Throughout the trip, the shared chat allowed companions to track the itinerary, and the conversational memory meant route changes for weather could be requested without starting a new session.
