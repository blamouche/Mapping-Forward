## Synthesis
The International Telecommunication Union (UIT), in collaboration with Côte d'Ivoire's telecommunications regulator ARTCI, organized a sub-regional training in Geographic Information Systems (GIS/SIG) and data management from September 21 to 25, 2026, at the Capitol Hôtel in Abidjan. The training is part of the Africa-BB-Maps initiative, co-piloted by UIT and funded by the European Union through its "Global Gateway" strategy.

This intermediate-level session followed a foundational training held in Cotonou, Benin, in June 2026. Participants included experts and delegates from Côte d'Ivoire, Benin, and Burundi, along with representatives from ARTAO. The training focused on advanced technical tools including QGIS, PostgreSQL-PostGIS database management systems, and the GeoServer platform — a stack commonly used for publishing and managing geospatial data.

According to Dana Jon Kamason, the Africa-BB-Maps project manager at UIT, the session aimed to strengthen operational skills in geospatial data processing, quality control, and digital data publication. The ultimate goal is to enable participants to precisely map existing internet infrastructure locations in the field and identify areas where broadband network improvements are needed, informing strategic investment decisions for high-performance broadband deployment.
