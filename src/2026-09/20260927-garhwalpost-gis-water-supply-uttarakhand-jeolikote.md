# Uttarakhand CS Orders GIS Geo-Referencing of Water Supply Schemes After Jeolikote Contamination

**Source**: https://garhwalpost.in/cs-reviews-jeolikote-contaminated-water-supply-case-orders-system-overhaul/
**Date**: 2026-09-27
**Author**: Garhwal Post Bureau
**Keywords**: GIS, geo-referencing, water supply, Uttarakhand, Jeolikote, mapping, Nainital, drinking water, infrastructure mapping

## Elevator pitch
Following a contaminated drinking water incident in Jeolikote, Nainital, Uttarakhand's Chief Secretary ordered GIS geo-referencing and geo mapping of all major water supply schemes across the state, including sources, storage, pipelines, sewer lines, and crossing points.

## Synthesis
Uttarakhand Chief Secretary Anand Bardhan has ordered a comprehensive GIS geo-referencing and geo mapping exercise for all major water supply schemes across the state, following a contaminated drinking water incident in Jeolikote, Nainital. The directive, issued during a review meeting at the Secretariat, requires mapping the entire water supply network from source to consumer, including storage facilities, pipelines, sewer lines, and crossing points.

The CS expressed strong displeasure that despite the state's extensive field network — including teachers, patwaris, gram panchayat officials, and tubewell operators — information about the Jeolikote contamination did not reach the state level in time. He directed that an effective information and feedback mechanism be developed using this field network to ensure immediate reporting of drinking water quality issues.

Physical verification of all major water supply schemes must be completed within 15 days, with checks for possible leakages at sources, storage, pipelines, sewer lines, crossing points, and consumer ends. The CS also ordered identification of crossing points between drinking water and sewerage lines, with remedial measures including pipe replacement where needed. The Pollution Control Board must ensure regular quality testing of drinking water, STP effluent, and surface water in major cities, with surprise inspections of all sewage treatment plants.
