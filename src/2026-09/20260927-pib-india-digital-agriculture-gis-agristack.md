# Outcome of Digital Agriculture Initiatives: India Leverages GIS and AgriStack

**Source**: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2225978
**Date**: 2026-09-27
**Author**: Press Information Bureau, Government of India
**Keywords**: AgriStack, GIS, digital agriculture, Farmer ID, Digital Crop Survey, Krishi Decision Support System, geo-referenced village maps, India, drone, geospatial

## Elevator pitch
India's Ministry of Agriculture reports that its AgriStack digital public infrastructure — combining geo-referenced village maps, farmer registries, and crop sown registries — has generated over 8.48 crore Farmer IDs and surveyed 28.5 crore plots, while the Krishi Decision Support System uses GIS to integrate satellite imagery, weather, soil, and crop data for agricultural planning.

## Takeaways
- AgriStack consists of three foundational registries: Farmers Registry, Geo-Referenced Village Maps, and Crop Sown Registry, maintained by state governments
- Over 8.48 crore (84.8 million) Farmer IDs generated as of February 2026, enabling Direct Benefit Transfer integration
- Digital Crop Survey conducted in 604 districts covering 28.5 crore plots during Kharif 2025
- Krishi Decision Support System is a geo-spatial platform using GIS to integrate satellite imagery, weather, soil, and crop data
- Maharashtra leveraged AgriStack to transfer over ₹14,000 crore to 89 lakh farmers for Kharif 2025 crop losses
- Chhattisgarh institutionalized Farmer ID and Digital Crop Survey for MSP-based paddy procurement covering 32 lakh farmers
- Namo Drone Didi scheme distributed 500 drones to women's self-help groups, with study showing diversified agricultural practices

## Synthesis
India's digital agriculture initiatives, evaluated by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare in 2025, demonstrate how GIS and geospatial data are transforming agricultural planning at national scale. The AgriStack digital public infrastructure integrates three foundational registries — the Farmers Registry, Geo-Referenced Village Maps, and Crop Sown Registry — creating a single source of truth for farmer identity, land, and crops.

The Krishi Decision Support System serves as a geo-spatial analytical platform that integrates satellite imagery, weather, soil, and crop data using Geographic Information System (GIS) to support agricultural planning and decision-making. It enables targeted advisories on crop, weather, and soil conditions. The Digital Crop Survey has achieved plot-level visibility of crops across 604 districts, covering over 28.5 crore plots during the Kharif 2025 season alone.

State-level implementations show tangible results: Maharashtra transferred over ₹14,000 crore to 89 lakh farmers for crop losses using AgriStack, while Chhattisgarh covered 32 lakh farmers for MSP-based paddy procurement. The Namo Drone Didi scheme has distributed 500 drones to women's self-help groups, with studies indicating improved agricultural practices and increased income opportunities in rural communities.
