## Elevator pitch
Birmingham Live reports that drivers surveyed by London Northwestern Railway repeatedly named Hagley Road as Birmingham's least favourite road, while the TomTom Traffic Index 2025 recorded an average evening rush-hour speed of just 13mph across the city, illustrating the scale of Birmingham's congestion problem.

## Synthesis
Birmingham Live reported on driver frustration with Birmingham's road network, with Hagley Road repeatedly identified as the city's least favourite road in a survey conducted by London Northwestern Railway. The article contextualizes this qualitative driver feedback with quantitative data from the TomTom Traffic Index 2025, which recorded an average evening rush-hour speed of just 13mph across Birmingham — though the data reflects the citywide average rather than Hagley Road specifically.

The combination of survey results and TomTom's traffic data paints a picture of significant urban congestion challenges in Birmingham. Hagley Road West in Quinton is identified as a particular source of driver frustration. The article illustrates how traffic index data from providers like TomTom can be paired with driver surveys to document and communicate urban mobility problems.
