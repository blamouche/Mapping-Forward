# Hyundai launches Pleos Connect in the US: HERE Maps meets Google Places

## Elevator pitch
Hyundai unveiled the US-spec Pleos Connect infotainment platform for the 2027 Tucson, combining HERE Maps navigation with Google Places search, alongside a Gleo AI voice assistant, QHD displays, and Wi-Fi 6.

## Takeaways
- Hyundai's Pleos Connect platform debuts in the US market with the 2027 Tucson, featuring 12.9-inch and 17-inch QHD (2560x1440) displays plus a 9.9-inch Slim Information Display
- American navigation combines HERE Maps with Google Places for point-of-interest search, merging automotive-grade map data with Google's business listing ecosystem
- The platform runs on Android Automotive OS with a dedicated Pleos App Market, supporting apps that work independently of smartphone mirroring, though wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto are retained
- Gleo AI, a large language model-based assistant, understands conversational context and multiple linked commands, activated by "Hey Gleo" or "Hey Hyundai"
- Connectivity includes Bluetooth 5.4 and Wi-Fi 6, with over-the-air software updates
- Hyundai retains physical buttons and dials for core functions rather than moving all controls to the touchscreen

## Synthesis
Hyundai revealed the US-specification version of its Pleos Connect infotainment platform on October 2, 2026, ahead of the 2027 Tucson launch. The system, previously unveiled in spring 2026, now has detailed regional specifications for the American market. The navigation stack is particularly notable: it pairs HERE Maps for turn-by-turn routing with Google Places for business and POI search, combining automotive-grade map data with Google's extensive business listing database.

Pleos Connect runs on Android Automotive OS but goes beyond standard Android Auto. Applications install directly through the Pleos App Market and function independently of a connected smartphone. The platform supports Gleo AI, a voice assistant built on a large language model that understands conversational context and chained commands. Connectivity includes Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.4, with OTA updates.

Hyundai's decision to combine HERE Maps with Google Places reflects a hybrid approach to in-car navigation, leveraging HERE's strength in automotive-grade map data and offline routing while tapping Google's superior POI database. The Tucson and Tucson Hybrid are scheduled to reach the US market in early 2027.
