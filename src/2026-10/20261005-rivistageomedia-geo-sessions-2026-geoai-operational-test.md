## Synthesis
Rivista Geomedia previews Geo Sessions 2026, an online event organized by NV5 Geospatial Software on October 14-15, 2026. The event explores how GeoAI, SAR, LiDAR and spectral technologies are moving from research to operational deployment. The focus is explicitly on the "operational test" — whether these technologies can deliver actionable intelligence from geospatial data in real-world scenarios.

The event covers a broad range of application domains including BIM, CAD, GIS, Earth Observation, Earth Sciences, and infrastructure monitoring. This reflects the increasingly interdisciplinary nature of geospatial technology, where AI-driven analysis bridges traditional boundaries between surveying, remote sensing, and infrastructure management.

Geo Sessions 2026 is part of a broader trend in the geospatial industry where the conversation is shifting from data collection — which has become increasingly automated and abundant — to data interpretation and action. The event examines the full pipeline from geospatial data acquisition to operational decision-making, with GeoAI as the connective tissue.
