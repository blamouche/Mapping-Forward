# Microsoft Signs Long-Term Xbox Deal and Expands AI Partner Ties with TomTom, ClickHouse, Atlan

**Source**: https://simplywall.st/stocks/us/software/nasdaq-msft/microsoft/news/microsoft-msft-signs-long-term-xbox-deal-and-expands-ai-part
**Date**: 2026-10-06
**Author**: Mitchell Lawler, Simply Wall St
**Keywords**: Microsoft, TomTom, ClickHouse, Atlan, DrugBank, Microsoft Fabric, Foundry, Copilot, AI partnerships, location intelligence

## Elevator pitch
Microsoft deepened enterprise and AI partnerships with TomTom, ClickHouse, Atlan, and DrugBank across Microsoft Fabric, Foundry, and Copilot, integrating location intelligence and specialized data into its AI infrastructure platform.

## Takeaways
- Microsoft signed a new long-term Xbox Publisher License Agreement with Take Two Interactive
- Enterprise AI partnerships expanded with TomTom, ClickHouse, Atlan, and DrugBank across Fabric, Foundry, and Copilot
- TomTom integration plugs location intelligence directly into Microsoft's data and AI platforms
- The partnerships support the narrative that AI workloads, subscriptions and usage-based services should run on shared Microsoft infrastructure
- Investors should watch for Fabric, Foundry, and Copilot usage metrics tied to these partners in upcoming quarterly updates

## Synthesis
Microsoft signed a new long-term Xbox Publisher License Agreement with Take Two Interactive covering future titles, consolidating prior Xbox deals into one framework with unified terms. More significantly for the geospatial industry, Microsoft deepened enterprise and AI partnerships with TomTom, ClickHouse, Atlan, and DrugBank across Microsoft Fabric, Foundry, and Copilot.

The TomTom partnership in particular plugs location intelligence directly into Microsoft's data and AI platforms. This aligns with the narrative that AI workloads, subscriptions and usage-based services should increasingly run on a shared Microsoft infrastructure base. These deals help support the case that higher AI usage intensity can sustain the heavy data center buildout and hybrid pricing model, even as margin and CapEx risks remain.

Investors should watch whether Microsoft starts breaking out or commenting on Fabric, Foundry and Copilot usage tied to these partners in upcoming quarterly updates, particularly metrics such as AI agent counts, OneLake data volumes, or revenue contributions from Marketplace-sourced solutions.
