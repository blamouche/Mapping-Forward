# DJI's New GEO Training Programme Reaches the Benelux with First Certified Training Dealer

**Source**: https://www.suasnews.com/2026/10/djis-new-geo-training-programme-reaches-the-benelux-first-certified-training-dealer/
**Date**: 2026-10-06
**Author**: Press release, sUAS News
**Keywords**: DJI, GEO training, drone mapping, photogrammetry, LiDAR, Matrice 4E, Zenmuse L3, Benelux, Drone Parts Center, GIS, RTK

## Elevator pitch
DJI Enterprise launched an official GEO training programme for its mapping drones and sensors, with Drone Parts Center in Nivelles, Belgium becoming the first certified training dealer in the Benelux, offering Matrice 4E and Zenmuse L3 courses for professional mapping and GIS analysis.

## Takeaways
- DJI Enterprise launched an official training programme for mapping drones and sensors, available in Europe only
- Drone Parts Center in Nivelles, Belgium is the first certified GEO training dealer in the Benelux
- Two courses available: Matrice 4E (RTK photogrammetry, centimetre-level accuracy, up to 2 km² per flight) and Zenmuse L3 (long-range LiDAR, up to 10 km² per flight)
- The Matrice 4E course covers advanced photogrammetry, Smart 3D Capture, D-RTK 3 base station deployment, modelling in DJI Terra, and GIS analysis
- The Zenmuse L3 course covers LiDAR principles, acquisition design, RTK/PPK operations, point cloud classification and reconstruction
- Training includes online self-study, 1-2 days of in-person training, and official DJI certification
- No prior surveying background required; course does not replace remote pilot licence
- A French version of the online portal is announced for January 2027

## Synthesis
DJI Enterprise has launched an official training programme for its mapping drones and sensors, available exclusively in Europe and delivered by dealers certified after a rigorous examination process. Drone Parts Center, based in Nivelles, Belgium, has become the first certified training dealer in the Benelux region. Its Operations Manager, Stéphane Tilmant, obtained his GEO Trainer Certification in August 2026.

The programme standardises training for professional mapping drone operators. Two courses are available: the Matrice 4E course covers RTK photogrammetry with centimetre-level accuracy, including advanced photogrammetry, Smart 3D Capture, corridor/area/oblique missions, D-RTK 3 base station deployment, modelling in DJI Terra, editing in DJI Modify, and GIS analysis. Typical applications include topographic surveys, construction progress monitoring, stockpile volumes, and accident scene reconstruction. The Zenmuse L3 course covers long-range LiDAR with up to two million pulses per second and vertical accuracy of about 3 cm at 120 m, including LiDAR principles, acquisition design, complex missions, RTK/PPK operations, and point cloud classification.

The training format combines online self-study on the DJI training portal with one to two days of intensive in-person training, field and office work, culminating in a practical and theoretical assessment and an official DJI certificate with five years of access to updated course content.

As Didier Van Nes, founder of Drone Parts Center, explains: "We sell increasingly capable drones and we see too many customers using only part of them because nobody taught them the method. With this certification we can finally hand over the manufacturer's know-how, not just the hardware."
