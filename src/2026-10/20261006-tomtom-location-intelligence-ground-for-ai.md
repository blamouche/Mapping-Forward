**Source**: https://www.tomtom.com/newsroom/explainers-and-insights/tomtom-location-intelligence-the-ground-for-ai-to-stand-on
**Date**: 2026-10-06

## Elevator pitch
TomTom positions itself not as a mapping company but as a location intelligence provider, offering a trusted data foundation with 30+ years of mapping expertise, 235+ countries covered, and billions of validated data points to ground AI systems in real-world context.

## Takeaways
- TomTom redefines its core mission as making the world understandable and data usable, with location intelligence as the newest expression
- The company provides "ground truth" data: 30+ years mapping, 235+ countries, billions of data points, 15+ years of proprietary on-road historic data
- Live traffic information is updated every 30 seconds
- Orbis Maps and Overture partnership ensure open, interoperable industry standards for the data foundation
- 77+ car brands use TomTom tech; global partners include Cariad, Microsoft, Meta, AWS
- TomTom is embedded in ecosystems across Microsoft, ESRI and other big tech platforms
- The Agent Toolkit is now available through the TomTom Maps SDK for agentic workflows

## Synthesis
TomTom has never really been in the business of making maps, argues the company's newsroom. At its core, the company has always been about making the world understandable and data usable — giving structure to unstructured data. Maps are one expression of that. Location intelligence is the newest.

The article outlines four pillars of TomTom Location Intelligence. First, ground truth: a trusted data foundation built on 30+ years of mapping the world, covering 235+ countries and territories, with billions of data points ingested, validated and continuously updated. The company holds 15+ years of on-road proprietary historic data, with live traffic information updated every 30 seconds.

Second, the company leverages Orbis Maps and the Overture Maps partnership to ensure open, interoperable industry standards. Third, industry partnerships embed TomTom across ecosystems: 77+ car brands use TomTom tech, with global partners including Cariad, Microsoft, Meta and AWS. Fourth, the TomTom Agent Toolkit — available through the Maps SDK — enables agentic workflows where AI can reason about location.

As organizations increasingly rely on location intelligence, Orbis is putting TomTom at the center of building the future of location-based tech, used by both machines and humans.
