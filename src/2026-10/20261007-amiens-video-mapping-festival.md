# Video Mapping Festival Returns to Amiens, Illuminating Seven Iconic Landmarks

**Source**: https://www.amiens.fr/Actualites/Eclairs-de-genie
**Date**: 2026-10-07
**Author**: Simon Playoult, Amiens Métropole
**Keywords**: video mapping, Amiens, festival, projection mapping, cultural heritage, Hauts-de-France, audiovisual

## Elevator pitch
The Video Mapping Festival returns to Amiens on October 16-17, 2026, illuminating seven landmarks including the cathedral, Tour Perret, and Hôtel de Berny, with the city recognized as a French pioneer in architectural projection mapping since its cathedral colorization in 1999.

## Takeaways
- Seven locations mapped across a ~2 km parcours in city center and Saint-Leu quarter
- Amiens pioneered architectural mapping in France with cathedral colorization starting in 1999
- First-time mappings: L'Homme sur sa bouée, Sport nautique façade, and Sacré-Cœur chapel
- Waide Somme 3D animation students will present workshop work on the chapel façade
- The festival closes its 9th edition after illuminating ~15 cities across Hauts-de-France in 2026
- Free access, October 16-17 from 19:30 to 23:30

## Synthesis
Amiens Métropole announces the return of the Video Mapping Festival on October 16-17, 2026, when seven iconic locations will be transformed through projection mapping. The approximately 2-kilometer parcours through the city center and Saint-Leu quarter includes the Tour Perret, Hôtel de Berny, the cathedral's Golden Virgin portal, the medieval garden, L'Homme sur sa bouée statue, the Sport nautique façade, and the Sacré-Cœur chapel.

Amiens holds pioneer status in France for architectural projection mapping, having introduced cathedral colorization in 1999. Three locations will be mapped for the first time, with the Sacré-Cœur chapel serving as an exceptional architectural canvas for students from Waide Somme, the local 3D animation school. The festival, organized by Rencontres audiovisuelles, concludes its 9th edition after illuminating approximately 15 cities across the Hauts-de-France region throughout 2026.
