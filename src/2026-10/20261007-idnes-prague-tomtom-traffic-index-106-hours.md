## Synthesis
An iDNES.cz commentary uses TomTom Traffic Index data to highlight Prague's persistent traffic problem: residents spend 106 hours per year stuck in congestion. The article argues that eight years of traffic difficulties have created what the author calls "traffic terror," noting that when the city's roads finally ease in the evening, they paradoxically become speeding zones rather than returning to normal flow. The piece connects the TomTom data to broader political critique of urban transport governance in Prague.
