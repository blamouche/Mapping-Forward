# Major Disruptions on A50 in Marseille After Two-Wheeler Accident, TomTom Reports 45+ Minute Delays

**Source**: https://maritima.fr/actualites/info-route/marseille/15959/de-grosses-perturbations-sur-la50-a-marseille-apres-un-accident-impliquant-un-deux-roues
**Date**: 2026-10-07
**Author**: Jean-Michel Darras, Maritima
**Keywords**: TomTom, A50, Marseille, traffic, accident, real-time traffic data, navigation

## Elevator pitch
An accident between a car and a two-wheeler on the A50 near Marseille caused severe traffic disruptions, with TomTom data showing over 45 minutes needed to clear the jam, illustrating how real-time traffic platforms track and communicate incident-driven congestion.

## Takeaways
- Accident between a car and a two-wheeler on A50 in the Toulon-to-Marseille direction around 8 AM
- The 50-year-old motorcyclist suffered shoulder injuries
- A second accident between two cars occurred near La Pomme in Marseille
- TomTom data indicated 45+ minutes needed to pass the bottleneck
- Disruptions extended back to Aubagne
- Heavy rainfall in the department compounded driving hazards

## Synthesis
Maritima reported significant traffic disruptions on the A50 highway near Marseille following an accident involving a car and a two-wheeler. The incident, which occurred around 8 AM in the Toulon-to-Marseille direction between La Penne-sur-Huveaune and La Valentine, left the 50-year-old motorcyclist with shoulder injuries. A subsequent accident between two cars near La Pomme further compounded delays.

TomTom's real-time traffic data showed that commuters needed more than 45 minutes to clear the resulting bottleneck, with disruptions extending back to Aubagne. The situation was aggravated by heavy rainfall affecting the Bouches-du-Rhône department since the previous night, highlighting how TomTom's traffic monitoring provides critical, up-to-the-minute information for drivers navigating incident-driven congestion.
