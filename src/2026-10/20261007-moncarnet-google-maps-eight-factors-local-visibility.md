# Google Maps: Eight Factors That Influence Local Business Visibility

**Source**: https://moncarnet.com/2026/10/07/google-maps-huit-facteurs-qui-influencent-la-visibilite-des-entreprises-locales/
**Date**: 2026-10-07
**Author**: Bruno Guglielminetti, Mon Carnet
**Keywords**: Google Maps, local SEO, Google Business, visibility, Localo, reviews, business listings

## Elevator pitch
An analysis of over two million Google Business profiles by Localo identifies eight recurring factors — from description completeness to review volume and photo count — that distinguish businesses ranking in the top positions of Google Maps local search results.

## Takeaways
- 75% of profiles in positions 1-3 have complete descriptions, vs. 40% for positions 11-20
- Top-ranked businesses average 70-word descriptions vs. 60 for lower-ranked ones
- Over 85% of top-3 businesses have a website linked to their profile
- Top businesses have 200+ reviews on average, vs. 150 for positions 11-20
- The 10 most relevant reviews for top businesses total ~350 words (~35 words per review)
- Reply length to reviews also correlates with better positioning
- Google limits business descriptions to 750 characters

## Synthesis
A Localo study of more than two million Google Business profiles reveals eight factors that correlate with higher visibility in Google Maps local search. Description completeness stands out: approximately 75% of profiles appearing in positions 1-3 have complete descriptions, compared to roughly 40% for positions 11-20. Top-ranked businesses also write slightly longer descriptions averaging 70 words.

Website presence is strongly associated with ranking — over 85% of top-3 businesses link to a website. Reviews play a dual role: top businesses average 200+ reviews (vs. 150 for lower positions), and the content matters too, with the 10 most relevant reviews totaling about 350 words. The length of business responses to reviews also correlates with better positioning. Mon Carnet notes these are observed correlations rather than proven causal factors, but they offer a practical roadmap for local businesses looking to improve their Google Maps visibility.
