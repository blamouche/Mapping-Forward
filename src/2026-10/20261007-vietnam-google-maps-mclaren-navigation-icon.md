# Google Maps Lets Users Replace Navigation Arrow with a McLaren Race Car

**Source**: https://www.vietnam.vn/fr/bien-mui-ten-chi-duong-google-maps-thanh-xe-dua-mclaren
**Date**: 2026-10-07
**Author**: Vietnam.vn
**Keywords**: Google Maps, McLaren, navigation icon, customization, branding, motorsport partnership

## Elevator pitch
Google Maps now allows users to replace the standard navigation arrow with a McLaren race car icon, deepening the commercial partnership between Google and the motorsport brand while offering a fun personalization option for navigation.

## Takeaways
- Google Maps users can replace the default navigation arrow with a McLaren race car icon
- The feature reinforces the commercial partnership between Google and McLaren
- It offers motorsport fans a customization option for their daily navigation
- This follows a trend of Google Maps introducing themed navigation icons through brand partnerships

## Synthesis
Vietnam.vn reports that Google Maps has introduced a McLaren race car as an alternative navigation icon, allowing users to replace the standard blue arrow during turn-by-turn directions. The addition reinforces the commercial relationship between Google and McLaren, giving motorsport enthusiasts a way to personalize their navigation experience. This follows Google Maps' pattern of introducing themed navigation icons through brand partnerships, adding a layer of consumer personalization to the otherwise utilitarian mapping interface.
