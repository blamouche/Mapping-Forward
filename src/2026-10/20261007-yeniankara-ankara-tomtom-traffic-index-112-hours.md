# Ankara Drivers Lose 112 Hours Per Year in Traffic, TomTom Traffic Index Reveals

**Source**: https://www.yeniankara.com.tr/ankara/ankara-trafik-suresi-suruculer-yilda-kac-saat-trafikte-kaliyor-185439
**Date**: 2026-10-07
**Author**: Yeni Ankara
**Keywords**: TomTom, Traffic Index, Ankara, Turkey, congestion, urban mobility, traffic data

## Elevator pitch
TomTom's 2025 Traffic Index data reveals that Ankara drivers lose approximately 112 hours per year in traffic during peak hours, with congestion levels rising 1.6 percentage points compared to 2024, and evening rush hour speeds dropping to just 19.5 km/h.

## Takeaways
- Ankara drivers lose 112 hours/year in traffic (≈4 days 16 hours), up 6h23m from 2024
- Average congestion level: 55.8%, a 1.6-point increase from 2024
- A 10km journey takes 21 minutes 3 seconds on average, 17 seconds longer than the previous year
- Evening peak congestion reaches 118.3%, with average speeds of 19.5 km/h
- At 6 PM, drivers cover only 4 km in 15 minutes, with congestion hitting 163%
- The busiest day was November 10, 2025, with 88% daily average congestion
- TomTom's index is based on 3.65 trillion km of journey data worldwide

## Synthesis
TomTom's 2025 Traffic Index paints a worsening picture for Ankara's motorists. The Turkish capital's drivers lose 112 hours annually to peak-hour traffic — roughly 4 days and 16 hours — representing a 6-hour 23-minute increase from 2024. The city's average congestion level reached 55.8%, up 1.6 points year-over-year.

The data reveals stark differences between morning and evening commutes. While morning peak congestion sits at 90.3%, evening peaks surge to 118.3%, with speeds dropping to 19.5 km/h. The most critical moment occurs around 6 PM, when congestion hits 163% and drivers manage only 4 km in 15 minutes. TomTom's methodology draws on 3.65 trillion kilometers of anonymous driving data globally, measuring congestion, average speed, journey time, and peak-hour time loss.
